# Decisions log

Generated by `cargo run --release -p mtg-tools -- interpretations --write` from the interpretation register (`docs/interpretations/*.md`), the "Revisit later" list of `docs/agents/finish-line-plan.md` and `docs/RULING_DISCREPANCIES.md`; don't edit by hand (`cargo test` fails when this file is out of date). To change what it says, change those files and regenerate.

Every judgment call the engine makes about the rules (a reading of an ambiguous or silent rule, a knowing deviation from the Comprehensive Rules, or a policy for a choice the rules leave to players) is an entry of the register. This file sorts them by who made the call, so a reviewer can see who decided what. Who decided is read from the entry's own words, "Decided by the user, <date>", "Decided by the ruling (...)" and the like, in its Decision line (or else its Why line). Each decision is shown in brief: the opening sentences of the entry's lines, cut at a sentence's end (" …" marks a cut), and a link to the full entry (the rules quoted, the question, the alternatives and the reasoning).

Sections:

- [Decided by the user](#decided-by-the-user) (123): the user chose the reading, newest first, by date: the user's own words where the entry quotes them, the reading chosen, the alternatives not chosen, and marks of low confidence ("for now", "not sure", "tentatively", ...) or of being on the "Revisit later" list.
- [Decided by the sources](#decided-by-the-sources) (9): a Wizards ruling, a rule of the Comprehensive Rules or a dated change answers the question, and the entry says so; one line each, with that basis.
- [Decided by the coordinator or a worker](#decided-by-the-coordinator-or-a-worker) (71): everything else decided: calls the coordinator, a worker or a review made, most of them defaults for choices an agent leaves unanswered. Those whose entry names no decider are listed apart; one line each, with the entry's reasoning.
- [Provisional](#provisional) (3): calls a reasonable judge could make the other way, awaiting the user.
- [Revisit later](#revisit-later) (11): readings the user accepted for now but wants to look at again before the project is called done, copied from the finish-line plan.
- [Ruling discrepancies](#ruling-discrepancies) (80): Wizards rulings the engine doesn't follow in full, or tests on another card than the one the data attaches them to, by kind and review status; a human checks each and reports the erroneous ones to Wizards.
- [Policies the user set](#policies-the-user-set) (10): standing rules the user set for whole classes of cases (which source wins, who chooses), drawn from their register entries: the user-decided entries of kind policy whose Affects line names more than one case.
- [Needs a source line](#needs-a-source-line) (0): decided entries whose words about who decided this file can't read; they need a "Decided by ..." line in the usual form, and are never guessed.

## Revisit later

From [the finish-line plan](agents/finish-line-plan.md#revisit-later-user-nov-12), "Revisit later (user, Nov 12)". Readings the user accepted for now, but wants to look at again before calling the project done:

- **Buyback and alternative-cost reductions.** The user: "I agree with the recommendations about buyback for now, but could discover that I am wrong about this in the future, so let's make sure it is noted somehow."
  - Entry `keyword-cost-reduction-on-an-additional-cost-within-it`: Memory Crystal is bounded by the buyback cost.
  - Entry `alternative-keyword-cost-reduction-reduces-additional-costs`: Catalyst Stone's reduction also comes off a buyback paid with flashback.
- **The gaps in returned-card values.** The user: "I accept the recommendations and will try to resolve the gaps later." These are in entry `returned-card-values-by-wording`:
  - "its power/toughness" after a graveyard return;
  - a card that never arrives (Containment Priest with Reanimate);
  - Deicide's searched name for a copied God.
- **A phased-out source's characteristics.** Entry `phased-out-source-still-acts` (decided Nov 12) uses the source's characteristics while it is phased out, not its last known information. Example: Spikeshot Elder under Glorious Anthem deals 1, not 2.
- **Loops:** a robust system later (D4, Nov 10). Includes the limited range of influence case: CR 104.4f makes a mandatory loop a draw only for the players involved and those within their range, while `settle-loop-is-a-draw` (decided Nov 12) draws the whole game. Also Helm of Obedience against Wheel of Sun and Moon (entry `mill-until-stops-when-nothing-moves`, a known deviation registered Nov 20): every milled card goes to the bottom of the library instead of the graveyard, so the process repeats forever, which CR 104.4b makes a draw; the engine ends the process once only cards it milled are left, and the game goes on.
- **Counter modifiers on an entering permanent.** Entry `entering-counter-modifiers-after-enters-with` (a deviation, to be fixed later; the user, Nov 12): the effects that modify how many counters are put apply after all of a permanent's "enters with" effects, so an opponent's Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider can't be interleaved with Master Biomancer (Spike Feeder enters with 2 counters, not the 3 the controller could choose).
- **The combat requirement search budget.** Entry `requirement-search-budget` (a deviation, accepted Nov 18): on boards with more than 100,000 distinct declarations, the maximum number of requirements obeyed (508.1d, 509.1c) is searched for within 200,000 nodes from two greedy starts, so on a board of many different creatures with interacting requirements and restrictions that no greedy start satisfies, a declaration obeying fewer requirements than the true maximum could be accepted. Plan: an exact method (a matching formulation) if a real case misses the maximum.
- **"N or more" block triggers.** Entry `block-count-triggers-at-the-nth-block` (accepted tentatively, Nov 19; the user: "I think I agree with the engine recommendation here, but I'm really not sure"): "blocks N or more" and "becomes blocked by N or more" trigger when a block brings the number to N, again after the number fell below N and a later block brings it back (Sorrow's Path swapping Serra Inquisitors' black blocker for another triggers it twice), not at most once each combat.
- **A subtype a resolving effect adds to a noncreature, and its base power and toughness.** Entries `subtype-gained-only-if-it-corresponds-as-the-effect-begins` (decided Nov 19) and `base-pt-set-on-a-noncreature-applies-once-it-is-a-creature` (decided Nov 20). The user: "I agree with the recommendation, though we should note that the original decision here is one of the more low confidence ones I've made" (the original decision being the Nov 19 Shadow Puppeteers subtype reading). Shadow Puppeteers' trigger resolving for a creature that Swift Reconfiguration made a noncreature Vehicle never makes it a Dragon, even once it's crewed (CR 205.3d applied as the effect begins), while the base 4/4 the same effect sets applies once it's a creature (CR 208.3a).
- **Entering effects whose source is turned face down.** Entry `simultaneous-entries-ordered-by-controller` (decided Nov 20 with low confidence; the user: "I'm really not sure on this one, so I'll take the recommendation but note it as low confidence"): the replacement effects that would modify how a permanent enters are fixed as the event is proposed, so one whose source another entering permanent's effect turns face down still applies, as one whose source leaves does (Rhythm of the Wild's ruling, 2019-05-03). Example: Ixidron entering with Grizzly Bears turns Grumgully, the Generous face down, and the Bears still get Grumgully's +1/+1 counter. Each such effect still applies only to the permanent as it would then exist (CR 614.12, 616.1f: a Metallic Mimic that has named Human gets no counter from Grumgully's). No ruling covers a source that stays but loses its abilities.
- **The "for as long as" edge cases.** Entries `copied-ability-duration-starts-as-the-copy-is-created`, `phasing-out-ends-a-waiting-for-as-long-as-duration`, `duration-about-another-object-ends-while-the-ability-waits` and `duration-the-resolution-sets-up-is-checked-as-the-effect-begins` (decided Nov 20; the user: "25-28 Are the same. I'll take the recommendation, but not very sure."): a copy's duration starts as the copy is put on the stack; the source phasing out while its ability waits ends a "for as long as" duration about it; a condition about another object that fails while the ability waits has ended, one that didn't hold at first never started; a condition the resolution itself sets up (a counter, a tap) is checked as the effect begins.
- **A modal spell's X before its modes.** Entry `x-announced-before-modes` (a deviation, decided Nov 20; the user: "I agree on 37 as a documented deviation"): the engine asks the value of X with the costs and the modes afterwards with the targets, the reverse of CR 601.2b's order (Invoke the Firemind's ruling: "first you choose the mode (draw cards or deal damage), then you choose the value of X"); the legal combinations are the same either way, only the order of the questions differs. Plan: ask the modes first, bounding the X offered by the modes chosen, for spells and abilities (`casting.rs`, `stack.rs`, `default_x.rs`).
