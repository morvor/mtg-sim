Every judgment call the engine makes about the rules (a reading of an ambiguous or silent rule, a knowing deviation from the Comprehensive Rules, or a policy for a choice the rules leave to players) is an entry of the register. This file sorts them by who made the call, so a reviewer can see who decided what, and links each to its full entry (the rules quoted, the question, the alternatives and the reasoning). Who decided is read from the entry's own words, "Decided by the user, <date>", "Decided by the ruling (...)" and the like, in its Decision line (or else its Why line).

  - Decision: Decided by the user, Nov 19. The engine's view of the card after the ability has resolved stands for the player's memory of what they saw, not for another look: the ruling's "It doesn't let you look at it later" is about looking at the card again at the table. While the permanent remains the same object its card can't change (it may be turned face up and down again, still the card the player looked at), so the view tells the player nothing they didn't learn as the ability resolved.

## Revisit later

From [the finish-line plan](agents/finish-line-plan.md#revisit-later-user-nov-12), "Revisit later (user, Nov 12)". Readings the user accepted for now, but wants to look at again before calling the project done:

- **Buyback and alternative-cost reductions.** The user: "I agree with the recommendations about buyback for now, but could discover that I am wrong about this in the future, so let's make sure it is noted somehow."
  - Entry `keyword-cost-reduction-on-an-additional-cost-within-it`: Memory Crystal is bounded by the buyback cost.
  - Entry `alternative-keyword-cost-reduction-reduces-additional-costs`: Catalyst Stone's reduction also comes off a buyback paid with flashback.
- **The gaps in returned-card values.** The user: "I accept the recommendations and will try to resolve the gaps later." These are in entry `returned-card-values-by-wording`:
  - "its power/toughness" after a graveyard return;
  - a card that never arrives (Containment Priest with Reanimate);
  - Deicide's searched name for a copied God.
- **A phased-out source's characteristics.** Entry `phased-out-source-still-acts` (decided Nov 12) uses the source's characteristics while it is phased out, not its last known information. Example: Spikeshot Elder under Glorious Anthem deals 1, not 2.
- **Loops:** a robust system later (D4, Nov 10). Includes the limited range of influence case: CR 104.4f makes a mandatory loop a draw only for the players involved and those within their range, while `settle-loop-is-a-draw` (decided Nov 12) draws the whole game. Also Helm of Obedience against Wheel of Sun and Moon (entry `mill-until-stops-when-nothing-moves`, a known deviation registered Nov 20): every milled card goes to the bottom of the library instead of the graveyard, so the process repeats forever, which CR 104.4b makes a draw; the engine ends the process once only cards it milled are left, and the game goes on.
- **Counter modifiers on an entering permanent.** Entry `entering-counter-modifiers-after-enters-with` (a deviation, to be fixed later; the user, Nov 12): the effects that modify how many counters are put apply after all of a permanent's "enters with" effects, so an opponent's Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider can't be interleaved with Master Biomancer (Spike Feeder enters with 2 counters, not the 3 the controller could choose).
- **The combat requirement search budget.** Entry `requirement-search-budget` (a deviation, accepted Nov 18): on boards with more than 100,000 distinct declarations, the maximum number of requirements obeyed (508.1d, 509.1c) is searched for within 200,000 nodes from two greedy starts, so on a board of many different creatures with interacting requirements and restrictions that no greedy start satisfies, a declaration obeying fewer requirements than the true maximum could be accepted. Plan: an exact method (a matching formulation) if a real case misses the maximum.
- **"N or more" block triggers.** Entry `block-count-triggers-at-the-nth-block` (accepted tentatively, Nov 19; the user: "I think I agree with the engine recommendation here, but I'm really not sure"): "blocks N or more" and "becomes blocked by N or more" trigger when a block brings the number to N, again after the number fell below N and a later block brings it back (Sorrow's Path swapping Serra Inquisitors' black blocker for another triggers it twice), not at most once each combat.
- **A subtype a resolving effect adds to a noncreature, and its base power and toughness.** Entries `subtype-gained-only-if-it-corresponds-as-the-effect-begins` (decided Nov 19) and `base-pt-set-on-a-noncreature-applies-once-it-is-a-creature` (decided Nov 20). The user: "I agree with the recommendation, though we should note that the original decision here is one of the more low confidence ones I've made" (the original decision being the Nov 19 Shadow Puppeteers subtype reading). Shadow Puppeteers' trigger resolving for a creature that Swift Reconfiguration made a noncreature Vehicle never makes it a Dragon, even once it's crewed (CR 205.3d applied as the effect begins), while the base 4/4 the same effect sets applies once it's a creature (CR 208.3a).

## Policies the user set

The user-decided entries of kind policy whose Affects line names more than one case (a list or a class of cards, not a single card): standing rules for whole classes of cases, each also listed under its date above.

- **`controller-picks-the-choosing-opponent`** (Nov 19, [decisions-policies](interpretations/decisions-policies.md#controller-picks-the-choosing-opponent-when-an-opponent-makes-a-choice-the-controller-of-the-spell-or-ability-picks-which-opponent-by-default-the-next-in-turn-order)): When "an opponent" makes a choice, the controller of the spell or ability picks which opponent (by default the next in turn order)
  - The user: "when "an opponent chooses" the controller should always get to pick the opponent unless the text, a ruling, or the CR specifically says otherwise."
