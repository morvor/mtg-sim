# Replacement and prevention

Judgment calls about replacement and prevention effects (CR 614–616) and the damage and
draw events they modify. Format and kinds: `docs/agents/items/interpretation-register.md`;
index: `docs/INTERPRETATIONS.md`.

## shared-color-source-locked: "A source of your choice that shares a color with X" compares with X's colors as the effect is created
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 609.7a "... The source is chosen when the effect is created. ..." / 609.7b "Some effects from resolved spells and abilities prevent or replace damage from sources with certain properties, such as a creature or a source of a particular color. When the source would deal damage, the “shield” rechecks the source’s properties. If the properties no longer match, the damage isn’t prevented or replaced. ..."
- Question: Protective Sphere ("a source of your choice that shares a color with the mana spent on this activation cost") and Mourner's Shield ("... that shares a color with the exiled card") name a property relative to something else. When the shield rechecks the source's properties (609.7b), which colors does it compare with: those of the mana or exiled card as the effect was created, or as they are then?
- Decision: The colors as the effect is created. The source must have one of them both when it's chosen and when it would deal damage; the exiled card leaving exile, or the activation's mana being long gone, doesn't change them.
- Alternatives: Recheck against the exiled card as it is when the damage would be dealt (impossible for the mana spent, which no longer exists).
- Why: The mana spent on the activation exists only while the ability resolves, so the effect has to remember its colors; treating the exiled card the same way keeps the two wordings consistent, and the source's own colors are still rechecked as 609.7b requires.
- Affects: Protective Sphere, Mourner's Shield.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/prevention.rs` (`lock_filter`: `SharesColor`, `SHARES_COLOR_WITH_MANA_SPENT`).
- Tests: `tests/cards/c4_mana_provenance.rs` (`protective_sphere_chooses_a_source_sharing_a_color_with_the_mana_spent`).

## per-source-prevention-division: A shared "prevent X of that damage" is divided by its controller only when one source's damage exceeds X
- Kind: policy
- Status: decided
- Rules: 615.7 "... If damage would be dealt to the shielded permanent or player by two or more applicable sources at the same time, the player or the controller of the permanent chooses which damage the shield prevents. ..." / 616.1 "If two or more replacement and/or prevention effects are attempting to modify the way an event affects an object or player, the affected object's controller (or its owner if it has no controller) or the affected player chooses one to apply ..."
- Question: Cover of Winter prevents X of the combat damage each creature would deal to you and/or creatures you control, shared among those recipients; its ruling says "you decide how to divide the prevention effect" when a creature's damage is assigned to several of them (trample). When is the division asked, and what if the answer is invalid?
- Decision: Before the simultaneous damage is replaced, for each creature whose damage to two or more of your recipients totals more than X, Cover of Winter's controller divides X among them (a `Divide` decision, at most each recipient's damage). When the total is at most X there's nothing to choose (all of it is prevented). An invalid answer or a default prevents X in the order the engine replaces the damage (APNAP by recipient, then `prevention::order_for_shields`).
- Alternatives: Always prevent in the engine's order without asking (the player loses a real choice); ask only for an order (can't express splitting X across two partially prevented recipients).
- Why: Follows the ruling; the default only fills in for a missing or invalid answer.
- Affects: Cover of Winter.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/replacement.rs` (`divide_per_source_prevention`, `PreventPerSource`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/actions.rs` (`deal_damage_batch`).
- Tests: `tests/cards/c4_replacements.rs` (`cover_of_winter_prevents_x_of_each_creatures_combat_damage`, `cover_of_winter_controller_divides_x_among_one_creatures_recipients`).

## words-target-on-activation: A next-draw replacement's "any target" is chosen as the ability is activated; if it's gone, the draw is still replaced and nothing is dealt damage
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 115.1c "An activated ability is targeted if it identifies something it will affect by using the phrase “target [something],” ... The target(s) are chosen as the ability is activated; see rule 602.2b." / 608.2b "If the spell or ability specifies targets, it checks whether the targets are still legal. ... If all its targets ... are now illegal, the spell or ability doesn't resolve." / 614.11 "Some effects replace card draws." / Mirrorwood Treefolk ruling: "If the target creature is not on the battlefield (or is not a creature) at the time the damage would be redirected, then the damage goes on this card." / Words of War ruling: "If multiple Words have been used prior to drawing a card, then you can choose which one to apply (and use up) each time you draw a card."
- Question: Words of War ("{1}: The next time you would draw a card this turn, this enchantment deals 2 damage to any target instead.") names a target inside a replacement effect that applies later. No rule or ruling says whether the target is chosen as the ability is activated or as the draw is replaced, nor what happens when the target is no longer there when the draw is replaced.
- Decision: Decided by the user, Nov 4. The target is the activated ability's (115.1c): chosen as it's activated, checked as it resolves (608.2b: if it's illegal then, the ability doesn't resolve and no shield is created), and locked into the shield. When the draw is replaced, the shield applies and is used up whatever became of the target; if the target object has left its zone (a new object, 400.7) or the player has left the game, the 2 damage is dealt to nothing. Its legality isn't checked again (the replacement effect isn't a spell or ability that targets), so hexproof or protection gained afterwards doesn't stop the damage (damage prevention by protection still applies, 702.16e).
- Alternatives: (a) The target is chosen as the draw is replaced (the replacement effect would choose targets like a triggered ability; nothing in 614 or 616 provides for that). (b) The replacement doesn't apply when its target is gone, and the draw happens (the shield would wait for a later draw or expire); this would make the shield's applicability depend on something 614.1a's "instead" doesn't mention.
- Why: 115.1c is explicit that "target" in an activated ability is chosen on activation, and the same wording on Glarecaster, General's Regalia and Mirrorwood Treefolk (targeted next-time redirections) works that way, with Mirrorwood's ruling: the effect still applies and the missing target simply gets nothing. The draw's replacement is the event the shield watches for; the damage is only what happens instead.
- Affects: Words of War; the other Words (Words of Wind, Waste, Wilding, Worship) have no target.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/oracle/patterns/replacements_next_draw.rs` (`next_draw_instead`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/prevention.rs` (`lock_def`: `ReplacementAction::Instead` → `lock_targets`).
- Tests: `tests/cards/c5_mana_replacements.rs` (`words_of_war_replaces_the_next_draw_with_damage_to_the_target_chosen_on_activation`, `words_of_war_target_gone_still_replaces_the_draw_without_damage`).

## runesword-damage-this-turn: Runesword's effects cover every creature its target has dealt damage to this turn, from the moment the damage is dealt
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 701.19c "... Effects that say that a permanent can't be regenerated don't preclude such abilities from being activated or such spells from being cast; rather, they cause regeneration shields to not be applied." / 704.5g "If a creature has toughness greater than 0, it has damage marked on it, and the total damage marked on it is greater than or equal to its toughness, that creature has been dealt lethal damage and is destroyed. ..." / 614.1a "Effects that use the word “instead” are replacement effects. ..."
- Question: Runesword's ability says "If the creature deals damage to a creature this turn, the creature dealt damage can't be regenerated this turn. If a creature dealt damage by the targeted creature would die this turn, exile that creature instead." Is the first sentence a trigger (which would resolve only after state-based actions destroyed, and regenerated, the creature dealt lethal damage), and does damage the target dealt earlier in the turn, before the ability resolved, count?
- Decision: Decided by the user, Nov 4. Both sentences are continuous effects created as the ability resolves, lasting until end of turn, about the creatures the targeted creature has dealt damage to this turn (the turn's damage history, as Torch the Tower's "a permanent dealt damage by ~"). A creature it deals damage to can't be regenerated from that moment, so a regeneration shield doesn't save it from lethal damage, and if it would die this turn it's exiled instead. Damage it dealt earlier in the turn counts too. The targeted creature is locked in as the effect begins; damage dealt by other creatures doesn't count.
- Alternatives: (a) Read the first sentence as "Whenever that creature deals damage to a creature this turn, that creature can't be regenerated this turn" (a delayed trigger): the ban would come too late for lethal damage, and the sentence would do nothing in Runesword's usual case. (b) Count only damage dealt after the ability resolves: "this turn" and "dealt damage by" don't say so; with an attacking target, damage before the ability resolves is rare (first strike).
- Why: The Oracle wording is a conditional statement, not a "whenever" trigger, and only a continuous effect gives the sentence its evident purpose. "Dealt damage by the targeted creature ... this turn" reads as the turn's damage history, like every other "dealt damage by ~ this turn" in the engine.
- Affects: Runesword.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/oracle/patterns/prevention_6.rs`; `crates/mtg-engine/src/prevention.rs` (`lock_damage_history`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/kw/damage_history_by.rs`.
- Tests: `tests/cards/c5_mana_replacements.rs` (`runesword_creatures_dealt_damage_by_the_target_cant_regenerate_and_are_exiled`, `runesword_doesnt_affect_creatures_damaged_by_other_creatures`).

## enter-sacrifice-instead-partial: "Sacrifice two untapped lands instead. If you do, ..." with fewer than two sacrifices those it can, and ~ doesn't enter
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 609.3 "If an effect attempts to do something impossible, it does only as much as possible." / 614.12 "Some replacement effects modify how a permanent enters the battlefield. ..."
- Question: Lotus Vale and Scorched Ruins say "If ~ would enter, sacrifice two untapped lands instead. If you do, put ~ onto the battlefield. If you don't, put it into its owner's graveyard." The sacrifice is an instruction, not an offer (Mox Diamond's "you may discard a land card instead" is one), so with two untapped lands they're sacrificed. With only one untapped land, is it sacrificed, and does ~ enter?
- Decision: Decided by the user, Nov 4. The one land is sacrificed (as much as possible, 609.3), and "if you do" is true only if two were sacrificed, so ~ goes to its owner's graveyard. With no untapped land nothing is sacrificed.
- Alternatives: Treat the instruction like a cost that can't be paid in part (nothing sacrificed with one land); treat it as optional like Mox Diamond (the previous reading, which let a player keep both lands and put ~ into the graveyard).
- Why: The card's text gives no choice and no cost; 609.3 is the general rule for an instruction that can't be done in full, and Lake of the Dead's and Heart of Yavimaya's rulings ("You have to sacrifice a swamp before this card is put onto the battlefield") settle that the sacrifice isn't optional; Lotus Vale's and Scorched Ruins' rulings ("If you don't sacrifice the lands, Lotus Vale never enters") say only that it doesn't enter, not what happens to the one land.
- Affects: Lotus Vale, Scorched Ruins (the one-land versions, Lake of the Dead and the others, have no partial case).
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/oracle/patterns/replacement_bodies.rs` (`enter_only_if_sacrificed`).
- Tests: `tests/cards/roundtrip_pairs_4.rs` (`lotus_vale_with_one_untapped_land_sacrifices_it_and_never_enters`, `lake_of_the_dead_sacrifices_a_swamp_without_asking`); `tests/cards/replacement_bodies_misc.rs` (`lotus_vale_enters_only_if_two_untapped_lands_are_sacrificed`).

## next-time-damage-batch: A one-use "the next time ... would deal damage / be dealt damage" effect applies to all the damage dealt at the same time
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 615.8 "Some prevention effects generated by the resolution of a spell or ability refer to the next time a specific source would deal damage. These effects prevent the next instance of damage from that source, regardless of how much damage that is. Once an instance of damage from that source has been prevented, any subsequent instances of damage that would be dealt by that source are dealt normally." / 615.12 "... Existing damage prevention shields won’t be reduced by damage that can’t be prevented." / 510.2 "... all combat damage that's dealt during the combat damage step is dealt simultaneously." / Glarecaster ruling: "If both you and this card would be dealt damage at the same time, or if either you or this card would be dealt damage from multiple sources at the same time, the redirection will apply to both chunks of damage."
- Question: Damage dealt at the same time is several damage events to the engine (one per source and recipient). A limited-use replacement or prevention effect ("the next time damage would be dealt to this creature and/or you this turn", "the next time target creature would deal damage this turn") is used up by the first of them it applies to. Does it then apply to the others dealt at the same time?
- Decision: Decided by the user, Nov 5. Yes. While damage dealt at the same time is being replaced, a limited-use effect used up by one of its events still applies to the others (as one entering-the-battlefield batch already did for "the next time one or more creatures enter"). So Glarecaster redirects both chunks, and Awe Strike prevents both the damage a trampler deals to its blocker and the damage it deals to the player. Effects that prevent a number of damage ("the next 3 damage") are unaffected: they're shields with an amount, not one-use effects. Damage of the batch that can't be prevented doesn't use up a prevention effect (615.12), so when part of the batch can't be prevented, the effect prevents the rest and is used up by it.
- Alternatives: Use the effect up on the first event only, so the others (in APNAP and shield order) are dealt normally; this contradicts the Glarecaster ruling and 615.8's "instance of damage ... regardless of how much damage that is".
- Why: The Glarecaster ruling settles redirection to two recipients from two sources; 615.8 treats one "instance" of a source's damage as a whole, however it's split among recipients. No ruling found treats simultaneous chunks separately. It was provisional because the ruling covers redirection, and the extension to every one-use damage effect is ours.
- Affects: Glarecaster, Awe Strike, and every one-use damage replacement or prevention effect (`AddReplacement { uses: Some(1) }` on a damage event).
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/actions.rs` (`deal_damage_batch`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/replacement.rs` (`used_by_this_entry`, `apply_replacement`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/zones.rs` (`damage_batch_uses`).
- Tests: `tests/rulings/r_p002_replacement.rs` (`glarecaster_redirects_every_chunk_dealt_at_the_same_time`, `a_next_time_prevention_covers_all_of_a_source_s_simultaneous_damage`, `a_next_time_prevention_is_used_up_by_a_batch_part_of_which_can_t_be_prevented`).

## shield-divided-among-recipients: A damage shield applying to simultaneous damage to several of its recipients is divided by its controller
- Kind: policy
- Status: decided
- Rules: 615.7 "... These work like shields. Each 1 damage that would be dealt to the “shielded” permanent or player is prevented. Preventing 1 damage reduces the remaining shield by 1. If damage would be dealt to the shielded permanent or player by two or more applicable sources at the same time, the player or the controller of the permanent chooses which damage the shield prevents. ..." / 614.9 (redirection effects) / Harm's Way ruling: "You choose which 2 damage is redirected. If you like, you can choose to redirect 1 damage that would be dealt by the chosen source to each of two different recipients." / Refraction Trap ruling: "you choose which of that damage to prevent. ... You don’t decide until the point at which the source would deal its damage."
- Question: 615.7 says who chooses when several sources damage one shielded recipient. A shield can also cover several recipients at once ("the next 2 damage that a source of your choice would deal to you and/or permanents you control"). When damage would be dealt to two or more of them at the same time, more than is left of the shield, who chooses which of it the shield applies to, and how?
- Decision: As the damage would be dealt, the shield's controller divides what's left of it among those recipients (a `Divide` decision over the distinct recipients, each at most the damage it would be dealt). Damage the shield can't apply to (it can't be prevented, or can't be redirected) takes no part, and when fewer than two recipients or no more damage than the shield is left, nothing is asked. A recipient dealt damage by several sources gets its part applied in the order its controller put that damage in (`order_for_shields`, CR 615.7). An invalid answer or a default applies the shield in the order the engine replaces the damage (APNAP by recipient, then `order_for_shields`).
- Alternatives: Ask each recipient's controller in turn (the rulings say "you" choose); never ask, applying the shield in the engine's order (loses the choice the rulings describe).
- Why: Follows the rulings. The cards with such shields cover only their controller and that player's permanents, so the shield's controller is also every recipient's controller.
- Affects: Harm's Way, Refraction Trap, Divine Deflection, Shining Shoal, and every shield with an amount (`ReplacementInstance::remaining`) on damage to several recipients.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/prevention.rs` (`divide_shields`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/replacement.rs` (`apply_replacement`: `PreventAmount`/`PreventAndThen`/`RedirectNext` with `per_source_division`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/actions.rs` (`deal_damage_batch`).
- Tests: `tests/rulings/r_p043_shields.rs` (`harms_way_redirects_just_2_of_simultaneous_damage_as_you_choose`, `refraction_trap_you_choose_which_simultaneous_damage_it_prevents_as_it_is_dealt`, `a_divided_shield_is_divided_among_recipients_then_ordered_by_source`).

## entering-counters-one-event: The counters of one kind a permanent enters with are put on it as one event, however many "enters with" effects contributed
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 122.6 "Some spells and abilities refer to counters being put on an object. This refers to putting counters on that object while it’s on the battlefield and also to an object that’s given counters as it enters the battlefield." / 614.1c "Effects that read “[This permanent] enters with . . . ,” “As [this permanent] enters . . . ,” or “[This permanent] enters as . . . “ are replacement effects." / 614.5 "A replacement effect doesn’t invoke itself repeatedly; it gets only one opportunity to affect an event or any modified events that may replace that event." / Hardened Scales ruling (2023-09-01): "If a creature you control would enter the battlefield with a number of +1/+1 counters on it, it enters with that many plus one instead." / Spark Double ruling (2019-05-03): "If it copies a creature, Spark Double enters with a +1/+1 counter on it, plus any counters that will be put on it from abilities it copied and other abilities of other objects." / Winding Constrictor ruling (2017-02-09): "If you would get counters of multiple kinds at the same time, Winding Constrictor increases the number of each of those kinds of counters by one. The same is true if counters of multiple kinds would be placed on an artifact or creature you control."
- Question: A permanent can be given counters by several "enters with" effects at once: Spike Feeder's own two +1/+1 counters and Master Biomancer's additional ones, or a Spark Double copying Spike Feeder (the copied ability's two, the copy exception's one). Is that one event of putting counters on it, which "if one or more +1/+1 counters would be put on a creature you control, that many plus one" (Hardened Scales) modifies once, or one event per "enters with" effect, each getting one more?
- Decision: One event for each kind of counter (and each player putting them, CR 122.6a): the permanent enters once and is given all its counters of that kind as it does. Hardened Scales makes Spark Double copying Spike Feeder enter with 4 counters (not 5), and Spike Feeder under Master Biomancer (power 2) enter with 5 (not 6).
- Alternatives: One event per "enters with" effect, each modified separately (the engine's previous behavior): Hardened Scales would add one counter for each effect that contributed, which no ruling describes.
- Why: Settled by the rules: the "enters with" effects are replacement effects modifying the one event of the permanent entering (614.1c), its counters are put on it in that event (122.6), and Hardened Scales gets one opportunity to modify it (614.5): "it enters with that many plus one", as its ruling says, "that many" being the number the "enters with" effects gave it. Kinds are separate (Winding Constrictor's ruling). Decided in the review of engine-audit-1/replacement (it was provisional only because no ruling names two "enters with" effects and Hardened Scales together). How the effects that modify the number are ordered with the "enters with" effects is `entering-counter-modifiers-after-enters-with`.
- Affects: Hardened Scales, Winding Constrictor, Corpsejack Menace, Vorinclex, and every permanent given counters of one kind by two or more "enters with" effects (copied ones, copy exceptions, Master Biomancer, Bramblewood Paragon, riot, ...).
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/actions.rs` (`perform_move`: the counters it enters with, merged by kind and putter before replacement).
- Tests: `tests/cr/r614_audit_counters.rs` (`hardened_scales_adds_one_counter_to_spark_doubles_whole_entry`, `hardened_scales_adds_one_counter_to_a_feeder_entering_under_master_biomancer`).

## entering-counter-modifiers-after-enters-with: Effects that modify how many counters are put apply to a permanent's entry after all its "enters with" effects
- Kind: deviation
- Status: provisional
- Rules: 616.1e "Any of the applicable replacement and/or prevention effects may be chosen." / 616.1f "Once the chosen effect has been applied, this process is repeated (taking into account only replacement or prevention effects that would now be applicable) until there are no more left to apply." / 616.2 "A replacement or prevention effect can become applicable to an event as the result of another replacement or prevention effect that modifies the event." / Hardened Scales ruling (2023-09-01): "If two or more effects attempt to modify how many counters would be put on a creature you control, you choose the order to apply those effects, no matter who controls the sources of those effects."
- Question: Spike Feeder ("enters with two +1/+1 counters") enters under its controller's Master Biomancer ("enters with a number of additional +1/+1 counters ... equal to Master Biomancer's power") while an opponent controls Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider ("If an opponent would put one or more counters on a permanent or player, they put half that many ..."). Once Spike Feeder's effect has given it two counters, Vorinclex's effect applies too (616.2), and the controller may apply it before Master Biomancer's (616.1e): 2 halved is 1, plus 2 is 3, where applying Master Biomancer's first gives (2 + 2) / 2 = 2. Can the controller interleave them?
- Decision: (to be fixed later; the user, Nov 12) Not in the engine: the "enters with" effects are applied as the entry is replaced, and the effects that modify how many counters are put (Hardened Scales, Corpsejack Menace, Doubling Season, Vorinclex) to the counters of each kind it then enters with, in an order the controller chooses among themselves (`entering-counters-one-event`): Spike Feeder enters with 2 counters, not the 3 the controller could choose. Counters of one kind that different players put (tribute's opponent, CR 122.6a) are separate events, so Hardened Scales adds one to each.
- Alternatives: Offer the counter-modifying effects among the entry's replacement effects once it has counters (exact, but the counters an entry is given would be replaced in two places, before and after it enters, and every "enters with" counter path, loyalty and lore included, would need the same treatment).
- Why: The order matters only when an effect reduces the number (an opponent's Vorinclex) or a player wants fewer counters; with effects that add or double, applying every "enters with" effect first gives the most counters, as a player would choose. Recorded for the user to accept or ask for the exact behavior: the user accepted it for now, to be fixed later (the user, Nov 12).
- Affects: Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider (an opponent's) with Master Biomancer, Bramblewood Paragon-style "enters with an additional counter" effects, copy exceptions (Spark Double) and tribute.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/actions.rs` (`perform_move`: the counters it enters with, replaced after the move's replacement effects).
- Tests: `tests/cr/r614_audit_counters.rs` (`an_opponents_vorinclex_halves_all_the_counters_a_feeder_enters_with_under_master_biomancer`).

## entering-would-be-controller-chooses: For a permanent entering the battlefield, the player it would enter under the control of chooses among replacement effects
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 616.1 "If two or more replacement and/or prevention effects are attempting to modify the way an event affects an object or player, the affected object’s controller (or its owner if it has no controller) or the affected player chooses one to apply, following the steps listed below. ..." / 614.12 "... To determine which replacement effects apply and how they apply, check the characteristics of the permanent as it would exist on the battlefield, taking into account replacement effects that have already modified how it enters the battlefield (see rule 616.1), ..." / Gather Specimens ruling (2008-10-01): "If two or more players have each cast Gather Specimens during the same turn and a creature would enter, the creature's would-be controller (the controller of the creature spell, for example) chooses one of the applicable Gather Specimens to apply. Then the new would-be controller of the creature repeats this process among the remaining Gather Specimens, and so on, until there are no more possible Gather Specimens effects to apply." / Crafty Cutpurse ruling (2018-01-19): "If two or more players each have resolved Crafty Cutpurse’s triggered ability and a token would be created, the token’s would-be controller chooses one of the applicable Crafty Cutpurse effects to apply. Then the new would-be controller of the token repeats this process among the remaining Crafty Cutpurse effects, and so on, until there are no more possible such effects to apply. Each effect can be applied to the token only once this way." / Land Equilibrium ruling (2009-10-01): "If a land that would enter has its own "If [this land] would enter" replacement effect, the player who will control that land chooses which replacement effect to apply."
- Question: Who chooses among the replacement effects that would modify how a permanent enters: the controller of the spell (or the owner of the card, which has no controller in a graveyard, hand, library or exile), or the player it would enter under the control of?
- Decision: Decided by the user, Nov 12 (as recommended): "I agree with all recommendations". The player it would enter under the control of, as the effects applied so far have it (`Game::entering_controller`): a creature spell an opponent's Gather Specimens takes has the remaining choices (another Gather Specimens, that player's copy effects) made by the new would-be controller; a card put onto the battlefield "under your control" from an opponent's graveyard has them made by the player putting it there. Simultaneous entries are ordered for these choices in APNAP order by the same player.
- Alternatives: The spell's controller or the card's owner throughout (the engine's previous behavior): contradicts the Gather Specimens ruling for spells; for a card with no controller, the parenthetical of 616.1 read literally gives its owner the choice.
- Why: The rulings for Gather Specimens and Crafty Cutpurse have the would-be controller choose, and the new one after a control change; Land Equilibrium's has "the player who will control that land" choose; and 614.12 judges the entering permanent as it would exist on the battlefield, under that player's control. No ruling addresses a card from a zone where it has no controller, and 616.1's "or its owner if it has no controller" can be read to apply; the user decided on Nov 12 that the would-be controller chooses there too (Reanimate on an opponent's card), as in the Gather Specimens ruling.
- Affects: Gather Specimens, Crafty Cutpurse's entering creatures, Xantcha, and reanimation or theft of an opponent's card ("put onto the battlefield under your control") with two or more replacement effects that need ordering (Essence of the Wild and Infinite Reflection, Master Biomancer and Spike Feeder's own counters).
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/replacement.rs` (`affected_player`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/actions.rs` (`move_objects`: the APNAP order of the choices).
- Tests: `tests/cr/r616_001_audit_would_be_controller.rs` (`the_new_would_be_controller_chooses_among_the_remaining_gather_specimens`, `after_gather_specimens_its_caster_orders_the_copy_effects`, `a_card_reanimated_from_an_opponents_graveyard_has_its_entry_ordered_by_the_reanimator`).
