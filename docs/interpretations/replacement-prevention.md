# Replacement and prevention

Judgment calls about replacement and prevention effects (CR 614–616) and the damage and
draw events they modify. Format and kinds: `docs/agents/items/interpretation-register.md`;
index: `docs/INTERPRETATIONS.md`.

## shared-color-source-locked: "A source of your choice that shares a color with X" compares with X's colors as the effect is created
- Kind: interpretation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 609.7a "... The source is chosen when the effect is created. ..." / 609.7b "Some effects from resolved spells and abilities prevent or replace damage from sources with certain properties, such as a creature or a source of a particular color. When the source would deal damage, the “shield” rechecks the source’s properties. If the properties no longer match, the damage isn’t prevented or replaced. ..."
- Question: Protective Sphere ("a source of your choice that shares a color with the mana spent on this activation cost") and Mourner's Shield ("... that shares a color with the exiled card") name a property relative to something else. When the shield rechecks the source's properties (609.7b), which colors does it compare with: those of the mana or exiled card as the effect was created, or as they are then?
- Decision: The colors as the effect is created. The source must have one of them both when it's chosen and when it would deal damage; the exiled card leaving exile, or the activation's mana being long gone, doesn't change them.
- Alternatives: Recheck against the exiled card as it is when the damage would be dealt (impossible for the mana spent, which no longer exists).
- Why: The mana spent on the activation exists only while the ability resolves, so the effect has to remember its colors; treating the exiled card the same way keeps the two wordings consistent, and the source's own colors are still rechecked as 609.7b requires.
- Affects: Protective Sphere, Mourner's Shield.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/prevention.rs` (`lock_filter`: `SharesColor`, `SHARES_COLOR_WITH_MANA_SPENT`).
- Tests: `tests/cards/c4_mana_provenance.rs` (`protective_sphere_chooses_a_source_sharing_a_color_with_the_mana_spent`).

## per-point-prevention-batch: A counter-bounded prevention shield counts its counters once for simultaneous damage
- Kind: deviation
- Status: decided
- Rules: 615.7 "Some prevention effects generated by static abilities ... prevent only a portion of the damage ..." / 615.5 "Some prevention effects also include an additional effect, which may refer to the amount of damage that was prevented. The prevention takes place at the time the original event would have happened; the rest of the effect takes place immediately afterward."
- Question: Rock Hydra ("For each 1 damage that would be dealt to ~, if it has a +1/+1 counter on it, remove a +1/+1 counter from it and prevent that 1 damage.") removes one counter for each 1 damage it prevents. When several sources deal damage to it at once (several blockers), how much is prevented?
- Decision: Each damage event is prevented up to the number of +1/+1 counters the Hydra has; the counters are removed once, after all the simultaneous damage (as many as the damage, at most the counters it has). With 3 counters and two simultaneous 2-damage events, 4 damage is prevented and 3 counters are removed.
- Alternatives: Count down the counters between the simultaneous events, so no more damage is prevented than the counters it had (3 in the example).
- Why: A prevention effect's additional instruction happens once after the simultaneous damage (615.5, as for the engine's other prevention follow-ups); keeping a running count would need a per-batch budget the pipeline doesn't have. Single-source damage (the usual case) is exact.
- Affects: Rock Hydra.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/oracle/patterns/prevention_5.rs` (`per_point_counter_prevention`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/replacement.rs` (`PreventAndThen`).
- Tests: `tests/cards/c4_replacements.rs` (`rock_hydra_prevents_damage_up_to_its_counters_removing_one_for_each`).

## per-source-prevention-division: A shared "prevent X of that damage" is used on a source's recipients in the order the damage is processed
- Kind: policy
- Status: decided
- Rules: 615.7 "Some prevention effects generated by static abilities ... prevent only a portion of the damage ..." / 616.1 "If two or more replacement and/or prevention effects are attempting to modify the way an event affects an object or player, the affected object's controller (or its owner if it has no controller) or the affected player chooses one to apply ..."
- Question: Cover of Winter prevents X of the combat damage each creature would deal to you and/or creatures you control, shared among those recipients; its ruling says "you decide how to divide the prevention effect" when a creature's damage is assigned to several of them (trample). How is it divided?
- Decision: The shield is applied to the source's damage events in the order the engine replaces simultaneous damage (APNAP by the recipient's controller, then the order `prevention::order_for_shields` gives), using up X before moving on; no decision is asked.
- Alternatives: Ask the player to divide X among the recipients (a new decision for a rare case).
- Why: The total prevented is always what the rules require (X of each source's damage); only which of your recipients keeps the damage could differ, which matters rarely.
- Affects: Cover of Winter.
- Code: `crates/mtg-engine/src/replacement.rs` (`PreventPerSource`, `prevented_per_source`); `crates/mtg-engine/src/actions.rs` (`deal_damage_batch`).
- Tests: `tests/cards/c4_replacements.rs` (`cover_of_winter_prevents_x_of_each_creatures_combat_damage`).
