# Interpretation register

Generated by `cargo run --release -p mtg-tools -- interpretations --write` from `docs/interpretations/*.md`; don't edit by hand.

Every rules judgment call the engine makes has an entry: an **interpretation** (the rules are ambiguous or silent and we chose a reading), a **deviation** (the engine knowingly differs from the Comprehensive Rules) or a **policy** (the rules leave it to a player or to procedure and the engine decides). **Provisional** entries are calls a reasonable judge could make the other way; they await review. Tests that pin an entry cite it with `interp!("<id>")`. What the engine doesn't support at all is a scope decision, listed in `docs/DEFERRED.md` instead.

## Provisional entries

- **range-combat-trigger-events** (interpretation, [players-multiplayer](interpretations/players-multiplayer.md)): The trigger event of "attacks", "blocks" and "blocks or becomes blocked" is the creature alone (CR 801.7)
  - Question: The example settles "becomes blocked" versus "becomes blocked by a creature". Which players and creatures are part of the event of the other combat triggers that don't name them: "whenever [a creature] attacks" (the player, planeswalker or battle it attacks; the other attacking creatures), "whenever [a creature] blocks" (the creature it blocks) and "whenever [a creature] blocks or becomes blocked" (the creature it blocks or is blocked by)?
  - Decision: As in the example, only what the trigger condition names: the event of "[a creature] attacks", "blocks", "becomes blocked" and "blocks or becomes blocked" is that creature attacking, blocking or becoming blocked, and it happens within a player's range of influence if the creature is within it. Conditions that name the other party ("attacks you", "attacks a player", "blocks a creature", "becomes blocked by a creature") still include it.
- **illegal-action-reversal-restores-libraries** (deviation, [stack-casting-costs](interpretations/stack-casting-costs.md)): Reversing an illegal action restores libraries too; no supported card can reach 733.1's exceptions
  - Question: An action that turns out to be illegal is reversed, but the library actions taken while attempting it stay. Does the engine keep them?
  - Decision: No. The engine reverses an illegal action by restoring a copy of the whole game taken before it began (`Game::roll_back`), libraries, their order and the random generator included. No fully supported card can reach the difference: a scan of the compiled abilities of all 31,293 fully supported cards (every cost, keyword cost and mana ability) found these library actions in costs, and none can be followed by a failure in the same action: costs exiling the top cards of a library (Arc-Slogger, Royal Herbalist, Seasoned Tactician, Whirling Catapult, Thought Lash), milling (Rot Farm Skeleton, Sinister Concoction; and Millikin, Deranged Assistant, The Warring Triad, whose mill costs make their abilities not mana abilities, 605.1a), putting cards on a library (Penance, Leashling, Hidden Retreat from hand; Battlefield Scrounger from graveyard; Timestream Navigator itself), and costs paid as an ability resolves (Gurzigost, Anurid Scavenger, Deep Spawn; the cumulative upkeep of Thought Lash and Psychic Vortex). The engine checks that every part of a cost can be paid after the mana is paid and before paying any other part, pays library actions after all other parts (601.2h), and nothing after the payment can make casting or activating illegal. Activated mana abilities can't move library cards (605.1a), the automatic payment uses only mana abilities whose costs are tapping, sacrificing, life and counters, and no supported mana ability (activated or triggered) moves, shuffles or reveals library cards (Rosheen, Sacellum Godspeaker and Metalworker reveal cards from a hand). "When you spend this mana" draws (Gilanra, Lapis Orb of Dragonkind, Brass Infiniscope) and Shaun & Rebecca's reflexive mill are ordinary triggered abilities, which 733.1 says don't trigger from an undone action. A card cast from the top of a library goes to the stack, which may be reversed, and the next card isn't revealed while it's cast (401.5). No cost searches or shuffles a library or reveals library cards. Abilities that add mana and draw a card (Chromatic Sphere, the Eggs such as Skycloud Egg) or mill (Millikin, Deranged Assistant, Charmed Pendant) aren't mana abilities (605.1a), so they can't be activated while an action is attempted. The cards that could reach the exceptions aren't legal in any format and stay unsupported (`docs/DEFERRED.md`): Panglacial Shinobi (library ninjutsu: activated from a library, which reveals the card, 602.2a, with a cost that shuffles a creature into a library) and the scrying mana abilities of Under-Construction Skyscraper and The Secret Lair.
- **end-of-combat-on-your-next-turn-without-combat** (interpretation, [triggers-durations](interpretations/triggers-durations.md)): "Until end of combat on your next turn" ends with a turn that has no combat
  - Question: Brazen Cannonade's "Until end of combat on your next turn, you may play that card" names the end of the combat phase of your next turn. If that turn has no combat phase (all combat phases skipped), when does the permission end?
  - Decision: It ends as that turn ends (in its cleanup step, with "until end of turn" effects). With a combat phase, it ends as the first combat phase of that turn ends.
- **batched-alternatives-one-batch** (deviation, [triggers-durations](interpretations/triggers-durations.md)): An ability with two "one or more" trigger conditions triggers once for a batch that matches both
  - Question: A single group of simultaneous events can contain events matching both "one or more" conditions (a permanent phasing out and a card being exiled at the same time). Does the ability trigger once per condition, or once?
  - Decision: The engine's batch detection gives an ability whose trigger conditions are several "one or more" batches one trigger per group of simultaneous events: that of the first condition the group's events match. Separate groups (the usual case: an effect phasing permanents out, another exiling cards) each trigger it.
