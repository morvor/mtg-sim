# Interpretation register

Generated by `cargo run --release -p mtg-tools -- interpretations --write` from `docs/interpretations/*.md`; don't edit by hand.

Every rules judgment call the engine makes has an entry: an **interpretation** (the rules are ambiguous or silent and we chose a reading), a **deviation** (the engine knowingly differs from the Comprehensive Rules) or a **policy** (the rules leave it to a player or to procedure and the engine decides). **Provisional** entries are calls a reasonable judge could make the other way; they await review. Tests that pin an entry cite it with `interp!("<id>")`. What the engine doesn't support at all is a scope decision, listed in `docs/DEFERRED.md` instead.

## Provisional entries

- **room-door-unlock-timestamp** (interpretation, [characteristics-layers](interpretations/characteristics-layers.md)): A Room door unlocked after the permanent entered has its static abilities' effects timestamped from the unlocking
  - Question: Unlocking a door gives the permanent its half's rules text, but neither the permanent nor any effect gets a new timestamp under 613.7. In which order do that half's static effects apply relative to effects that began between the Room entering and the door being unlocked?
  - Decision: From the unlocking: the static abilities of a half unlocked after the permanent entered have the later of the permanent's timestamp and the moment that door was (last) unlocked, in layers and in rule-modifying effects alike; a door locked and unlocked again is timestamped from the new unlocking. A half that was cast (and so entered unlocked, 709.5d) uses the permanent's timestamp.
- **put-onto-battlefield-blocking-retriggers-becomes-blocked-by** (interpretation, [combat](interpretations/combat.md)): A creature put onto the battlefield blocking triggers "becomes blocked by a creature" again
  - Question: Deathgazer: "Whenever this creature blocks or becomes blocked by a nonblack creature, destroy that creature at end of combat." Aetherplasm (blue) blocks it, returns itself to hand and is put back onto the battlefield blocking Deathgazer. The old trigger's "that creature" is the old object (CR 400.7), but does the new Aetherplasm trigger Deathgazer again?
  - Decision: Yes. The engine follows CR 509.3d: Deathgazer becomes blocked by the new Aetherplasm, its ability triggers again, and the new Aetherplasm is destroyed at end of combat. Aetherplasm dodges only the abilities that already triggered for the old object.
- **linked-reveal-trigger-as-reflexive** (deviation, [dice-coins](interpretations/dice-coins.md)): Priority Boarding's "whenever you reveal a card ... this way" is checked as the first ability reveals
  - Question: "Whenever you roll a die, you may reveal the top card of your library. Do this only once each turn. Whenever you reveal a card with mana value less than the result this way, you may exile it. If you do, you may play it this turn." The second triggered ability triggers on the first one's reveal, with "the result" the first ability's roll. How is it modeled?
  - Decision: The engine compiles the second ability into the first: after the reveal, if Priority Boarding is still on the battlefield and the revealed card's mana value is less than the result of the roll that triggered the first ability, a triggered ability triggers then (like a reflexive one, CR 603.12) and does the rest, chosen and resolved as usual.
- **history-counts-players-who-left** (interpretation, [players-multiplayer](interpretations/players-multiplayer.md)): "For each opponent who [did something] this turn" counts opponents who have since left the game
  - Question: An effect counts the players for whom something happened this turn ("for each of your opponents who lost life this turn", "for each opponent who was dealt damage this turn"). Is a player who has since left the game counted? The rulings disagree: Belbe, Corrupted Observer, Teysa, Opulent Oligarch, Kaito, Bane of Nightmares, Moonshae Pixie, Rakdos, Lord of Riots, Chandra's Incinerator, Dunerider Outlaw and Thought Sponge say yes; Gnoll War Band's (2022) says "Opponents who have left the game will not be counted when determining how many opponents have been dealt damage this turn."
  - Decision: Counted. `Value::EventsThisTurn` and `Value::EventsDuring` with `Tally::Players` count every player the matching events were about, in the game or not. Counts of players now ("for each opponent you have") are unaffected: they only see players in the game. Likewise a value taken over players of what each did this turn (`Value::OverPlayers` of a `Value::EventsThisTurn` or `Value::EventsDuring` tally, or of a threshold on one: "the greatest number of cards an opponent has drawn this turn", "for each opponent who drew two or more cards this turn") considers every player, in the game or not; other values over players ("the highest life total among all players") only see players in the game.
- **nihiloor-taps-one-opponent-at-a-time** (deviation, [triggers-durations](interpretations/triggers-durations.md)): Nihiloor's "for each opponent, tap up to one untapped creature you control" taps for each opponent in turn
  - Question: Nihiloor: "When Nihiloor enters, for each opponent, tap up to one untapped creature you control. When you do, gain control of target creature that player controls with power less than or equal to the tapped creature's power ..." The ruling taps the chosen creatures at the same time. How does the engine perform it?
  - Decision: For each opponent in turn order (starting after you), you choose up to one untapped creature you control and tap it, and that tap's reflexive ability (about that opponent and that tapped creature) is created; the next opponent's choice follows. The reflexive abilities go on the stack together afterwards, in the order you choose (603.3b), as the ruling says.
- **ice-cauldron-last-exiled** (interpretation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): Ice Cauldron's "last card exiled with ~" is the last card its first ability exiled with a permission to cast it
  - Question: Which card is "the last card exiled with" the Cauldron, and is it still that card once it's cast from exile?
  - Decision: The card its first ability most recently exiled (and gave the permission to cast); the mana can be spent only to cast a spell that was that card in exile as it was cast (the spell is a new object, CR 400.7, whose previous object was the exiled card). If an activation exiled nothing, the card exiled before stays the last one.
- **cyclopean-tomb-land-choice** (policy, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): Cyclopean Tomb's controller chooses which of its lands loses its mire counters, among those with mire counters
  - Question: Several lands qualify: which one? And does a qualifying land that no longer has mire counters (they were removed some other way) use up an upkeep?
  - Decision: The Tomb's controller chooses among the qualifying lands that still have mire counters (all qualifying lands if none has any). A land stops qualifying once mire counters have been removed from it this way; removing none (it had none) doesn't count as removing one "with ~".
- **library-arranged-by-owner-not-instructing-player** (deviation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): Cards an effect puts into another player's library "in any order" are arranged by their owner
  - Question: When a spell's controller puts cards owned by another player into that player's library "in any order" (Misinformation, Drafna's Restoration, Lodestone Bauble, Agonizing Memories), who arranges them: the cards' owner (401.4) or the player the text instructs to put them (the card's "in any order", read as overriding 401.4, as the Misinformation and Sealed Fate rulings imply)?
  - Decision: For cards moved from another zone (graveyard, hand) into a library, the owner arranges them (`zones::order_simultaneous`), whatever the text says. Cards rearranged within a library by a dig ("look at the top N cards of target opponent's library ... put the rest back in any order") are arranged by the player the text instructs, as Sealed Fate's ruling says (`dig_steps::rearrange`).
- **self-shuffle-gone-library-still-shuffled** (interpretation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): "Its owner shuffles it into their library" shuffles that library even when it has left
  - Question: Blitz Hellion ("At the beginning of the end step, this creature's owner shuffles it into their library.") has left the battlefield when its ability resolves. It stays where it is; is its owner's library shuffled?
  - Decision: Yes. The engine follows CR 701.24c, which is newer than the ruling and has an example of the same shape (Guile exiled in response to "shuffle it into its owner's library"): the object stays where it is and the library is shuffled. Shuffle triggers trigger.
