# Interpretation register

Generated by `cargo run --release -p mtg-tools -- interpretations --write` from `docs/interpretations/*.md`; don't edit by hand.

Every rules judgment call the engine makes has an entry: an **interpretation** (the rules are ambiguous or silent and we chose a reading), a **deviation** (the engine knowingly differs from the Comprehensive Rules) or a **policy** (the rules leave it to a player or to procedure and the engine decides). **Provisional** entries are calls a reasonable judge could make the other way; they await review. Tests that pin an entry cite it with `interp!("<id>")`. What the engine doesn't support at all is a scope decision, listed in `docs/DEFERRED.md` instead.

## Provisional entries

- **conditional-pt-setting-not-cda** (interpretation, [characteristics-layers](interpretations/characteristics-layers.md)): "During your turn, ~'s power and toughness are each ... During turns other than yours, ... each 2" sets power and toughness in layer 7b on the battlefield
  - Question: Angry Mob (printed 2+*/2+*) says "During your turn, Angry Mob's power and toughness are each equal to 2 plus the number of Swamps your opponents control. During turns other than yours, Angry Mob's power and toughness are each 2." Each sentence sets the values only if a condition is met (whose turn it is), but together they set them on every turn. Is that one characteristic-defining ability (layer 7a, every zone) or conditional setting effects (layer 7b, battlefield only)?
  - Decision: Conditional setting effects: two static abilities, each with its condition ("it's your turn" / "it isn't"), setting power and toughness in layer 7b while the creature is on the battlefield; elsewhere it has its printed 2+* with * as 0 (2/2). The same reading the engine already gives Gaea's Liege ("As long as ~ isn't attacking, ... As long as ~ is attacking, ...", `statics_cda::conditional_cda`).
- **control-gain-prohibition-before-controller** (interpretation, [characteristics-layers](interpretations/characteristics-layers.md)): "Other players can't gain control of [them]" stops changes of control away from the permanent's controller, judged as that player controls it
  - Question: Guardian Beast says "As long as ~ is untapped, noncreature artifacts you control ... other players can't gain control of them." The CR has no rule for "can't gain control". When does it apply, and what happens to a control-changing effect that took hold while Guardian Beast was tapped once it untaps?
  - Decision: A control-changing effect (layer 2) doesn't give a permanent to a player when the prohibition applies to it as its controller before that change sees it: the prohibition's controller controlled it, it matches "noncreature artifacts you control" with that controller, and the new controller is another player. A permanent another player already controls (because the effect began while the prohibition didn't apply) stays with that player when the prohibition applies again. Sacrifices, destruction and other events are unaffected.
- **name-setting-in-layer-3** (interpretation, [characteristics-layers](interpretations/characteristics-layers.md)): An effect that sets a permanent's name applies in layer 3
  - Question: Psychic Paper says the equipped creature's "name and creature type are the last chosen name and creature type". No layer in 613.1 names an object's name; in which layer does a name-setting effect apply?
  - Decision: With the text-changing effects, in layer 3 (612.8 lists name changes in the text-changing rules), in timestamp order with them; the creature type part is a type-changing effect in layer 4 that replaces the creature's creature types with the chosen one. Each applies only once its choice was made (607.5a).
- **that-excess-damage-this-turn** (interpretation, [combat](interpretations/combat.md)): "that excess damage" after "was dealt excess damage this turn" is the turn's total
  - Question: Overclocked Electromancer: "Whenever this creature deals combat damage to a creature, if that creature was dealt excess damage this turn, you get X {E}, where X is that excess damage." Is X the excess damage of this combat damage, or all the excess damage the creature was dealt this turn?
  - Decision: All the excess damage that creature was dealt this turn (the excess damage the condition asks about), counted as the ability resolves.
- **put-onto-battlefield-blocking-retriggers-becomes-blocked-by** (interpretation, [combat](interpretations/combat.md)): A creature put onto the battlefield blocking triggers "becomes blocked by a creature" again
  - Question: Deathgazer: "Whenever this creature blocks or becomes blocked by a nonblack creature, destroy that creature at end of combat." Aetherplasm (blue) blocks it, returns itself to hand and is put back onto the battlefield blocking Deathgazer. The old trigger's "that creature" is the old object (CR 400.7), but does the new Aetherplasm trigger Deathgazer again?
  - Decision: Yes. The engine follows CR 509.3d: Deathgazer becomes blocked by the new Aetherplasm, its ability triggers again, and the new Aetherplasm is destroyed at end of combat. Aetherplasm dodges only the abilities that already triggered for the old object.
- **next-roll-extra-die-same-roll** (interpretation, [dice-coins](interpretations/dice-coins.md)): Several "the next time ... would roll" extra-die effects all apply to that one roll
  - Question: Two Bamboozling Beeble effects wait for the same player's next roll ("The next time target player would roll one or more dice this turn, instead they roll that many dice plus one and you choose one of those rolls to ignore"). Does the next roll use up both, and in what order are the rolls ignored? And with a static "instead roll that many dice plus one and ignore the lowest roll" (an extra die the roller ignores)?
  - Decision: Each such effect applies once to the player's next roll instruction (or planar die roll) this turn, so all pending ones are used up by it: the player rolls one more die for each. The static extra-die effects' lowest rolls are ignored first; then each Beeble effect's controller, in the order the effects were created, chooses one of the remaining rolls to ignore, seeing all the results.
- **linked-reveal-trigger-as-reflexive** (deviation, [dice-coins](interpretations/dice-coins.md)): Priority Boarding's "whenever you reveal a card ... this way" is checked as the first ability reveals
  - Question: "Whenever you roll a die, you may reveal the top card of your library. Do this only once each turn. Whenever you reveal a card with mana value less than the result this way, you may exile it. If you do, you may play it this turn." The second triggered ability triggers on the first one's reveal, with "the result" the first ability's roll. How is it modeled?
  - Decision: The engine compiles the second ability into the first: after the reveal, if Priority Boarding is still on the battlefield and the revealed card's mana value is less than the result of the roll that triggered the first ability, a triggered ability triggers then (like a reflexive one, CR 603.12) and does the rest, chosen and resolved as usual.
- **history-counts-players-who-left** (interpretation, [players-multiplayer](interpretations/players-multiplayer.md)): "For each opponent who [did something] this turn" counts opponents who have since left the game
  - Question: An effect counts the players for whom something happened this turn ("for each of your opponents who lost life this turn", "for each opponent who was dealt damage this turn"). Is a player who has since left the game counted? The rulings disagree: Belbe, Corrupted Observer, Teysa, Opulent Oligarch, Kaito, Bane of Nightmares, Moonshae Pixie, Rakdos, Lord of Riots, Chandra's Incinerator, Dunerider Outlaw and Thought Sponge say yes; Gnoll War Band's (2022) says "Opponents who have left the game will not be counted when determining how many opponents have been dealt damage this turn."
  - Decision: Counted. `Value::EventsThisTurn` and `Value::EventsDuring` with `Tally::Players` count every player the matching events were about, in the game or not. Counts of players now ("for each opponent you have") are unaffected: they only see players in the game. Likewise a value taken over players of what each did this turn (`Value::OverPlayers` of a `Value::EventsThisTurn` or `Value::EventsDuring` tally, or of a threshold on one: "the greatest number of cards an opponent has drawn this turn", "for each opponent who drew two or more cards this turn") considers every player, in the game or not; other values over players ("the highest life total among all players") only see players in the game.
- **reversal-kept-library-actions-trigger** (interpretation, [stack-casting-costs](interpretations/stack-casting-costs.md)): Abilities trigger on the library actions a reversal keeps, but not on the payment they were part of
  - Question: The library actions an illegal action took while it was attempted stay (`illegal-action-reversal-restores-libraries`). Do abilities trigger on them ("whenever you draw a card", "when ~ leaves the battlefield", "whenever one or more cards are put into exile from your library")? And on the payment a replacement effect made one of them (a creature sacrificed for mana that Darksteel Colossus's ability shuffles into the library instead: "whenever you sacrifice a permanent")?
  - Decision: The kept actions are actions that happened, not undone ones, so abilities trigger on them as on any other: as zone changes (a permanent leaving the battlefield for a library: Nefarious Lich's "When Nefarious Lich leaves the battlefield, you lose the game" after Lich's Mirror shuffled it away in the middle of a payment), draws, mills, shuffles and reveals. The payment, the cast or the activation they were part of is undone, so nothing triggers on it: a kept move is performed again without the cause that was the payment (a sacrifice, a discard, a destruction, a cost; `reversal::kept_cause`), so "whenever you sacrifice" and "whenever you discard" don't trigger, and neither does anything on the parts of the event the replacement didn't keep (damage, life). What triggered while the action was attempted is dropped with it, and triggers again from the kept actions, in the restored game, once they're performed.
- **jeweled-amulet-several-types** (interpretation, [stack-casting-costs](interpretations/stack-casting-costs.md)): When mana of several types was spent on Jeweled Amulet's activation, its controller chooses the type noted
  - Question: The printed cost is {1}, so one type of mana is spent. A cost increase ("Activated abilities cost {2} more to activate unless they're mana abilities", Suppression Field) can make the controller spend mana of several types: which one is "the type of mana spent"?
  - Decision: As the ability resolves, its controller chooses one of the types spent, and that type is noted (with one type spent, that one).
- **x-defined-by-target** (interpretation, [stack-casting-costs](interpretations/stack-casting-costs.md)): An {X} in an activation cost that the text defines by the ability's target must be announced as that value
  - Question: Reflecting Mirror: "{X}, {T}: Change the target of target spell with a single target if that target is you. The new target must be a player. X is twice the mana value of that spell." X is defined by the target, but the cost's X is announced (601.2b) before targets are chosen (601.2c). How is X determined?
  - Decision: The player announces X, and the target must be a spell whose mana value makes the definition true (twice its mana value is X); an X no target can satisfy (an odd X here) can't be announced. The result is that X is exactly the defined value for the chosen target, as 107.3c requires.
- **x-defined-for-a-player** (interpretation, [stack-casting-costs](interpretations/stack-casting-costs.md)): "X is the number of ... in an opponent's graveyard as you cast this spell" is that number for one opponent, announced by the caster
  - Question: Spoils of War ({X}{B}): "X is the number of artifact and/or creature cards in an opponent's graveyard as you cast this spell." With several opponents, whose graveyard defines X? Its ruling: "X is set as you cast the spell to be exactly the number of appropriate cards in an opponent's graveyard. You can't choose a higher or lower value for X."
  - Decision: The caster picks the opponent; announcing X picks among the values the opponents' graveyards give (X must be one of them, CR 601.2b), so X is exactly the number for one opponent. With one opponent, X is that number.
- **batch-they-condition-all** (interpretation, [triggers-durations](interpretations/triggers-durations.md)): "If they [did something]" in a "one or more" trigger means all of them
  - Question: Twilight Diviner: "Whenever one or more other creatures you control enter, if they entered or were cast from a graveyard, create a token that's a copy of one of them." When several creatures enter at once and only some of them came from a graveyard (or were cast from one), does the condition hold?
  - Decision: "they" is the whole group: the condition holds only if each of the creatures entered from a graveyard or was cast from one (as "if they entered from a graveyard" already reads in the engine). "One of them" is then any of the creatures that entered. Wordings with "one or more of them" ("if one or more of them entered from a graveyard") are the some-of-them reading.
- **one-or-more-an-opponent-controls-per-opponent** (interpretation, [triggers-durations](interpretations/triggers-durations.md)): "One or more creatures an opponent controls deal combat damage to you" triggers once for each opponent
  - Question: Norn's Decree: "Whenever one or more creatures an opponent controls deal combat damage to you, that opponent gets a poison counter." When creatures of several opponents deal combat damage to you at once (shared team turns, CR 805.10a: both players of the attacking team attack), does it trigger once, or once for each of those opponents? And who is "that opponent"?
  - Decision: Once for each opponent whose creatures dealt you combat damage, and "that opponent" is the controller of that trigger's creatures. Without shared team turns only the active player's creatures deal combat damage, so it triggers once and that player gets the counter.
- **other-than-trigger-object** (interpretation, [triggers-durations](interpretations/triggers-durations.md)): "Other [type]s" in the intervening "if" of a trigger about "a [type]" on a source without that type means other than the triggering object
  - Question: Portcullis (an artifact) reads "Whenever a creature enters, if there are two or more other creatures on the battlefield, exile that creature." "Other" usually means other than the object with the ability, which can't be what an artifact counting creatures means. Other than what? And what if Portcullis is itself a creature (March of the Machines)?
  - Decision: In the intervening "if" of an enters, leaves-the-battlefield or dies trigger about "a [type]" (not about the source), "other [type]s" means other than the triggering object when the source's printed type line lacks that type. The reading is fixed when the text is compiled: an animated Portcullis is one of the "other creatures" (it isn't the entering creature), so it counts.
- **nihiloor-taps-one-opponent-at-a-time** (deviation, [triggers-durations](interpretations/triggers-durations.md)): Nihiloor's "for each opponent, tap up to one untapped creature you control" taps for each opponent in turn
  - Question: Nihiloor: "When Nihiloor enters, for each opponent, tap up to one untapped creature you control. When you do, gain control of target creature that player controls with power less than or equal to the tapped creature's power ..." The ruling taps the chosen creatures at the same time. How does the engine perform it?
  - Decision: For each opponent in turn order (starting after you), you choose up to one untapped creature you control and tap it, and that tap's reflexive ability (about that opponent and that tapped creature) is created; the next opponent's choice follows. The reflexive abilities go on the stack together afterwards, in the order you choose (603.3b), as the ruling says.
- **looked-at-hand-card-stays-seen** (interpretation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): A card looked at in another player's hand stays visible to the looker while it remains there
  - Question: Urza's Bauble: "Look at a card at random in target player's hand." By 701.20e/701.20a the card is shown only for as long as the effect needs it, but the player remembers what they saw. Should the looker's view keep showing it?
  - Decision: The ability's controller looks at one card chosen at random (only they see it, it isn't revealed) and may see that card for as long as it remains in that hand; once it changes zones it's a new object and the permission ends. In mtg-api observations the card shows in the looker's view.
- **alteration-host-of-two-types** (interpretation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): "Another permanent of that type" for a host with both named types
  - Question: Enchantment Alteration moves "target Aura attached to a creature or land to another permanent of that type". For an Aura on a land creature (Dryad Arbor), "that type" could be either.

- **ice-cauldron-last-exiled** (interpretation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): Ice Cauldron's "last card exiled with ~" is the last card its first ability exiled with a permission to cast it
  - Question: Which card is "the last card exiled with" the Cauldron, and is it still that card once it's cast from exile?
  - Decision: The card its first ability most recently exiled (and gave the permission to cast); the mana can be spent only to cast a spell that was that card in exile as it was cast (the spell is a new object, CR 400.7, whose previous object was the exiled card). - **cyclopean-tomb-land-choice** (policy, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): Cyclopean Tomb's controller chooses which of its lands loses its mire counters, among those with mire counters
  - Question: Several lands qualify: which one? And does a qualifying land that no longer has mire counters (they were removed some other way) use up an upkeep?
  - Decision: The Tomb's controller chooses among the qualifying lands that still have mire counters (all qualifying lands if none has any). A land stops qualifying once mire counters have been removed from it this way; removing none (it had none) doesn't count as removing one "with ~".
- **library-arranged-by-owner-not-instructing-player** (deviation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): Cards an effect puts into another player's library "in any order" are arranged by their owner
  - Question: When a spell's controller puts cards owned by another player into that player's library "in any order" (Misinformation, Drafna's Restoration, Lodestone Bauble, Agonizing Memories), who arranges them: the cards' owner (401.4) or the player the text instructs to put them (the card's "in any order", read as overriding 401.4, as the Misinformation and Sealed Fate rulings imply)?
  - Decision: For cards moved from another zone (graveyard, hand) into a library, the owner arranges them (`zones::order_simultaneous`), whatever the text says. Cards rearranged within a library by a dig ("look at the top N cards of target opponent's library ... put the rest back in any order") are arranged by the player the text instructs, as Sealed Fate's ruling says (`dig_steps::rearrange`).
- **self-shuffle-gone-library-still-shuffled** (interpretation, [zones-objects](interpretations/zones-objects.md)): "Its owner shuffles it into their library" shuffles that library even when it has left
  - Question: Blitz Hellion ("At the beginning of the end step, this creature's owner shuffles it into their library.") has left the battlefield when its ability resolves. It stays where it is; is its owner's library shuffled?
  - Decision: Yes. The engine follows CR 701.24c, which is newer than the ruling and has an example of the same shape (Guile exiled in response to "shuffle it into its owner's library"): the object stays where it is and the library is shuffled. Shuffle triggers trigger.
