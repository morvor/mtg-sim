# Card support, round 6: work plan for the last unsupported cards

Generated in the planning item `cards6-plan` (Nov 4) from the release `mtg-tools` and the Scryfall data in `data/`, at the integration branch's commit ae329d28, after all six round-5 items had landed (cards5-game-rules-dice, cards5-choices-players, cards5-mana-replacements, cards5-referents-statics, cards5-costs-sets, cards5-manual-defs; `git log --grep '^cards5-<slug>:'`). Round 5: `card-support-plan-5.md`; round 4: `card-support-plan-4.md`; earlier rounds: `card-support-plan-3.md`, `-2.md`, `card-support-plan.md`. The inputs, lists and the check script are in `round6/`; the probe scripts are round 3's (`round3/probe.py`, `round3/autoprobe.py`).

## Baseline

- `mtg-tools card-coverage`: **32,059 / 32,111** paper cards fully supported (99.8%; 31,908 compiled, 151 with hand-written abilities); abilities 60,130 / 60,185. Verified: 15,767; round-trip or better: 27,922. On Nov 1 (when the item was written): 32,040; at the end of round-5 planning (Oct 29): 31,951.
- Unsupported paper cards: **52**, with **55** distinct unsupported texts (55 card-text lines; `mtg-tools unsupported --paper --by-card`, numbers as N, the card's own name as ~). Snapshot: `round6/baseline.tsv`.
- Of these, **38 texts on 35 cards are deferred** under DEFERRED.md (`round6/deferred.tsv`: the 24 sticker cards with 27 texts; Pearl Collector, Tenacious Pup and Sigardian Evangel, digital-only mechanics; Steamflogger Boss, Contraptions; Chaos Orb and Falling Star, dexterity; the five Conspiracy face-down draft cards). The list is round 5's deferred list minus nothing: every deferred text is still unsupported, and the digital-version row "Name Sticker" Goblin is kept (recorded, not counted).
- The other **17 texts on 17 cards** are the long tail: each card has exactly one unsupported text, every text was in a landed cards5 item (cards5-referents-statics 9, cards5-choices-players 6, cards5-costs-sets 1, cards5-manual-defs 1) and most in two to four items before. No text is new: all 55 are in a cards5 list or round 5's deferred list.
- This plan places all 55 texts exactly once (`round6/check_plan.py` passes against a fresh `mtg-tools unsupported --paper --by-card`): **15** in 4 compiled cards6 items, **2** in cards6-manual-defs, **38** deferred. No item is in flight (all cards5 items have landed), so there is no in-flight or pending list.

## Method

1. Snapshot: `mtg-tools unsupported --paper --by-card` and `--raw` from the release binary built at ae329d28. Each text was matched against the cards5 lists and `round5/deferred.tsv` (the previous item is in each list's `previous_item` column).
2. Re-probe (`round3/autoprobe.py` over the 17 texts, the card's other paragraphs kept as context): the first failing sentence of each. Three texts' first failing piece moved since round 5: Baron Helmut Zemo's cost compiles (cards5-costs-sets built the mana-symbol count), so "Copy those exiled cards" fails first; Vesuvan Shapeshifter's optional as-enters choice compiles (cards5-choices-players), so the choice as it is turned face up and the copy body fail; Chef's Kiss now fails first on the gain-control target's filter (its plain form compiles) rather than the reselection.
3. Drilling (145 hand-written probes, `round6/drills.tsv` ids `C6-<card>-<n>`; 47 compile, 98 fail): each failing sentence cut down to the failing words on the real card, by replacing one phrase at a time with a simpler one that compiles and by trying the construct on neutral cards (Divination, Icy Manipulator, Grizzly Bears, Glorious Anthem). Each list's `note` starts with "drilled: X compiles; Y fails", names the second and third failing pieces, and names the engine need where the gap is in the engine rather than the grammar.
4. Groups: as the item asked, each item is one engine feature or one grammar area rather than a family of wordings. The 15 compiled texts make four items by area (referents and values; copies and casting from them; per-player results, choices and loops; turn history and permissions), so no two items edit the same files except copy.rs (noted in both briefs). Two genuine one-offs make the manual item.
5. One-off check: every construct that looked unique was searched for over all in-scope paper cards (paper by `ScryfallCard::is_paper`'s rule and legal or restricted in a format), by construct, not only by words; the regexes are in each list note. A construct found on one card stays compiled when it is a parameter or wording of a compiled construct (Xanathar's top-of-library permissions are One with the Multiverse's with a player; Toggo's token name is the self-name rule; Timesifter's tie loop is the tie rule of the greatest-mana-value comparison it shares with Psychic Battle; Lim-Dûl's "last cards you looked at" is the last iteration of Dance with Calamity's loop; Glyph of Reincarnation's "the player who controlled that creature the last time it became blocked" is a field of the block history both Glyphs need; Backdraft's per-spell damage is a source filter on the compiled damage history; Chef's Kiss' exclusion is an argument of the random reselection). Two constructs kept compiled are shared with a hand-written card (found in review): Timesifter's "the player who exiled the card with the greatest mana value" is Psychic Battle's reveal-off (src/cards/psychic_battle.rs), and Chef's Kiss' random reselection is Grip of Chaos's (src/cards/grip_of_chaos.rs); as constructs of two cards both must be compiled, and the briefs let the items make those manual cards call the compiled pieces. Two texts are genuine one-offs: River Song (draws from the bottom; its hook landed in cards5-manual-defs) and Vesuvan Shapeshifter (a copy chosen as it is turned face up that ends when it is turned face down).
6. Counts: "touched" = paper cards with at least one of the item's texts; "all texts here" = cards whose every unsupported text is in the item (what the check script computes); every card here has one text, so the two are equal and every card is unlocked by its item alone. Effort = texts × difficulty factor (1.0 = parser grammar over existing engine support; higher where engine work, shared-core risk or decisions are expected). The factors are the highest so far: these texts have resisted two to five items each.

## Dependencies and scheduling

- **No dependencies between items**: no card has texts in two items, and no text's second failing piece belongs to another item.
- **Shared files**: copy.rs is touched by cards6-referents-grammar (Chef's Kiss' copy and reselection) and cards6-copies-casting (casting from among copies): merge often. cards6-history-permissions' casting.rs edit (playing from another player's library) and cards6-copies-casting's cast cap are different functions; merge often. oracle/effects.rs and phrases.rs are shared by all: keep edits small. cards6-manual-defs touches only src/cards/ and generic hooks in facedown.rs/until.rs.
- **Suggested waves** (two agents at a time on these machines): wave 1 — cards6-manual-defs and cards6-referents-grammar (cheapest; 6 cards). Wave 2 — cards6-copies-casting and cards6-history-permissions (6 cards). Wave 3 — cards6-per-player-results (5 cards). The three larger items could also run as one agent each in any order; they edit different files.

## How far this gets

- With each text done with probability p independently (every card here has one text, so the expected cards equal the expected texts): p = 55% → about 9 of the 17 cards; 70% → 12; 85% → 14. River Song (a registration on a tested hook) is near certain; the per-player and history texts are the least likely (they failed in three to five items).
- The rate of the last round on texts that had resisted earlier items: cards5-referents-statics left 9 of its texts (it planned 43, 38 cards), cards5-choices-players 6 of 43, cards5-costs-sets 1 of 14, cards5-manual-defs 1 of 7 (River Song waited for another item); round 5 as a whole fixed 136 - 17 = 119 of 136 planned texts (87%), above its projection of 55-70%. These 17 are the ones that resisted it, so expect p = 55-75%: about 9-13 cards, which would bring the count to about 32,068-32,072 of 32,111 (99.87-99.88%).
- **What is left after round 6**: the 35 cards with deferred texts (DEFERRED.md) and about 4-8 of these 17. Past that there is no long tail to group: each remaining card would be its own engine feature or a hand-written definition, planned one by one, or a user decision on deferral. Reaching 100% of the counted paper cards needs the deferred categories reopened (see "Decisions needed").

## Decisions needed

No decision blocks round 6: no tail text is out of scope, and no deferral is proposed. One question only the user can answer, for after round 6:

1. **Whether to reopen a deferred category to go past ~99.9%.** After round 6 the unsupported counted paper cards will be the 35 deferred ones plus the few tail cards round 6 can't finish. 24 of the 35 are Unfinity sticker cards, which need sticker sheets modelled as objects outside the game with {TK} ticket costs (DEFERRED.md, "Sticker sheets"); the other 11 are single cards in categories deferred for good reasons (digital-only mechanics, Contraptions, dexterity, Conspiracy face-down draft). Recommendation: keep them all deferred; if the user wants the headline number higher, sticker sheets are the only category where one feature unlocks many cards (24), and they would be a separate project (sheets per player, ticket counters, the stickers' printed abilities), not a card-support item.

Readings for implementers to record in the interpretation register (not user decisions): Captain America's divided damage whose amount the cost determines (CR 601.2d, 601.2h; carried over from rounds 4 and 5); who chooses "one of those sorcery spells" for Backdraft (the controller; the CR is silent); the order of choices when casting copies under a total-mana-value cap (Uldaros); The Toymaker's Trap once all five numbers have been chosen ("If you do" fails).

## Adversarial review

Done by the planner as a separate pass after the first placement (no subagent was needed for 17 texts; the drill and review probes were run directly with scripts):

- **Every family sampled with a fresh minimal construct on a neutral card** (33 review probes, `round6/drills.tsv` ids `C6-R-...`, 27 failing, 6 controls compiling): `values` ("Count the number of creatures you control. If the number is even, draw a card"; two effects under one duration on Glorious Anthem); `cost-referents` (an unattach cost's Equipment as a divided amount fails, an exiled card's mana value divided compiles); `token-names` ("Sacrifice Gizmo" in a token named Gizmo fails, "Sacrifice this artifact" compiles); `spell-targets` (a filtered gain-control target, "Change the targets of target spell at random"); `copies` (one target per card type from an opponent's graveyard; "Copy those exiled cards" after an exile cost; "cast one of the copies"; a total-mana-value cap over copies); `per-player-results` (the player who revealed the greatest mana value with a draw; "the creature card they exiled"; each searcher's split; "Each player who controlled one of those creatures"); `each-player-choices` (a number "that hasn't been chosen" on an artifact; an opponent guessing a secret number); `repeat-loops` (a paid optional loop; shuffle then put the looked-at cards on top); `block-history` (target creature as the anchor, destroy-all and single-target); `damage-history` ("Choose a player who attacked this turn"; damage dealt by instant spells this turn); `permissions` (look at each opponent's library top any time; play target player's library top until end of turn); and the one-offs' general forms (a choice as a creature is turned face up; "Until ~ is turned face down"; every player drawing from the bottom). All confirm a general gap, none is specific to the card's wording.
- **Every one-off claim searched** over in-scope paper cards: River Song ("draw.{0,30}bottom of"), Vesuvan Shapeshifter ("is turned face up, you may choose", "until (~|this creature) is turned face down", a granted "turn ~ face down") — one card each. The single-card constructs kept compiled were searched too and are named with their shared construct in method step 5 and in the list notes: Toggo ("named (\w+) with ... Sacrifice \1"), Chef's Kiss ("reselect the targets", "at random"), Timesifter ("tied players"), Lim-Dûl's Vault ("last cards you looked at", "As many times as you choose": Dance with Calamity), Backdraft ("(equal to|half) the damage dealt", "Choose a player who"), Glyph of Reincarnation ("the last time it became blocked"), Xanathar ("top card of (their|that player's|target ...) library" with play or any time), The Toymaker's Trap ("hasn't been chosen": 26 cards, mostly modal; "guesses": 5 cards), Uldaros ("of each card type": Grime Gorger), Zemo ("of the copies|from among the copies": 6 cards).
- **No proposed deferral** (above); the deferred list is round 5's, unchanged, and every row is checked against DEFERRED.md's named cards and categories.
- **Compiled-elsewhere claims checked**: every card a note says compiles (Goblin Game, Rocco, Dance with Calamity, Infernal Offering, Demonic Pact, Long List of the Ents, Liar's Pendulum, Glyph of Doom, Grime Gorger, Invoke Calamity, Verdant Mastery, Touch of the Eternal, Chaos Lord, One with the Multiverse, The Ruinous Powers, Goblin Test Pilot, Aethersnatch, Boltbender, Scheming Fence, Arcane Bombardment) has no unsupported text (`mtg-tools unsupported --card`) and no hand-written definition in src/cards/. ## Files

- `items/cards6-<slug>.md` — the briefs; `items/cards6-<slug>.texts.tsv` — each item's texts (cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note).
- `round6/baseline.tsv` — the snapshot (card, text) the plan was made from.
- `round6/deferred.tsv` — the deferred texts (cards, text, example card, category).
- `round6/drills.tsv` — the drill and review probes (id, card, probe text, OK/FAIL), for workers to start from.
- `round6/check_plan.py` — always regenerates the live list from `target/release/mtg-tools` (`unsupported --paper --by-card`) and fails on any live text not placed in exactly one list (a cards6 item list or the deferred list), on a binary older than the last commit touching crates/ (`--stale-ok` to skip), on list rows not in the snapshot or with wrong card counts or example cards, on a list without a brief, and on a work-item table above that doesn't match the lists; it reports snapshot texts supported since and which cards6 items have commits.
- Probe scripts: `round3/probe.py` and `round3/autoprobe.py` (unchanged).
