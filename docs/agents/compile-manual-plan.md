## 1. Policy and what the existing plans say

- `src/cards/mod.rs` policy: manual only for genuine one-offs; "compile first"; one block per registration, exact
  text match; `manual-check` (run by `cargo test`) fails when a block becomes compilable (**the definition must then
  be deleted**), is stale, duplicated or untested. So compiling a card = make its block parse + delete the file; the
  `m_<card>.rs` test stays as the behaviour test (manual-check only requires tests for cards still manual).
- No card is required to stay manual. The items (`cards-manual-defs`, `-2`, `cards3..6-manual-defs`) only say why
  each text is a one-off ("only this card", "unique") and never mark any as uncompilable; `docs/DEFERRED.md` has no
  manual-related entry (the deferred dexterity/Conspiracy cards are not in `src/cards/`). The one sentence that cuts
  the other way is the policy's own premise ("a construct shared by two or more cards must be compiled") — the Later
  item removes the "two or more": one-off constructs get an AST node/pattern too. **The policy text in
  `cards/mod.rs` and CLAUDE.md "Hand-written card abilities" must be rewritten** (registry becomes a staging area to
  be emptied; new one-offs still allowed temporarily with a reason, but each needs a follow-up item).
- Round 4 review already reclassified two "one-offs" as shared and compiled them (Ice Cauldron's noted mana ↔
  Jeweled Amulet; Torrent of Lava's on-stack static ↔ Kaervek's Torch) — precedent for this round.
- `docs/agents/items/MANUAL.texts.tsv` is the **round-2 candidate list, now stale**: 92 texts; 33 of its cards are
  compiled already (Altar of the Pantheon, Approach of the Second Sun, Damping Sphere, Shared Fate, Yurlok, ...) and
  103 of today's manual cards are not in it. Regenerate it from `cards::all()` (card, face, text, reason, group) as
  the round's input list, or retire it.
- Hook doc comments in `kw/mod.rs` sometimes hard-code the one-off ("Deification is the only one", `loyalty_removed_by_damage`);
  those comments go when the hook is generalised.

## 4. Hook inventory (which hooks exist, who uses them, one-off or not)

(70), `custom_effect_possible`, `custom_trigger` (9), `custom_trigger_looks_back`, `custom_value` (5), `custom_modification`
