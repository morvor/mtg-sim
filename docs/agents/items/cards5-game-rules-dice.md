# cards5-game-rules-dice: Game-rule, dice, coin, Attraction and draft grammar III: rolls whose tables come later or that each player makes, rolls as costs and as-enters replacements, coin loops and reflexive flips, durations that end on a roll, game-start actions, sideboard choices, draft abilities, effect-created triggers' durations and optional bodies — 21 texts / 21 cards (20 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 21 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 21 paper cards; 20 become fully supported when this item alone is done (Vedalken Squirrel-Whacker's roll replacement is hand-written in cards5-manual-defs). Two texts (family `effect-triggers`: Season of the Bold, Gaze of Pain) were added when cards4-triggers-durations landed during the plan's review without them; they are here because this item already adds durations to until.rs. The other 19 were in cards3-game-rules-dice, which landed after round 4 was planned, and are still unsupported after it; it left 25 texts in all: these 19, Celebr-8000 and Squirrel-Whacker's replacement (genuine one-offs: cards5-manual-defs), Danse Macabre, Caller of the Untamed and Goblin Artisans (whose failing pieces are referents: cards5-referents-statics) and Mr. House (mana provenance: round5/pending-recheck.tsv). The plan re-probed every text (round3/autoprobe.py) and drilled it (round5/drills.tsv, ids `C5-...`; review probes `C5-R-...`). The full list ships as items/cards5-game-rules-dice.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile dice grammar (a results table that follows other sentences after "Roll a d20.", table rows that define X for an earlier sentence, "each player rolls a d20" and "If one or more opponents had the highest result", "Roll a d8" as a cost, "As ~ enters, roll a six-sided die twice. Its base power becomes the first result and its base toughness becomes the second result.", "The next time target player would roll one or more dice this turn, instead they roll that many dice plus one and you choose one of those rolls to ignore", following returned cards through "then exile them again" in a table row); coins ("If the coin comes up heads, ~ enters as a N/N creature with haste.", "Flip a coin until you lose a flip or choose to stop flipping", "When you win the flip, copy that spell", "Choose a source you control and flip a coin"); Attractions ("whenever you visit it, copy the exiled card" for a chosen Attraction, "until any player rolls a N, whichever comes first", "phases out until you roll a N or less while rolling to visit your Attractions", "that hasn't been phased out with this Attraction", "Whenever you reveal a card with mana value less than the result this way"); the game start and outside the game ("you're not the starting player", beginning the game "with a luck counter on it" and exiling a card from your hand, "when each opponent casts their first spell of the game", "reveal exactly two cards you own with different names from outside the game. An opponent chooses one of them"); draft abilities ("Reveal ~ as you draft it. After you draft ~, you may add a booster pack to the draft.", "Each player passes the last card from each booster pack to a player who drafted a card named ~"). Effect-created triggers ("Until the end of your next turn, whenever you cast a spell, ..." [Season of the Bold]; an optional body "you may choose to have it deal damage ... If you do, ..." in "Until end of turn, whenever ..." [Gaze of Pain]).
