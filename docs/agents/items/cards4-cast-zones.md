# cards4-cast-zones: Casting-permission and card-flow grammar IV: cards a player exiled, permissions with new parts, attaching as cards move, as-enters actions, reveal-until and digs by other players, splits between players and zones, object phrases over several zones — 50 texts / 47 cards (47 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 50 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 47 paper cards; all 47 become fully supported when this item alone is done (no card here has a text owned by another item). Every text was in a round-3 item that has landed and is still unsupported after it (cards3-cast-zones 39, cards3-costs-mana-replacements 4, cards3-referents-values 4, cards3-history-conditions 2, cards3-combat-statics 1). Each text was re-probed (card-support-plan-4.md, "Method") and drilled to the failing words: column note says what compiles and what fails ("drilled: ..."), with the review's corrections ("review: ...") and the next failing piece where the second probe found one ("second probe ..."); 12 texts have a second piece. The full list ships as items/cards4-cast-zones.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile the card-flow texts round 3 left: permissions over "cards you exiled" and "cards they exiled with ~" (who exiled a card), permissions used once over a group ("one of the exiled cards"), once per card type, over another player's library top, with reflexive "When they do" riders; Auras and Equipment that enter or return attached to a chosen host or player (random, "another one of your opponents", "any number of Aura cards ... attached to creatures you control"); as-enters actions that aren't choices ("As ~ enters, you get an emblem", "As ~ transforms, attach it to a player", "As ~ enters, exile X creature cards from your graveyard. If you can't, ..."); reveal-until run by another player or once per object of a multi-target spell; per-player digs and splits ("Each player looks at the top five cards ... and may reveal ...", "one of those cards under your control and the rest under their control"); and object phrases the zone grammar misses ("from your graveyard or from exile" for ~ itself, "from your hand or graveyard", the top N cards of a graveyard, a three-zone target list, a three-part exile list).

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards; the drilled failing words; the engine need):
- cards a player exiled (family `exiled-by-player`): 7. e.g. "You may play lands and cast noncreature spells from among cards you exiled that have fetch counters on them ..." [Haldan, Avid Arcanist]; "Each player may play lands and cast spells from among cards they exiled with ~ this turn." [Uba Mask]; "Once each turn, you may play a card from exile with a collection counter on it if it was exiled by an ability you controlled ..." [Evelyn, the Covetous]; "its controller looks at the top card of that opponent's library and exiles it face down. They may play that card for as long as it remains exiled." [Gonti, Night Minister]; Uba Mask's and Shared Fate's draw replacements ("that player exiles that card face up instead", "exiles the top card of one of their opponents' libraries face down instead"), which feed the per-player exiled sets.
  Failing piece: "cards you exiled" / "cards they exiled with ~" / "exiled by an ability you controlled" (probe: "You may play lands and cast spells from among cards you exiled with ~." fails on a neutral card; "cards you own in exile with fetch counters" compiles).
  Engine need: exile provenance: record, for each card in exile, the player who exiled it and the ability (source, controller) that did, and keep it per linked set ("cards they exiled with ~" is one set per player, CR 607.2a); Filter qualities over it; draw replacements that exile the card that would be drawn face up (Uba Mask) or another player's top card face down (Shared Fate) into those sets.
- permissions with new parts (family `play-permissions`): 7. e.g. "you may play the top card of their library" [Xanathar, Guild Kingpin]; "For as long as you control ~, you may play one of the exiled cards" [Kotose, the Silent Spider]; "Until end of turn, for each nonland card type, you may cast a spell of that type from among the exiled cards without paying its mana cost." [Aminatou's Augury]; "they may cast a spell from among other cards exiled with ~" [Knowledge Pool]; "When they do, exile the top card of their library." [Share the Spoils]; "You may cast up to one target instant card and/or up to one target sorcery card from your graveyard each with mana value X or less" [Finale of Promise]; Talent of the Telepath's spell mastery paragraph ("you may cast up to two ... instead of one").
  Failing piece: the permission's scope or count (probes: "For as long as you control ~, you may play that card" compiles, "one of the exiled cards" fails; "Until end of turn, you may cast a spell from among the exiled cards ..." compiles, "for each nonland card type ... a spell of that type" fails).
  Engine need: a permission used up after one use over a group (Kotose), one use per card type (Aminatou), permissions over another player's library top with "look at any time" (Xanathar; the agent observation must show that card to the permission holder), a reflexive trigger on using a permission (Share the Spoils), two target groups in one cast-from-graveyard permission (Finale), a later paragraph that changes the count of the previous permission (Talent).
- attaching as cards move (family `attach-moves`): 11. e.g. "return any number of Aura cards from your graveyard to the battlefield attached to creatures you control" [Storm Herald]; "Then attach ~ to another one of your opponents chosen at random." [Maddening Hex]; "put it onto the battlefield attached to that player" [Curse of Misfortunes]; "return the other cards exiled this way to the battlefield ... attached to that creature" [Flickerform]; "you may attach to it any number of Auras on the battlefield and you may put onto the battlefield attached to it any number of Aura cards that could enchant it from your graveyard and/or hand" [Bruna, Light of Alabaster]; "return any number of Aura cards that were attached to it ... then attach any number of Equipment that were attached to it" [Cass, Hand of Vengeance]; "If ~ is still on the battlefield, put that Aura card onto the battlefield attached to it. Otherwise, ..." [Auratouched Mage]; "Whenever an Aura you control becomes attached to a nonland permanent an opponent controls with mana value less than or equal to that Aura's mana value, gain control of that permanent for as long as that Aura is attached to it." [Eriette, the Beguiler].
  Failing piece: the host choice (probes: "Return target Aura card ... attached to target creature you control" and "search ... for an Aura card that could enchant ~, put it onto the battlefield attached to ~" compile; "any number of Aura cards ... attached to creatures you control", "attach ~ to an opponent chosen at random", "attached to that player", "If ~ is still on the battlefield" fail on neutral cards).
  Engine need: putting Aura cards onto the battlefield attached to a host chosen per Aura among legal hosts (CR 303.4f; a player for Curses), random choice of a player or of a creature the Aura can enchant, last-known attachments of a creature that died (Cass: the Auras and Equipment that were attached to it), the "still on the battlefield" condition, and a trigger "an Aura you control becomes attached to [quality] permanent" with a relational mana value. Eriette's duration "for as long as that Aura is attached to it" is a "for as long as" duration (cards4-triggers-durations' family `durations`): coordinate with that item.
- as-enters actions (family `as-enters-actions`): 4. e.g. "As ~ enters, you get an emblem with ..." [Valki, God of Lies]; "As ~ transforms into ~, attach it to a player." [Curse of Leeches]; "As ~ enters, choose a creature you control it could be attached to. If you do, it enters attached to that creature." [Grifter's Blade]; "As ~ enters, exile X creature cards from your graveyard. If you can't, put ~ into its owner's graveyard instead of onto the battlefield.   Failing piece: the as-enters action (probes: the "When ~ enters" forms compile; "As ~ enters, attach it to a player" and "As ~ enters, choose a creature you control. If you do, it enters attached to that creature" fail; "As ~ enters, you gain 3 life" compiles).
  Engine need: replacement-time actions of entering (CR 614.1c, 614.12): an emblem, attaching to a chosen player or creature as it enters or transforms (CR 301.5b and 301.5e for Equipment, 303.4f for Auras), an as-enters cost-like exile with an "if you can't" alternative that changes the destination, and a counter of a chosen kind per card.
- reveal-until (family `reveal-until`): 6. e.g. "The controller of target artifact sacrifices it, then reveals cards ... until they reveal an artifact card."   Failing piece: the subject or the per-object loop (probes: "Destroy target permanent. Its controller reveals ... shares a card type with it ..." and the one-target loop compile; "The controller of target artifact sacrifices it" and the multi-target loop fail).
  Engine need: reveal-until steps run by the controller of each affected object, in a loop over a group (APNAP for several players), the filter referring to the object of the earlier step (last-known card types), a group step after the loop ("those players put the exiled cards onto the battlefield").

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 4):
- These texts are the longest tail: every one was in a round-3 item that has landed (column previous_item) and is still unsupported after it, most of them after a round-1 and a round-2 item too. The round-3 items' reports aren't in the repository and their commits name few leftovers, so the plan re-probed every text: column failing_piece is the first failing sentence (or cost, trigger, line), and column note has the drilled failing words ("drilled: ... compiles; ... fails"), the review's corrections and the second failing piece. Before starting a family, read the round-3 item's brief and its commits (`git log --grep '^<item>:'`), then re-probe (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; `docs/agents/round4/drills.tsv` has the plan's 340-odd drill probes and their results; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Where a text failed in round 3 because the engine can't do it (the engine need in each family), build the engine part first and test it with a custom card before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- In flight while round 4 was planned: cards3-followups-choices, cards3-game-rules-dice and cards3-manual-defs (round4/inflight.tsv; cards3-triggers-durations landed during planning and its leftovers are cards4-triggers-durations). Two texts wait for constructs cards3-followups-choices owns (round4/pending-recheck.tsv); a text here that depends on one says so in its note. Don't build an in-flight item's construct here: merge origin and use it once it lands, or report the text.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other in-scope paper card shares the construct: search the card data before deciding), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards4-manual-defs. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --paper --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate, proposed deferral).
