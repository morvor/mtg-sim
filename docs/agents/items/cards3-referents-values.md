# cards3-referents-values: Referent and value grammar IV: results "this way", referents to earlier objects, computed values, "instead"/"otherwise" alternatives, last-known information — 133 texts / 133 cards (132 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 133 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 133 paper cards; 132 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the other 1 also has a text owned by another item, listed under ALSO BLOCKED BY). The review added 6 texts from cards2-triggers-delayed and moved Phosphorescent Feast here from cards3-costs-mana-replacements (see ADDED IN REVIEW). After cards2-copies-tokens-counters landed, 5 of its texts that are still unsupported were added (see ADDED AFTER CARDS2-COPIES-TOKENS-COUNTERS LANDED). 120 of the texts were in an earlier card item and are still unsupported after it landed (cards2-referents-values 59, cards-conditions-referents 8, cards2-followups 8, cards-history-conditions 7, cards2-filters-conditions 6, cards-zone-moves 6, ...). 14 texts have a second failing piece found by the plan's probes (column note). The full list ships as items/cards3-referents-values.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile sentences that read what an earlier instruction of the same ability did or touched. (1) Conditions and counts on results: "If a player is dealt damage this way", "If you sacrificed a permanent this way, you may return another permanent card", "You gain 2 life if a Lesson card is milled this way" after a choice from the milled cards, "If no creature cards were revealed this way", "for each land card put into a graveyard this way", "If you put +1/+1 counters on five Dragons this way". (2) Referents: "another permanent card" (other than the sacrificed one), "the last card exiled", "the chosen creature" after bolster, "the creature you control" after a fight, "those names", "both cards", "one of those permanents at random", "the card exiled this way", counters and Equipment moved from ~ to another object. (3) Values: "the difference" (between two named quantities), "half X, rounded up", "the excess", "that excess damage", "the damage dealt", snapshots "as you cast ~" / "as this ability resolves", "total toxic value", counts of kinds among objects. (4) "instead" / "otherwise" alternatives that replace an earlier instruction (spell mastery, adamant, bargained, clash won). (5) Last-known information of objects that left: "if it had seven or more verse counters on it" (exiled as a cost), "If that Equipment was attached to a creature", "If the modified creature was sacrificed". Most of the engine exists (vars, PrevAffected / this-way records, Value arithmetic, LKI); the work is the Builder's referent tracking and recording a few more results.

[Deadly Brew]; "You gain 2 life if a Lesson card is milled this way." [Sparring Dummy]
  Failing piece: "If a player is dealt damage this way" fails on its own on any damage spell (probe: "~ deals 2 damage to any target.   Engine need: Builder referent tracking: bind the object(s) of keyword actions that choose (bolster, fight, amass), name sets of exiled/revealed cards ("those names"), the last object of a repeated instruction, per-player pairs ("those players ... those permanents"), and objects that were attached to or had counters on a source that left (last known information, CR 608.2h). Moving all counters of all kinds is shared with cards2-copies-tokens-counters (check what landed there first).
- computed values ("the difference", "half X, rounded up", "the excess", "the damage dealt", "as you cast ~", "as this ability resolves", "total toxic value", "number of planeswalker types among ...", "different mana values among ...") [Ancient Cellarspawn]; "~ deals X damage to target opponent or planeswalker and half X damage, rounded up, to you, where X is the number of Mountains you control." [Eternal Flame]; "~ deals damage equal to the excess to any target other than that permanent." [Toralf, God of Fury // Toralf's Hammer]
  Failing piece: the value phrase: "loses life equal to the difference" (difference between two quantities named earlier), "half X damage, rounded up" (probe: fails generally on a plain X spell), "damage equal to the excess" (fails generally), "where X is that excess damage", "equal to the damage dealt", "where X is the greatest power among creatures you controlled as you cast ~", "the number of abilities from among flying, first strike, ...", "total toxic value", "different names among unlocked doors".
  Engine need: Value grammar over existing Value nodes: Difference(a, b), Half(x, rounding), Excess (excess_damage.rs records it, CR 120.4a, 120.10), damage dealt by the previous instruction, snapshot values taken when the spell was cast (CastInfo) or the ability resolves (CR 608.2h), counts of keywords/types among objects. The distributing value of Yannik and Captain America ("divided as you choose among one, two, or three targets") needs the divided-damage target count rule (CR 601.2d).

(family `instead-otherwise`): 13. e.g. "Spell mastery — If there are two or more instant and/or sorcery cards in your graveyard, you may cast up to two instant and/or sorcery spells from among the revealed cards instead of one." [Once and Future]; "If the revealed land card was a Mountain, ~ deals double that damage instead." [Goblin Charbelcher]
  Failing piece: the alternative: "you may cast up to two instant and/or sorcery spells from among the revealed cards instead of one", "instead return those cards to your hand and exile ~", "~ deals double that damage instead", "Otherwise, ~ deals 1 damage to it", "If you win, you may put that creature on top of its owner's library instead".
  Engine need: followup_instead.rs rewrites "[cond], [alternative] instead" into If { cond, then: alternative, otherwise: previous }; missing: alternatives that change a count ("up to two ... instead of one"), that replace several earlier instructions at once (Once and Future), that reuse "that damage" ("double that damage"), clash results (CR 701.30) and "Otherwise" after a non-If instruction. CR 608.2c: read the whole text before following it.

RULES NOTES:
- CR 608.2c: instructions are followed in order, but later text can modify earlier text ("instead" alternatives); read the whole ability first.
- CR 608.2h: if an effect requires information from the game, it uses the current information, or last known information if the object left the zone it was expected in.
- CR 603.10a: leaves-the-battlefield abilities look back in time (Dawn Evangel: the Auras attached to the creature as it died).
- CR 120.4a, 120.10: excess damage (Toralf, Overclocked Electromancer, The Last Agni Kai in cards3-costs-mana-replacements).
- CR 701.30a: clash (Pollen Lullaby, Whirlpool Whelm: "If you win").
- CR 107.1a: when an effect divides or halves, round as the text says ("rounded up"/"rounded down"); Eternal Flame rounds up.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 3):
- These texts are the long tail: most were in a round-1 or round-2 item (column previous_item) and are still unsupported after it landed. Before starting a family, read that item's brief and its commits (`git log --grep '^<item>:'`) for the reason it stopped; re-probe the text (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text) to find the exact failing words: the failing_piece column is the sentence (or cost, trigger, mode, table row) that fails, not always the smallest failing phrase. Column note "then also: ..." is the next piece that still failed with the first one replaced by a filler (sometimes only a referent to the replaced words).
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts of cards that also have a text owned by a round-2 item still in progress (round3/inflight.tsv: cards-manual-defs-2; cards2-triggers-delayed and cards2-copies-tokens-counters landed while round 3 was planned, and their still-unsupported texts are planned in cards3 items) are listed under ALSO BLOCKED BY: do your text; the card becomes supported when both land.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other in-scope paper card shares the construct: search the card data before deciding), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for a manual-definitions item. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --paper --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate, proposed deferral).
