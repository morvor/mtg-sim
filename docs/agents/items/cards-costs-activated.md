# cards-costs-activated: Cost grammar I: activated-ability costs and activation cost modifiers — 288 texts / 292 cards (239 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 288 unsupported ability texts (288 on paper cards) blocking 292 paper cards; 239 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards-costs-activated.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Parse every cost part compositionally: "Remove a [kind] counter from [a permanent you control | another permanent you control | ~]", "Remove X ki counters from ~", "Exile the top card of your library/graveyard", "Exile two creature cards from a single graveyard", "Exile an instant or sorcery spell you control", "Tap three untapped Advisors, Artificers, and/or Monks you control", "Put a card from your hand on top of your library", "Sacrifice another creature or an artifact", "Sacrifice an Aura attached to ~", "Unattach ~", "Pay life equal to ...", "Remove three quest counters from ~ and sacrifice it", loyalty/keyword costs ("Equip—Sacrifice another nonland permanent", "Cumulative upkeep—..."); and activation cost modifiers ("This ability costs {1} less to activate for each ...", "Activated abilities of [filter] cost {N} more to activate unless they're mana abilities", "Equip abilities you activate of other Equipment cost {1} less", "X can't be greater than ...").

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards):
- cost parts: "Remove a [kind] counter from [a permanent you control / ~]": 70. e.g. "{N}, Remove a +N/+N counter from a creature you control" [Sage of Fables]; "Remove two counters from ~" [Gwen Stacy // Ghost-Spider]; "Remove four quest counters from ~ and sacrifice it" [Quest for Pure Flame]
- cost parts: exile/discard/put/tap variants ("Exile the top card of your library", "Tap three untapped Advisors", "Put a card from your hand on top"): 109. e.g. "Unattach ~" [Blinding Powder]; "{T}, Unattach ~" [Heartseeker]; "{N}, Sacrifice another creature or an artifact" [Mold Folk]
- activated-ability cost modifiers ("This ability costs {N} less to activate for each ...", "Activated abilities of ... cost {N} more"): 77. e.g. "Equip abilities you activate that target ~ cost {N} less to activate." [Dwarven Mauler]; "Activated abilities of creatures you control cost {N} less to activate." [Biomancer's Familiar]; "This ability costs {N} less to activate for each legendary creature and planeswalker you control." [Mobilized District]
- other (mixed; read the texts): 32. e.g. "N" [Jeska, Thrice Reborn]; "N" [Garruk, Cursed Huntsman]; "N" [Nahiri, the Unforgiving]

APPROACH: 1. In costs.rs, parse cost parts as VERB + object phrase (reuse phrases.rs) instead of fixed strings; add CostPart variants only where a new action is needed (remove counters from another permanent; exile from library top/graveyard; tap N untapped [filter]; put from hand on top).
2. Activation cost modifiers: a `CostModifier` with `CostTarget::Abilities(filter on source and ability kind)`, value-based amounts ("for each"), and conditions; route "This ability costs ..." sentences (currently split off as activation restrictions) to it.
3. Coordinate with cards-restrictions: `costs::split_activation_restrictions` lives in costs.rs — this item owns costs.rs; restrictions adds its phrases via a small hook.

PARSER FILES INVOLVED: oracle/costs.rs (parse_cost, parse_cost_part, counter_kind, split_activation_restrictions), patterns/r118_costs.rs, r118_untap_cost.rs, k702_keyword_costs.rs, either_cost.rs, costs_for_a_duration.rs, oracle/statics.rs (parse_cost_modifier)

FILES THIS ITEM OWNS: oracle/costs.rs; new oracle/patterns/cost_parts.rs and activation_cost_modifiers.rs; may edit r118_costs.rs, r118_untap_cost.rs, k702_keyword_costs.rs, either_cost.rs

ENGINE CAPABILITIES: CostPart may lack: remove counters from a chosen permanent you control, exile from top of library, tap N untapped permanents of a filter (exists for convoke-like?), exile a spell you control from the stack. Activation cost modifiers for other objects' abilities (CostTarget) — check casting.rs applies them.

BASELINE (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`; counts include other items' texts and digital-only cards): `remove a +1/+1 counter from a creature you control` → 8 texts / 9 cards; `remove a counter from` → 26 texts / 26 cards; `this ability costs` → 52 texts / 52 cards; `activated abilities of` → 41 texts / 43 cards; `exile the top card of your` → 64 texts / 64 cards

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS:
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (an Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- A text can contain a second failing construct owned by another item. Fix it when it is small; otherwise leave it and list it under remaining gaps with the owning item.
- One-offs. If a text's construct is genuinely unique (no other card shares it) and compiling it would be hacky, don't force it: once cards-manual-defs has landed, give it a hand-written definition in src/cards/ with a test; before that, list it as a manual candidate.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` and the baseline filters above. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
