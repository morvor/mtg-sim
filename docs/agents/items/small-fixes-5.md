# small-fixes-5: Omniscience with "flash if you pay more", Kefnet's round trip, the round-trip list

Implementation item (effort high; review at max). Quote every CR rule and ruling you rely on verbatim; check every ruling of the cards involved before calling anything provisional.

1. **Omniscience and a card's own "as though it had flash if you pay {2} more"** (mutation-2/permissions, bug 5; outside permissions.rs, in casting.rs / kw::offered_costs::extend_cast_options): with Omniscience ("You may cast spells from your hand without paying their mana costs."), the card's own flash way is never offered free (Rout, Harbinger of the Tides, Ghitu Fire and the other cards of that wording, 9 in all). CR 601.3c, 118.9d; Omniscience's ruling "You can, however, pay additional costs". Fix generally: an alternative cost (without paying its mana cost) combines with the card's own additional cost that grants flash; tests per wording.
2. **Myr Welder with two imprinted Unstable Amulets** (mutation-2/permissions, bug 4: grants identified only by source object, CR 607.1c, 607.5): first check whether gaps-residual-2 (the per-object ability-link counter, landing separately) fixed it; if not, fix it here; test either way.
3. **God-Eternal Kefnet's "That copy costs {2} less to cast." doesn't round-trip** (small-fixes-4's remaining gap): fix the renderer or the compiled form so it round-trips.
4. **roundtrip-passing.txt**: run the full round-trip check with --write and commit the list (small-fixes-4 found 9 more cards passing from other items: Amphibious Kavu, Dwarven Soldier, Hedron Blade, Lairwatch Giant, Mirror Box, Rashka the Slayer, Serra Inquisitors, Swift Silence, Thijarian Witness); report the before/after count.

## Done (implementer, Nov 20)

1. `Game::permitted_cast_options` (`casting.rs`): a free-from-hand static permission (Omniscience) is offered with each of the card's own ways that has no alternative cost, not only the plain faces: the flash-for-{2}-more way (CR 601.3c, 118.9a, 118.9d), and the same gap for fused (CR 702.102a, Wear // Tear's ruling), Quantum Reduction's "flash if it's cast using teamwork" and prototyped (CR 718.3, Blitz Automaton's ruling): the combination now runs after the keyword ways are collected, on every own way without an alternative cost except face down, as `kw::offered_costs::extend_cast_options` does. Tests `tests/cr/r601_003c_omniscience_own_flash_cost.rs` (all nine cards; Rout; Ghitu Fire's ruling; Wear // Tear fused; Quantum Reduction; Blitz Automaton).
2. gaps-residual-2 hadn't landed, so fixed here: an "until you exile another card with ~" permission records the link of the ability that gave it (`PlayTerms::given_by_link`, set in `resolve.rs`'s `WithPlayTerms`), and `permissions::given` compares source, link and player. It relies only on the links of abilities gained from different cards being distinct, which gaps-residual-2's per-object counter guarantees (today's hash can in principle collide). Register `until-another-per-ability-instance` (decided). Tests `tests/cr/r607_005_until_another_instances.rs` (Myr Welder, Mairsil).
3. Renderer (`render/play_terms.rs`): a cost reduction on casting the copy an effect created renders "That copy costs {N} less to cast" (`casts_created_copy`). God-Eternal Kefnet round-trips.
4. `docs/roundtrip-passing.txt`: 28609 -> 28619 lines (the nine cards small-fixes-4 listed, and Kefnet).

## Review (Nov 20)

- Part 1 checked against CR 118.9a, 601.2b, 601.3c, 118.9d and the rulings of every card involved: the free permission is combined only with the card's own ways that have no alternative cost, in the hand, never face down; its own alternative costs (evoke, bestow, overload, morph) never combine with it (new test, Walker of the Grove's and Kestia, the Cultivator's rulings). The same change also fixed offering (Patron of the Kitsune with Omniscience, cast at instant speed by sacrificing a Fox, CR 702.48a), and the flash way's other two wordings (Tegwyll's Scouring's "by tapping three untapped creatures you control with flying", Molten Exhale's "if you behold a Dragon"); tests added for each. The Ghitu Fire test now announces an X of 3 (it passed on an engine that let X be announced for a free cast, whose default X happened to be 0); the nine-card test no longer cites 118.9a, which it didn't exercise.
- With mana for both, the player is asked how to cast it (the card's own way paying its mana cost, listed first, or with Omniscience's alternative cost; the default is the first that could be begun, register `default-first-listed-choice`); the free way's option was labelled with a debug dump of the cost (`Cost { mana: None, parts: [] }`), now "without paying its mana cost" as an offered one is (`casting.rs`, `cast_spell`); tested with Rout and seven Plains.
- Part 2: both tests fail with the link left out of `permissions::given`; gaps-residual-2 hadn't landed. Register `until-another-per-ability-instance` stays decided (CR 607.5: "They can’t be linked to any other ability, regardless of what other abilities the object may currently have or may have had in the past."; Mairsil's 2017-08-25 ruling links the abilities gained from the card they came from: "If Mairsil gains an activated ability that's normally linked to another activated ability of the card it came from, those two abilities Mairsil gains are linked for as long as Mairsil remains on the battlefield.").
- Part 3: `casts_created_copy` no longer accepts a sequence (whose other steps could cast spells the reduction would apply to as well); Kefnet still round-trips, and no card's rendering changed.
- Part 4: 28616 cards pass (the list grew from 28606 to 28616 cards; 28619 lines with the header).
