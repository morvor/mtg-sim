# cards2-followups: Follow-up grammar: "if [player] does/doesn't/can't", per-player results, "instead" alternatives, repeat loops, punishers and reflexive triggers — 132 texts / 133 cards (128 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 132 unsupported ability texts on paper cards (116 grammar; 4 engine; 4 with a second construct owned by an in-progress round-1 item ("also needs"); 3 former one-off claims re-checked as shared constructs; 5 round-1 manual candidates re-checked as shared constructs), blocking 133 paper cards; 128 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item or wait for a round-1 item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards2-followups.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile the sentences that only make sense after an earlier instruction of the same ability and refer to what it did or who did it: follow-ups whose subject is another player ("If the player does, they create ...", "If they can't, they lose 3 life", "If the first player does"), per-player outcomes of an each-player step ("Each player who doesn't loses 3 life", "Each opponent who can't discards a card", "For each opponent who didn't discard two nonland cards this way, you draw a card"), conditional alternatives with "instead" ("If that creature has flying, it deals twice that much damage to itself instead", "If ~ was kicked, put two of those cards into your hand instead"), loops ("Then repeat this process for instant cards with mana values 3 and 4", "Then target opponent does the same", "Repeat the following process X times"), punishers paid by any player or by a non-mana action ("unless any player pays {1}", "unless they put a -1/-1 counter on a creature they control", "Any opponent may have you put that card into your graveyard. If a player does, ..."), and reflexive triggers on results ("When you do, choose one —", "When one or more cards are milled this way, ...", "you may pay {G} up to three times. When you pay this cost one or more times, choose up to that many —"). Most of the engine exists (Effect::May / PayOptional / If / RepeatProcess / AsPlayer / Reflexive, simultaneous.rs per-player frames); the work is mostly the Builder's follow-up grammar plus a few recorded results.

RULES NOTES:
- CR 118.12: "[Do something]. If [a player] [does, doesn't, or can't], [effect]" — the action is a cost paid on resolution; "does" checks whether the player chose to pay or started to pay, regardless of what events actually occurred. 118.12a: "[Do something] unless [a player does something else]" means "[A player may do something else]. If [that player doesn't], [do something]".
- CR 608.2c: instructions are followed in the order written, but later text can modify earlier text ("instead" alternatives replace the earlier instruction; read the whole ability). 609.3: an impossible instruction does as much as possible ("If you can't" is true when the action couldn't be done).
- CR 608.2e and 101.4 / 101.4b: an ability with several steps involving several players: the choices for each step are made in APNAP order (each knowing the earlier choices), then that step happens simultaneously, then the next step. Plaguecrafter ruling: "first the player whose turn it is chooses a creature or planeswalker they control, then each other player in turn order does the same, knowing the choices made before them. Then all the chosen permanents are sacrificed at the same time."
- Any-player payments (Temporal Extortion ruling): "the active player gets the option to perform the action. If that player declines, the next player in turn order gets the option. As soon as any player performs the action, the spell is countered, but the remaining players still get the option." Rhystic Scrying ruling: "Each player (starting with the current player and going in turn order) gets the option to pay". Sin Prodder ruling: opponents get the option in turn order starting with the one after you.
- Repeat loops: Torment of Hailfire rulings: "State-based actions aren't checked in between repetitions of this process"; "Each repetition through this process is done separately" (triggers see earlier repetitions); in multiplayer each opponent in turn order makes their choice once, then the actions happen simultaneously, then the process repeats.
- Reflexive triggers, CR 603.12: a reflexive triggered ability ("when [a player] [does or doesn't]", "when [something happens] this way") triggers based on events earlier in the resolution and is put on the stack the next time a player would receive priority; it chooses its targets and modes then (Tranquil Frillback ruling: "goes on the stack with no targets and no modes chosen"; Saruman of Many Colors ruling: "You don't choose a target ... at the time it triggers"). 603.12a: normally it triggers once per occurrence, but paying a cost one or more times makes it trigger only once ("choose up to that many").
- Modal: CR 700.2, 700.2d (no mode chosen twice unless allowed).
- Surveil results: CR 701.25a (any number into the graveyard, the rest on top in any order).
- Oath of Druids ruling: "The targeted player controlling more creatures than the current player is a part of the targeting requirement" (checked on resolution too).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 2):
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts marked "also needs <round-1 item>" belong to a round-1 item that is still in progress for one construct and to this item for the other. Do your construct; if that item has landed when you finish, make the whole text work, otherwise list the text as waiting for it. A text can also contain a second construct that the probes didn't reach: fix it when it is small, otherwise list it with the item that owns that area.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other card shares the construct), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards-manual-defs-2. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card, including a multiplayer test for each-player outcomes and any-player payments) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
