# cards6-copies-casting: Copies of exiled cards and casting from among them: one target group per card type, cards exiled by a cost as "those exiled cards", and caps on casting from among copies (a count, "up to three of the copies", and a total mana value, "any number of spells with total mana value N or less from among the copies") — 2 texts / 2 cards (2 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 2 unsupported ability texts on 2 paper cards (Uldaros Theorix, Baron Helmut Zemo); each is its card's only unsupported text. Uldaros was in cards5-referents-statics (`results`) and Zemo in cards5-costs-sets (`cost-sets`, ALSO BLOCKED BY cards5-referents-statics for this very cap); both items landed and left them. cards5-costs-sets built Zemo's cost (the black mana symbols among the exiled set compile now), so both cards now fail on the same construct: casting from among copies with a cap. One item, one feature.

GOAL: Compile "When ~ enters, if you cast him, exile up to one target nonland card of each card type from your graveyard. Copy those cards. You may cast any number of spells with total mana value N or less from among the copies without paying their mana costs." [Uldaros Theorix] and "Boast — Exile any number of black cards from your graveyard with fifteen or more black mana symbols among their mana costs: Copy those exiled cards. You may cast up to three of the copies without paying their mana costs." [Baron Helmut Zemo].
