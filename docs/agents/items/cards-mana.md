# cards-mana: Mana grammar: spend restrictions, mana production variants, mana-spent conditions, mana-ability modifications — 81 texts / 81 cards (72 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 81 unsupported ability texts (81 on paper cards) blocking 81 paper cards; 72 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards-mana.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Spend restrictions as filters over what the mana pays for ("Spend this mana only to cast Equipment spells or activate equip abilities", "only to cast spells with mana value 4 or greater or spells with {X} in their mana costs", "only to cast a creature spell of the chosen type or a legendary creature spell", "only to pay a disturb cost or cast an instant or sorcery spell", "only to cast Room spells and unlock doors", "Spend only colored mana on X", "Spend only mana produced by basic lands to cast ~"), production variants ("Add two mana of different colors", "Add an amount of {C} equal to X plus one", "Add one mana of ~'s last noted type", "A player of your choice adds {C}", "Add one mana of any color among legendary permanents you control"), mana-spent conditions on spells ("If at least seven red mana was spent to cast ~"), and mana-ability modifications ("All lands lose all abilities except mana abilities", "Mana of any type can be spent to activate ~'s abilities").

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards):
- spend restrictions ("Spend this mana only to cast ... or activate ...", "Spend only colored mana on X"): 33. e.g. "Spend only black mana on X." [Consume Spirit]; "Spend only red mana on X." [Crimson Hellkite]; "Spend this mana only to pay cumulative upkeep costs." [Adarkar Unicorn]
- mana production variants ("add two mana of different colors", "add an amount of {C} equal to X plus one", "one mana of ~'s last noted type"): 32. e.g. "Add one mana of that color for each different power among creatures you control." [Selvala, Eager Trailblazer]; "You add {R}." [Red Death, Shipwrecker]; "Sacrifice any number of lands, then add that much {C}." [Mana Seism]
- mana-spent conditions on the spell ("if at least seven red mana was spent"): 8. e.g. "Mana of any type can be spent to cast a spell this way." [Klaw, Master of Sound]; "Mana of any type can be spent to activate ~'s abilities." [Sharkey, Tyrant of the Shire]; "If {B}{R} was spent to cast ~, you choose the permanents for each player instead." [Mythos of Snapdax]
- mana-ability modifications ("lands lose all abilities except mana abilities", "mana of any type can be spent to activate"): 4. e.g. "Until end of turn, any time you could activate a mana ability, you may pay N life." [Yavimaya Bloomsage // Channel]; "All lands lose all abilities except mana abilities." [Blood Sun]; "~ has all activated abilities of lands your opponents control except mana abilities." [Sharkey, Tyrant of the Shire]
- other (mixed; read the texts): 4. e.g. "any other player may pay that spell's mana cost." [Ice Cave]; "Destroy target land that is snow or could produce {C}." [Break the Ice]; "each player who tapped a land for mana this turn sacrifices a land of their choice." [Desolation]

APPROACH: 1. One spend-restriction parser: a disjunction of payment targets (spell filter incl. chosen type; ability kinds: equip, disturb cost, unlock door, turn face up, cumulative upkeep; costs containing {C}) → `ManaProduction` restriction field (mana_restrictions.rs).
2. Production: amounts as Value, "different colors", "any color among [filter]", recipients ("a player of your choice", "target player adds").
3. Mana-spent conditions on the spell's own cast info (shared with cards-history-conditions for triggers on other spells).

FILES THIS ITEM OWNS: patterns/mana_*.rs, r106_mana.rs (+ new oracle/patterns/mana_grammar.rs); effects.rs p_add_mana

ENGINE CAPABILITIES: Spend restrictions keyed to ability kinds (equip, disturb, unlock) and to "costs that contain {C}" may need new restriction targets in mana.rs; "last noted type" is a noted-information value (draft.rs-like notes).

BASELINE (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`; counts include other items' texts and digital-only cards): `spend this mana only to` → 54 texts / 54 cards; `spend only` → 10 texts / 11 cards; `add two mana of different colors` → 4 texts / 4 cards; `add one mana of any color among` → 3 texts / 3 cards; `mana was spent to cast ~` → 4 texts / 4 cards

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS:
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (an Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- A text can contain a second failing construct owned by another item. Fix it when it is small; otherwise leave it and list it under remaining gaps with the owning item.
- One-offs. If a text's construct is genuinely unique (no other card shares it) and compiling it would be hacky, don't force it: once cards-manual-defs has landed, give it a hand-written definition in src/cards/ with a test; before that, list it as a manual candidate.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` and the baseline filters above. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
