# cards-graveyard-hand: Card-flow grammar II: hand, graveyard and library card actions (exile from graveyards, discard/draw variants, reveal from hand, graveyard statics) — 347 texts / 353 cards (317 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 347 unsupported ability texts (347 on paper cards) blocking 353 paper cards; 317 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards-graveyard-hand.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Object-level card actions outside the battlefield: graveyard exile with counts and sources ("exile up to two target cards from a single graveyard", "exile all cards from all graveyards", "exile X target cards from target player's graveyard"), discard variants ("discard any number of cards", "discard all the cards in your hand, then draw that many cards", "you may discard a card or pay {1}"), draw variants ("draw cards equal to ...", "draw that many cards plus one"), reveal from hand ("reveal any number of creature cards with power 4 or greater from your hand", "you may reveal a Dinosaur card from your hand"), hand/library moves ("put a card from your hand on top of your library", "put any number of cards from your hand on the bottom of your library, then draw that many cards plus one"), optional either-or costs inside effects ("you may sacrifice an artifact or discard a card. If you do, ..."), and graveyard statics ("cards in graveyards lose all abilities", "each nonland card in your graveyard has mayhem", "as long as there are ten or more cards in a single graveyard").

APPROACH: 1. Treat zone-qualified card phrases as object phrases with a zone ("target cards from a single graveyard" = targets with a same-graveyard constraint, "cards in your hand" etc.) and make discard/draw/exile/reveal/put patterns accept any count Value and any card filter.
2. "A or B" optional instructions ("sacrifice an artifact or discard a card") as a choice between two effects with "If you do" bound to whichever was performed.
3. Graveyard/hand statics: static effects whose affected set is cards in a zone (layer 6 ability grants/removal to cards in graveyards, CR 613.1f) — check that statics can apply to non-battlefield objects.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS:
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (an Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- A text can contain a second failing construct owned by another item. Fix it when it is small; otherwise leave it and list it under remaining gaps with the owning item.
- One-offs. If a text's construct is genuinely unique (no other card shares it) and compiling it would be hacky, don't force it: once cards-manual-defs has landed, give it a hand-written definition in src/cards/ with a test; before that, list it as a manual candidate.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` and the baseline filters above. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
