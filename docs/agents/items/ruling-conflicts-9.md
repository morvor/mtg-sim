# ruling-conflicts-9: "search for exactly two cards ... that have different names" when only one name can be found

Run at effort max, with an independent investigator subagent and a separate adversarial skeptic subagent, as in ruling-conflicts-1. The user's standing rule: the CR and rulings should not contradict; judgment calls go in the register before the user decides.

**Raised by:** rulings-P181 (landed 1e73d1b). The engine makes an unregistered judgment call.

**The question.**
- Burning-Rune Demon (Oracle): "When this creature enters, you may search your library for exactly two cards not named Burning-Rune Demon that have different names. If you do, reveal those cards. An opponent chooses one of them. Put the chosen card into your hand and the other into your graveyard, then shuffle."
- Today, if the library holds only one distinct qualifying name, the engine finds just that one card (CR 701.23b) and the opponent's "choice" sends it to the hand. "Exactly two" could instead mean two or none (the search can't find a single card), or that the whole "if you do" fails.
- Relevant rules to quote in full: 701.23a, 701.23b ("... that player isn't required to find some or all of those cards even if they're present in that zone"), 701.23d, 701.23g, 608.2c/608.2d (doing as much as possible), 609.3, and any rule on "exactly".
- Related wordings to survey: Extrapolate the Impossible ("You may reveal exactly two cards you own with different names from outside the game. An opponent chooses one of them. ..."), Grown from the Stump ("Place exactly two cards named Hydra Head ... If you can't, ..."), and any other "exactly N" search/reveal and "different names" searches (Gifts Ungiven, Intuition, Fact or Fiction-style piles, Mythos of Brokkos, the Kaldheim demons' cycle-mates). Collect every ruling on finding fewer cards than asked, with dates; check the Kaldheim release notes text if present in data.

**Outcome.**
- **A source decides it** (a ruling or the CR plainly): make the engine follow it generally (one rule for "exactly N" searches, not a Burning-Rune Demon special case), with tests citing cr!/ruling!, and a decided-by-source register entry.
- **Nothing decides it:** keep or change the engine to the best-supported reading, add a PROVISIONAL register entry with the alternatives and every source quoted verbatim with dates, and pin it with a test using interp!.
- Follow worker.md's one-item-per-session rule and notify the coordinator when it lands.

## STATUS

Investigated and implemented (Nov 18) by an independent investigator; the adversarial skeptic's review follows separately.

**Conclusion: no source decides the shortfall** (in ruling-conflicts-1's terms D: nothing conflicts, the CR and every ruling are silent on it; not C, because the one ruling on an "exactly N" shortfall, Spry and Mighty's, is about a choice, not a search). As this item's brief asks, the engine now follows the best-supported reading, registered **provisional** as `exactly-n-cards-are-n-or-none` (docs/interpretations/zones-objects.md): "exactly N" cards searched for or revealed are N cards or none. A searcher finds N of them or, as CR 701.23b allows for cards with a stated quality, none, never between one and N−1; if the zones searched don't hold N cards that can be found together, none are found, and a library that was searched is still shuffled (CR 118.12b, 701.23g). Burning-Rune Demon with only one other name in the library finds nothing, reveals nothing, and shuffles. Extrapolate the Impossible's reveal already worked this way, and Spry and Mighty's "Choose exactly two creatures you control" does by its ruling.

**Engine defects found and fixed.**
1. The search grammar dropped "exactly" (`search_grammar.rs`, `count`), so Burning-Rune Demon's search was "up to two": its controller could find a single card even with two or more names available, and that card went to their hand (the opponent "chose" the only card). Turtles Forever likewise could find one to three cards. No reading that gives "exactly" any meaning allows that (608.2c), so this part isn't provisional; the shortfall is.
2. Burning-Rune Demon shuffled its controller's library even when they chose not to search, against its ruling (2021-02-05): "If you choose not to search your library, no cards are revealed or put anywhere else, and you don't shuffle." The ruling counted as CITED (`tests/cards/zones_dig_search.rs` cites "If you choose not to search your library, no cards are revealed or put anywhere else" and checks that nothing moves), but its "and you don't shuffle" was untested and wrong: the trailing "then shuffle" compiled to an unconditional shuffle (the dig grammar's `rest_tail`) after the "If you do" chain. Decided by the ruling and CR 118.12; fixed for the structure, not provisional.

**Readings tried.**
- **Adopted: N or none.** "Exactly" makes the number found N; 701.23b still lets a searcher find none; a shortfall finds none. The closest source is Spry and Mighty's ruling: the same words, "exactly two", where only one creature is there, and the spell does nothing rather than as much as possible (609.3 would otherwise have the player choose the one creature, since "Choose exactly two creatures you control" is mandatory). Wizards' rulings use "exactly one" to exclude both fewer and more (the sacrifice costs); an optional action isn't done in part (Consecrated Sphinx, 701.17b, 702.52b; Egon in the same set); the instructions after the search need two cards ("An opponent chooses one of them. ... the other into your graveyard"); and the template contrasts with "up to N", whose rulings allow fewer. Forge reads it the same way.
- **(A) The engine's earlier reading:** "exactly" ignored, so the searcher could find zero, one or two cards whatever the library held. Rejected: it gives "exactly" no effect (608.2c), and lets one card be found when two can.
- **(B) As many as possible:** "exactly N" as 701.23d's "must find that many cards (or as many as possible, if the zone doesn’t contain enough cards)", so one card is found when only one name is there, and goes to the hand. For it: 609.3 and its "Discard two cards" example. Against: no ruling reads "exactly" so; Spry and Mighty's ruling reads the same words the other way; the opponent's choice the card is built around disappears. The strongest alternative, and why the entry is provisional.
- **(C) The search can't be chosen:** with fewer than N findable, "you may search" can't be taken (like 701.17b's "If given the choice to do so, they can’t choose to take that action."), so nothing is revealed and nothing is shuffled. Rejected: 701.23g and 118.12b let the player choose to search even when the later actions are impossible, and the Dominaria CR changes article says so in so many words; looking through a library is always possible, only finding N cards isn't. Under the adopted reading the difference is only the shuffle and "whenever a player searches" triggers.
- **(D) Must find N when N can be found** (no failing to find in an "exactly" search). No source; 701.23b's freedom stays for a hidden zone, and the search itself is optional. Only the choice's minimum would change.

**Engine** (commit 3fa9607).
- `ability.rs`: `SearchPart::exactly` (serialized only when true, so no other search's structure fingerprint changes).
- `oracle/patterns/search_grammar.rs` (`count`, `specs`): "exactly N" sets it instead of being dropped.
- `search_rules.rs` (`choose`): an "exactly" part finds N cards or none; when N can't be found together (with different names, or the relationship the part's filter requires, `target_groups::can_choose_together`), the searcher isn't asked and finds none, and the zones are still searched; otherwise the choice is "choose exactly N cards, or none" (0 to N, or N for a quantity), an answer of another size is invalid (the default finds N), and cards found together for a relationship are completed to N.
- `oracle/render/effects.rs`: renders "exactly N".
- `oracle/patterns/search_grammar_2.rs`: after "If you do, reveal those cards" (an optional search whose cards stay where they are), a later "then shuffle" shuffles only if the player searched (`shuffle_if_you_searched`, `Condition::Custom(YOU_SEARCHED_THIS_WAY)`). Only Burning-Rune Demon has this structure.

**For the user.** Accept the provisional entry `exactly-n-cards-are-n-or-none`: "exactly N" cards are N or none, and none when N can't be found or revealed together; a player who searched still shuffles. Recommendation: **yes**.
