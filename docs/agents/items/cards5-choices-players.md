# cards5-choices-players: Choice and player grammar VI: objects chosen by other players or as sets, each player's choices and their results, players picked by their results, loops a player repeats, type and name choices, modal headers — 43 texts / 42 cards (41 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 43 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 42 paper cards; 41 become fully supported when this item alone is done (Deification's other text is hand-written in cards5-manual-defs). Every text was in a landed item and is still unsupported after it: 36 were in cards3-followups-choices (landed after round 4 was planned); Emergent Ultimatum and Mirror of Fate (cards3-cast-zones) waited in round4/pending-recheck.tsv for it and are still unsupported; Guff Rewrites History, Rootweaver Druid and Guild Feud were left by cards4-cast-zones, and Eumidian Wastewaker by cards4-referents-statics; Zevlor, Elturel Exile was left by cards4-triggers-durations (landed during the plan's review). Most have resisted two or three items, so the plan re-probed every one (round3/autoprobe.py) and drilled it to the failing words on the real card and on neutral cards (round5/drills.tsv, ids `C5-...`; review probes `C5-R-...`). The full list ships as items/cards5-choices-players.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile choices of objects made by another player or as a set ("that opponent chooses target creature", "choose any number of other spells and/or abilities", "choose any number of other creatures that spell could target", "the other cards" left after an opponent's choice, "choose up to seven face-up exiled cards you own", distributing among "it and any number of commanders", "its controller chooses target permanent ... that shares a card type with it", "choose target opponent and up to one target creature they control", "the name of a nonland card revealed this way", the union referent "that permanent or card", "for each other opponent, choose that player or a permanent they control, copy that spell, and the copy targets the chosen player or permanent"); each player's choices and what is done with them ("Each player chooses a creature they control ... each creature chosen this way", the voter's choice, "their chosen card", "For each permanent chosen this way, its controller may exile it" (compiles since the review's merge; the chosen permanents "still on the battlefield" as a count fails), "for each land target player controls in excess of the number you control, choose ...", piles of permanents with a lasting restriction on the chosen pile, a secret number "that hasn't been chosen" and an opponent's guess); players picked by their results ("Each player who shuffled ... this way", "The player who exiled the card with the greatest mana value", "for each opponent dealt damage", "each player who owns a spell you cast this way", per-opponent optional taps with a reflexive trigger, players manifesting cards from their hands, "Those permanents' owners shuffle them", another player's dig split between controllers or chained with commas, "you and defending player each ... this way"); loops ("As many times as you choose", "then do the same for", "unless it has the same name as another card exiled this way", alternating exiles until a hand is empty); type and name choices (planeswalker types, choices made as an Equipment becomes attached, "its name and creature type are the last chosen name and creature type", a card type shared among craft materials, the optional "you may choose" as a permanent enters or is turned face up, a choice during the untap step that limits what untaps); modal headers ("a modal spell", "the number of times you chose a mode for that spell", a random mode the controller may choose instead).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 5):
- These texts are the long tail: every one was in at least one landed item (column previous_item) and is still unsupported after it, most after two or three. The plan re-probed each (column failing_piece: the first failing sentence, cost, trigger or line) and drilled it (column note: "drilled: X compiles; Y fails", with the drill ids of round5/drills.tsv, and the second failing piece where there is one). Re-probe before starting a family (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Where a text fails because the engine can't do it (the engine need in each family), build the engine part first and test it with a custom card before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
