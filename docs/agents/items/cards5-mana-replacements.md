# cards5-mana-replacements: Mana, replacement and prevention grammar: mana spent of given colors and from given sources on one payment, optional extra mana chosen by a land's controller, mana-symbol counts and a card's colors as mana, draw replacements by cause, with a revealed card, an offer to other players or a target, an opponent's land-entering action, prevention-result triggers over groups of sources, and damage-history replacements — 10 texts / 10 cards (10 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 10 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 10 paper cards; all 10 become fully supported when this item alone is done. Nine were left by cards4-costs-mana-replacements (landed at 15183001; its families `mana-production`, `cost-sets`, `mana-symbols`, `draw-replacements`, `prevention` and `other-replacements`); Mr. House, President and CEO waited in round5/pending-recheck.tsv for that item's mana provenance and still failed after it; it has compiled since cards5-game-rules-dice landed (22d3ca41, a dice-only pattern in dice_coins_5.rs over `history_conditions::spent_value`), so 9 texts are open here (see STATUS). The re-plan ("Update: cards4-paper-effects and cards4-costs-mana-replacements landed" in card-support-plan-5.md) re-probed every text (round3/autoprobe.py) and drilled it to the failing words on the real card and on neutral cards (round5/drills.tsv, ids `C5-W-...`). The full list ships as items/cards5-mana-replacements.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile mana grammar ("its controller may add an additional {U}" in a land's mana trigger and "If that Island is snow, ... {U}{U} instead", "for each {B} or {R} spent this way" for a cumulative upkeep payment and mana of given colors spent generally, "for each mana from Treasures spent to activate this ability" and "a six-sided die plus an additional six-sided die for each ...", "if it has three or more colored mana symbols in its mana cost", "add three mana in any combination of its colors", "If you don't reveal it"); replacements ("If an opponent who controls at least as many lands as you do would put a land onto the battlefield, that player instead puts that land onto the battlefield then sacrifices a land of their choice", "The next time you would draw a card this turn, ~ deals N damage to any target instead", "they reveal it instead. Then any other player may pay N life. If a player does, ... Otherwise, ...", "If a cycling ability of another nonland card would cause you to draw a card, instead ... a card that shares a card type with the cycled card"); and prevention and damage results ("Whenever damage from a creature is prevented this way, ... to that creature" for a shield over "sources your opponents control", "the creature dealt damage can't be regenerated this turn", "If a creature dealt damage by the targeted creature would die this turn, exile that creature instead").

  Failing piece: drills: "its controller adds an additional {U}" compiles and "you may add" compiles for your own lands, the optional addition by the tapped land's controller fails, and so does the snow "instead" amount; the cumulative upkeep trigger compiles, counts of mana of given colors spent fail for every payment (Bladecoil Serpent's "{U}{U} spent to cast it" and Spoils of the Hunt's "mana from a Treasure that was spent to cast this spell" compile); the Treasure condition on an activation compiles, the count fails, and a die count "plus an additional die for each ..." or "a die for each ..." fails ("Roll X dice, where X is ..." compiles); a colored-mana-symbol count fails as a condition (Charmed Pendant's count compiles as a value), "any combination of its colors" fails, "If you don't reveal it" fails where "If you don't" compiles.
  Engine need: the choice in a mana trigger made by the tapped land's controller (CR 106.12a, 605.1b), the mana spent on one payment by color and by source kept on that payment (cumulative upkeep, CR 702.24a; an activation, CR 602.2b, 601.2h), a dice count as a Value (CR 706.2), mana of any combination of a card's colors (CR 106.1, 105.2).
- replacements (family `replacements`): 4. e.g. "If an opponent who controls at least as many lands as you do would put a land onto the battlefield, that player instead puts that land onto the battlefield then sacrifices a land of their choice." [Land Equilibrium]; "{N}: The next time you would draw a card this turn, ~ deals N damage to any target instead." [Words of War]; "If a player would draw a card, they reveal it instead. Then any other player may pay N life. If a player does, put that card into its owner's graveyard. Otherwise, that player draws a card." [Zur's Weirding]; "If a cycling ability of another nonland card would cause you to draw a card, instead exile cards from the top of your library until you exile a card that shares a card type with the cycled card. ..." [Unpredictable Cyclone]
  Failing piece: drills: a replacement of an opponent putting a land onto the battlefield fails with any result (the static "Lands your opponents control enter tapped" and a trigger form compile); untargeted next-time draw replacements compile, a targeted result fails; "they reveal the top card of their library instead" compiles, "they reveal it instead" fails, and "any other player may pay" and "Otherwise" after "If a player does" fail (Dash Hopes' "Any player may pay 5 life. If a player does" compiles); the body under "If you would draw a card" compiles, the cause "a cycling ability [of another nonland card]" fails (Rain of Gore's "If a spell or ability would cause its controller to gain life" compiles), and "the cycled card" fails as a referent.
  Engine need: a replacement keyed on which player puts a land onto the battlefield, however it happens (rulings: played or put by an effect, under any player's control; the same land may be sacrificed; with the land's own enters replacement its controller orders them, CR 616.1), the draw's cause and the cycled card kept on the draw event (CR 702.29, 121), an offer to each other player in turn order (CR 101.4) inside a draw replacement, a target chosen when a next-time replacement's ability is activated (CR 115.1c, 602.2b). Add an interpretation entry (docs/interpretations/, `interp!` in the Words of War test) for the reading: no rule or ruling says when Words of War's target is chosen; CR 115.1c makes "any target" in an activated ability a target chosen on activation, and Mirrorwood Treefolk's ruling ("If the target creature is not on the battlefield ... at the time the damage would be redirected, then the damage goes on this card") is the precedent for a target chosen on activation and checked when the next-time effect applies. The entry must also settle what happens if the target is illegal when the draw is replaced (the draw is still replaced and no damage is dealt, or the replacement doesn't apply); Words of War's own ruling ("If multiple Words have been used prior to drawing a card, then you can choose which one to apply", CR 616.1) can be tested with `ruling!`.
- prevention and damage results (family `prevention-damage`): 2. e.g. "Prevent all damage that would be dealt to you this turn by sources your opponents control. [Judgment of Alexander]; "If the creature deals damage to a creature this turn, the creature dealt damage can't be regenerated this turn. If a creature dealt damage by the targeted creature would die this turn, exile that creature instead." [Runesword]
  Failing piece: drills: the shield compiles and so does "Each commander creature you control deals damage equal to its power to target creature"; "Whenever damage [from a creature] is prevented this way" fails after a shield over a group of sources with any body (Samite Ministration's form after a single-source shield compiles); "Whenever that creature deals damage to a creature this turn, that creature can't be regenerated this turn" compiles but Runesword's "If ..., the creature dealt damage ..." wording fails, and "dealt damage by the targeted creature would die this turn, exile ... instead" fails (Torch the Tower's "dealt damage by ~" compiles).
  Engine need: prevention-result triggers for every source of a group shield (CR 615.5, 603.7) with the source as "that creature"; damage history keyed on a chosen creature as the source for the turn, read by a regeneration ban (CR 701.19) and a dies-replacement (CR 614.1a).
