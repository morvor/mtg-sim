# cards6-per-player-results: Per-player results and choices VII: each player's own result of a per-player step ("the card they exiled", "one of those cards ... and the rest"), players picked by a comparison of their results with a tie loop, players filtered by what they controlled, a number choice remembered per object and guessed by an opponent, and an optional repeat with a cost per iteration and its last iteration as a referent — 5 texts / 5 cards (5 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 5 unsupported ability texts on 5 paper cards (Guff Rewrites History, Rootweaver Druid, Timesifter, The Toymaker's Trap, Lim-Dûl's Vault); each is its card's only unsupported text. All five were in cards5-choices-players (landed; families `per-player-results`, `each-player-choices`, `repeat-loops`), and three or four rounds before it. What they share: a step done by each player (or each iteration) whose result the next sentence reads per player. The engine has each piece for one wording (Rocco, Street Chef's "each player may play the card they exiled this way"; Psychic Battle's and Goblin Game's "the player who reveal(s/ed) ..."; Verdant Mastery's split of found cards; Dance with Calamity's "As many times as you choose"; the modal "choose one that hasn't been chosen"); the grammar takes only those wordings.

GOAL: Compile "For each player, choose target nonenchantment, nonland permanent that player controls. Those permanents' owners shuffle them into their libraries. Each player who controlled one of those permanents exiles cards from the top of their library until they exile a nonland card, then puts the rest on the bottom of their library in a random order. Each player may cast the nonland card they exiled without paying its mana cost." [Guff Rewrites History]; "When ~ enters, each opponent may search their library for up to three basic land cards. They each put one of those cards onto the battlefield tapped under your control and the rest onto the battlefield tapped under their control. Then each player who searched their library this way shuffles." [Rootweaver Druid]; "At the beginning of each upkeep, each player exiles the top card of their library. The player who exiled the card with the greatest mana value takes an extra turn after this one. If two or more players' cards are tied for greatest, the tied players repeat this process until the tie is broken." [Timesifter]; "At the beginning of your upkeep, secretly choose a number between N and N that hasn't been chosen. If you do, an opponent guesses which number you chose, then you reveal the number you chose. If they guessed wrong, they lose life equal to the number they guessed and you draw a card. If they guessed right, sacrifice ~." [The Toymaker's Trap]; "Look at the top five cards of your library. As many times as you choose, you may pay N life, put those cards on the bottom of your library in any order, then look at the top five cards of your library. Then shuffle and put the last cards you looked at this way on top in any order." [Lim-Dûl's Vault].

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards; the failing piece; the engine need):
- per-player results (family `per-player-results`): 3. [Guff Rewrites History], [Rootweaver Druid], [Timesifter]
  Failing piece: Guff: the possessive subject "Those permanents' owners" (where "The owners of those permanents" compiles), then the filter "Each player who controlled one of those permanents" (Infernal Offering's "Each player who sacrificed a creature this way" compiles), then "Each player may cast the nonland card they exiled without paying its mana cost" (Rocco's form compiles only verbatim). Rootweaver: a split of each searcher's found cards between two destinations (all under one controller compiles; the controller's own split compiles). Timesifter: "The player who exiled the card with the greatest mana value" with any body other than Psychic Battle's, then the tie loop among the tied players.
  Engine need: each player's result of a per-player step kept per player (the exiled card, the found set) and read by the next sentence; the last-known controllers of a set an effect moved, as a player filter (last known information, CR 608.2h); a split chosen by each searching player in APNAP order (CR 101.4; ruling: the next opponent in turn order searches and puts first; one card found goes under your control); a comparison over the players' results selecting a player, repeated among tied players until the tie breaks (rulings: only tied players continue; a player who exiles no card can't take the turn; with two Timesifters two extra turns; most recently created extra turn first, CR 500.7). The tie loop is unique to Timesifter; build it as the tie rule of the shared comparison, and if it can't be, report it as a manual candidate.
- each player's choices (family `each-player-choices`): 1. [The Toymaker's Trap]
  Failing piece: "secretly choose a number between 1 and 5" compiles; "that hasn't been chosen" fails; then "an opponent guesses which number you chose, then you reveal the number you chose" (guesses of yes/no questions compile: Liar's Pendulum, Master of Predicaments, Gollum, Scheming Guide, The Seventh Doctor), and "they lose life equal to the number they guessed".
  Engine need: the numbers chosen kept on the object (ruling: a new object or a second copy has no memory; like the modal "hasn't been chosen" history, modal_history.rs), an opponent's guess of a number as a decision routed to that opponent (choices.rs), the guess as a value. If all five numbers have been chosen, "If you do" fails (nothing chosen): record the reading.
- repeat loops (family `repeat-loops`): 1. [Lim-Dûl's Vault]
  Failing piece: Dance with Calamity's "As many times as you choose, you may exile the top card of your library" compiles; with a payment per iteration ("you may pay 1 life, ...") or Lim-Dûl's multi-step body it fails; "Then shuffle and put the last cards you looked at this way on top in any order" fails.
  Engine need: an optional repeat whose iteration starts with a cost (CR 118, 119.4: paying life) and whose body is several steps over "those cards"; the last iteration's looked-at set as the referent after the loop; shuffling the library without that set, then putting it on top in any order (rulings: with no life paid, shuffle the rest and put the five back; with five or fewer cards you see the same cards).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 6):
- These texts are the last of the long tail: every one was in at least one landed item (column previous_item; most in two to five) and is still unsupported after it. The plan re-probed each on Nov 4 (column failing_piece: the first failing sentence) and drilled it (column note: "drilled: X compiles; Y fails", with the drill ids of round6/drills.tsv, `C6-...`, and the second and third failing pieces). Re-probe before starting (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; write the card's own name as ~ in probe texts; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Each item is one engine feature or one grammar area, not a family of wordings: build the engine part first and test it with a custom card (`CardDef::custom`) before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several wordings, including the drills' neutral-card forms.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). Add TestGame tests with the real card asserting the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for the card's Scryfall rulings exercised: these cards' rulings are all UNCOVERED today). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). The plan searched every one-off-looking construct over all in-scope paper cards (paper and legal or restricted in a format; the searches are in each note); the genuine one-offs are in cards6-manual-defs. If a text here turns out to need a construct no other card has and the engine can't express it as grammar, don't force a pattern: list it in your report as a manual candidate. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) may be edited by other items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files (`oracle/patterns/<topic>_6.rs`), and merge origin often.
