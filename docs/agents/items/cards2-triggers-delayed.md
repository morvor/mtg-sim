# cards2-triggers-delayed: Trigger grammar III and durations: delayed triggers from resolving abilities, durations bound to other players and objects, event causes, trigger limits — 175 texts / 173 cards (159 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 175 unsupported ability texts on paper cards (95 grammar; 58 engine; 6 with a second construct owned by an in-progress round-1 item ("also needs"); 16 former one-off claims re-checked as shared constructs), blocking 173 paper cards; 159 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item or wait for a round-1 item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards2-triggers-delayed.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Three related areas of the trigger and duration machinery. (1) Durations: "during target opponent's next turn" and "during its controller's next combat phase" (start later, last one turn), "until that player's next end step", "for as long as that creature remains tapped / is enchanted / they're the monarch / ~ remains attached to it", and one-shot continuous effects with those durations (hands revealed, abilities can't be activated, mana kept until end of combat, X additional land plays). (2) Delayed triggers created by resolving spells and abilities: "Whenever that creature deals combat damage to a player this combat", "When you next cast a spell this turn", "Return ~ ... at the beginning of their next upkeep", "at the beginning of the next end step, destroy that creature if it attacked this turn", "when that token dies", and pay-to-stop delayed triggers (Glass Asp). (3) Trigger conditions that need more information on events (who tapped or exiled it, attack-declaration taps, the spell or ability that caused a discard, shuffle, zone change or life gain, echo and cumulative upkeep payments, fights, untap-step untaps, mana of the chosen color, phasing, each counter removed), trigger-condition grammar (event disjunctions, combined "enters or dies" bodies, linked-object subjects, every player of a batch), and trigger limits and trigger statics ("This ability triggers only once", "only twice each turn", additional triggers, torpor variants).

RULES NOTES:
- CR 603.7: delayed triggered abilities are created during resolution (603.7a) and don't trigger before they exist; 603.7b: one trigger only (the next time its event occurs) unless it has a stated duration such as "this turn"; 603.7c: a delayed trigger that refers to a particular object affects it even if its characteristics change, but not if it left the zone it's expected in (a returned card is a new object); 603.7d/e: source and controller of a delayed trigger. Berserk ruling: "If the target permanent stops being a creature before the next end step, it is still destroyed when the delayed trigger resolves."
- CR 611.2a/b/c: a resolving effect's duration; "for as long as" durations that never start do nothing; effects that modify characteristics or change control lock in their affected set when they begin. CR 610.3: "until [event]" exiles return the object when the event happens (610.3a: not exiled at all if the event already happened).
- Next-turn durations: Gideon Jura rulings: the requirement "applies during each combat phase of the affected player's next turn (as opposed to applying during the affected player's next combat phase)", and "doesn't lock in what it applies to" (it applies to whatever creatures that player controls then); a creature that can't attack him may attack another player or planeswalker. CR 614.10 / 614.10a: skips are replacement effects; anything scheduled for a skipped step waits for the next occurrence that isn't skipped.
- Pay-to-stop: CR 116.2c (a special action that stops a delayed triggered ability). Glass Asp ruling: the affected player may pay {2} as a special action any time they have priority, once, until their next draw step begins.
- Trigger events: CR 603.2c (one trigger per occurrence; Protean Hydra ruling: "If multiple +1/+1 counters are removed from Protean Hydra at once, its last ability will trigger that many times"), 603.2e ("becomes tapped" needs an actual change; Verity Circle rulings: a creature entering tapped doesn't trigger it; a vigilant attacker that later becomes tapped does), 508.1f (attackers become tapped by being declared, not as a cost), 701.26a (tap), 701.14a (fight), 502.3 (untap step), 106.12a (tapped for mana of a type), 119.4 (paying life is losing life), 702.24a (cumulative upkeep), 702.30a (echo), 702.26a/b (phasing), 603.10a (look-back triggers).
- Trigger limits: Acrobatic Cheerleader rulings: once its ability triggers it won't trigger again while it remains on the battlefield, even if the ability was countered; if it leaves and returns it's a new object and can trigger again. Nadu, Winged Wisdom rulings: the granted ability triggers twice each turn for each creature, and a new Nadu grants a different ability with its own count. Torpor Orb rulings: replacement effects and "as [this] enters" abilities are unaffected; a creature entering at the same time as Torpor Orb doesn't cause triggers. CR 603.2d (additional triggers), 603.2h ("Do this only once each turn").

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 2):
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts marked "also needs <round-1 item>" belong to a round-1 item that is still in progress for one construct and to this item for the other. Do your construct; if that item has landed when you finish, make the whole text work, otherwise list the text as waiting for it. A text can also contain a second construct that the probes didn't reach: fix it when it is small, otherwise list it with the item that owns that area.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other card shares the construct), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards-manual-defs-2. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (a TestGame test per family with a real card, including a two-turn test for each next-turn duration and an additional-combat-phase test for "this combat" windows) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
