# ruling-research-2: "that opponent" of a "one or more creatures an opponent controls" trigger after the creatures change control

Research item, effort max, with an independent investigator subagent and a separate adversarial skeptic subagent (as in ruling-conflicts-1). The user (Nov 19), on the provisional entry `that-opponent-fixed-as-it-triggers` (docs/interpretations/triggers-durations.md): "We should research this more deeply, tell the agent who researches it to search the web and examine the CR comprehensively to try to figure this out. I am really not sure here".

**The question.** Norn's Decree: "Whenever one or more creatures an opponent controls deal combat damage to you, that opponent gets a poison counter." Coveted Jewel: "Whenever one or more creatures an opponent controls attack you and aren't blocked, that player draws three cards and gains control of this artifact. Untap it." Before the ability resolves, the creatures change control (the Jewel's controller gains control of an unblocked attacker in response, or the creature leaves the battlefield). Is "that opponent"/"that player" fixed as the ability triggers (the engine's provisional reading) or the creatures' controller as it resolves (CR 608.2h's current information)? Contrast the user's Nov 5 decision for Mutiny (`target-player-reference-rechecked`, stack-casting-costs.md: a target restriction's "that player" is rechecked on resolution, via 608.2b) and say whether that decision bears on this one.

## Result (Nov 20)

Full report, every source quoted with its date and URL: `docs/agents/reports/ruling-research-2.md`.

- **No source decides it.** No CR rule or example, no ruling in the data, no release note that could be read and no web source addresses "that player" / "that opponent" of a "[creatures] an opponent controls" trigger after the creatures change control. **F (fixed as it triggers) is better supported**: the investigator about 70%, the adversarial skeptic about 60–65% (verdict UPHELD). The entry `that-opponent-fixed-as-it-triggers` stays **provisional**, rewritten with the sources.
- **The rulings split by wording**, and that is now the engine's rule everywhere: a player-referring "that player" / "that opponent" / "they" whose antecedent is the trigger condition's "an opponent" is fixed as the ability triggers (closest support: the monarch draw ruling, "If the triggered ability that causes the monarch to draw a card goes on the stack, and a different player becomes the monarch before that ability resolves, the first player will still draw the card.", and Emberwilde Captain's); an object-based "its controller" / "that creature's controller" / "that source's controller" is the object's controller as the ability resolves, or as it last existed (CR 608.2h; Tainted Aether, Ankh of Mishra, Celestial Mantle, Relic Putrescence, and Archfiend of Spite's "The player who loses life or permanents is the player who controls the source as Archfiend of Spite's triggered ability resolves. In some rare cases, this may not be the same player who controlled the source as it dealt damage. ..." even though its condition is "a source an opponent controls").
- **For R**, besides 608.2h: WotC paraphrases ward's "that player" as "The controller of the spell or ability" (Bronze Guardian, 2021-04-16); CR 726.2 words the initiative's steal "the controller of those creatures" (whose condition names two players, so "that player" would be ambiguous there; the release notes say "the first player"); and the CR moved the defending-player rule from "at the time it became an attacking creature" (508.4, CR 2017-07-07) to current information with a last-known fallback (508.5, CR 2017-08-25).
- **The web**: searches for Coveted Jewel with the attacker stolen in response, Norn's Decree, ward with Commandeer or Aethersnatch, Michiko Konda, the monarch and initiative triggers and general "that player locked in" questions found only card pages, the official rulings, Cranial Insertion articles 2760/2763 on Coveted Jewel in team games (not openable), a Magic Judges forum topic (49911, "Coveted Jewel and creatures not declared attacking", blocked) and Cardmarket's "#CMAskTheJudge Episode 66: A Real Jewel" (blocked): nothing on a control change. Blocked: magic.wizards.com and media.wizards.com (so the ONC release notes for Norn's Decree couldn't be read), apps.magicjudges.org, www.cardmarket.com, and reddit.com through the search tool.
- **Engine consistency (the skeptic's main finding)**: the construction was read both ways. Now F also for Blood Seeker, Suture Priest, Gideon the Oathless, Polluted Bonds, Nightshade Harvester, Sire of Stagnation, Invader Parasite, Archaeomancer's Map, Confounding Conundrum, Tunnel Ignus, Charismatic Conqueror, Michiko Konda and (added by the review) Trespasser's Curse's "a creature enchanted player controls" (all "that player" / "they" wordings; none has a ruling on a control change); and R, by their object-based wording, for the becomes-the-target triggers' "that spell's controller" / "counter that spell unless its controller pays" (Frost Titan, Bonecrusher Giant, Lava Runner, Retromancer, Forsaken Wastes, Fractured Loyalty, Reality Smasher, Diffusion Sliver, Unsettled Mariner, Amulet of Safekeeping, Valkmira) and the monarch's "its controller becomes the monarch" (CR 725.2). The initiative keeps the player fixed (its card says "that player"), as an open question, now pinned by a test.
- **Questions for the user**: (1) F or R (provisional); (2) was the Mutiny rationale (`target-player-reference-rechecked`: "nothing in the rules locks the referent in when targets are chosen") meant generally? Taken generally it points to R; its operative basis (608.2b re-checks targets) doesn't reach trigger conditions; (3) the initiative: fixed (the card's "that player") or CR 726.2's "the controller of those creatures" on resolution; (4) confirm the two changes to R above.
