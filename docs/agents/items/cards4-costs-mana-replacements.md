# cards4-costs-mana-replacements: Cost, mana and replacement grammar V: mana provenance beyond Treasures, mana production and its replacements, mana symbols as values, additional costs and set-valued costs, energy paid, draw replacements, prevention shields — 55 texts / 53 cards (53 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 55 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 53 paper cards; all 53 become fully supported when this item alone is done. Every text was in a round-3 item that has landed and is still unsupported after it (cards3-costs-mana-replacements 52, cards3-cast-zones 3). Each text was re-probed and drilled to the failing words (column note: "drilled: ...", "review: ...", "second probe ..."); 11 texts have a second piece. The full list ships as items/cards4-costs-mana-replacements.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile the cost, mana and replacement texts round 3 left: conditions and spend restrictions on where mana came from ("if mana from an artifact was spent to cast it", "that mana from a Treasure was spent to cast", "if {S} of any of that spell's colors was spent", "cast it or activate it", "Spend only mana produced by Treasures", "shares a color with the mana spent on this activation cost"); mana production with new parts (mana triggers on a land's ability or on mana of a color, optional additional mana, "three mana of that type", the color of the mana just added, colors among the exiled cards used to craft it, Terra's comma list); mana symbols as values ("For each colored mana symbol in the milled card's mana cost"); replacements of how mana is produced or spent ("lands tapped for mana produce mana of the chosen color", "spells and abilities you control that would add colored mana instead add that much white mana", "For one spell this turn, you may spend mana as though it were mana of any type"); additional costs that choose or sacrifice sets ("choose a creature you control or a warped creature card you own in exile", "sacrifice one or more creatures", "choose a creature type and behold two creatures of that type", "collect evidence X, where X is ..."); costs over sets with aggregate qualities ("with four or more card types among them", "with total mana value X", "with fifteen or more black mana symbols among their mana costs", "total power 12 or greater" in an unless cost); energy paid this way; draw replacements with several steps, a cause, a target or a reveal; prevention shields with relational source choices, X amounts over several recipients, prevention-result triggers and "Only you may activate this ability".

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 4):
- These texts are the longest tail: every one was in a round-3 item that has landed (column previous_item) and is still unsupported after it, most of them after a round-1 and a round-2 item too. The round-3 items' reports aren't in the repository and their commits name few leftovers, so the plan re-probed every text: column failing_piece is the first failing sentence (or cost, trigger, line), and column note has the drilled failing words ("drilled: ... compiles; ... fails"), the review's corrections and the second failing piece. Before starting a family, read the round-3 item's brief and its commits (`git log --grep '^<item>:'`), then re-probe (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; `docs/agents/round4/drills.tsv` has the plan's 330-odd drill probes and their results; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Where a text failed in round 3 because the engine can't do it (the engine need in each family), build the engine part first and test it with a custom card before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- In flight while round 4 was planned: cards3-followups-choices, cards3-game-rules-dice, cards3-triggers-durations and cards3-manual-defs (round4/inflight.tsv). Five texts wait for constructs those items own (round4/pending-recheck.tsv); a text here that depends on one says so in its note. Don't build an in-flight item's construct here: merge origin and use it once it lands, or report the text.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other in-scope paper card shares the construct: search the card data before deciding), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards4-manual-defs. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --paper --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate, proposed deferral).
