# cards-cast-permissions: Casting permissions: play/cast from zones, impulse durations, "from among cards exiled with ~", flash grants, mana flexibility — 350 texts / 352 cards (266 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 350 unsupported ability texts (350 on paper cards) blocking 352 paper cards; 266 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards-cast-permissions.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile permission statements compositionally: WHO may cast/play WHAT (spell/card filter, "one of those cards", "spells from among cards exiled with ~", "a spell with mana value less than or equal to ...") FROM WHERE (graveyard, exile, top of library, outside the game) FOR HOW LONG (this turn, until the end of your next turn, until your next end step, for as long as it remains exiled, for as long as you control ~, until you exile another card with ~) HOW OFTEN (once during each of your turns, "you can't cast more than one spell this way each turn") WITH WHICH COST (without paying its mana cost, by paying N life and discarding a card in addition, by foraging, "mana of any type can be spent", "spend mana as though it were mana of any color", as though it had flash) and WHICH SPELLS ("the first creature spell you cast each turn").

APPROACH: 1. One permission parser producing the existing `PlayPermission`/`GrantPlayPermission` with fields for filter, zone, duration, frequency limit, cost modification and mana-type flexibility; route existing cast_* patterns through it where they're subsets.
2. Durations reuse `effects::duration_suffix` (extend it: "until the end of your next turn", "until your next end step", "for as long as it remains exiled", "until you exile another card with ~").
3. "cast ~ from your graveyard by [cost] in addition to paying its other costs" = alternative zone permission with additional cost (CR 601.2b, f).

ENGINE CAPABILITIES: Check PlayPermission supports: frequency limits per turn, permissions tied to a linked exile set, "until you exile another card with ~", spending mana as though any color/type, permissions for other players ("the exiled card's owner may cast"), and outside-the-game sources (shared with cards-game-rules). Add generic fields, not card-specific ones.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS:
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (an Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- A text can contain a second failing construct owned by another item. Fix it when it is small; otherwise leave it and list it under remaining gaps with the owning item.
- One-offs. If a text's construct is genuinely unique (no other card shares it) and compiling it would be hacky, don't force it: once cards-manual-defs has landed, give it a hand-written definition in src/cards/ with a test; before that, list it as a manual candidate.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` and the baseline filters above. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
