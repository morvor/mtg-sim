# cards5-manual-defs: Hand-written card definitions V: one-off abilities with no shared construct — 5 texts / 5 cards (2 unlocked alone; 7 texts when planned, 2 done)

SCOPE (since cards4-paper-effects and cards4-costs-mana-replacements landed): 5 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 5 paper cards; 2 become fully supported when this item alone is done (Lodestone Bauble, North Star, added by the re-plan "Update: cards4-paper-effects and cards4-costs-mana-replacements landed" in card-support-plan-5.md: the two items proposed them as one-offs and the re-plan's searches confirm it). Celebr-8000 and Curse of Leeches, planned here first, are done (STATUS) and have left the list. The other three cards also have a compiled text in another cards5 item (River Song: cards5-referents-statics; Deification: cards5-choices-players; Vedalken Squirrel-Whacker: cards5-game-rules-dice). Two texts were planned as one-offs before and left (River Song's and Deification's, in cards3-manual-defs, which landed without them: round 4's review found them in round4/inflight.tsv with no live owner); three were left by cards3-game-rules-dice and cards4-cast-zones and are one-offs by the searches below. The plan's one-off check searched all in-scope paper cards' Oracle text (paper, legal or restricted in some format) for each construct, not the words, and the review repeated it (card-support-plan-5.md, "Adversarial review"); every other leftover text has a construct that another in-scope card shares, so it is in a compiled item. The full list ships as items/cards5-manual-defs.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Write hand-written definitions (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, a test per card in tests/cards/m_<card>.rs, `mtg-tools manual-check`) for the texts below (five planned first, two added by the re-plan). Rules the AST can't express go in a `KeywordRules` impl with `kinds() -> &[]` using the registry-wide hooks, finding their sources by the custom ability, never by name (CLAUDE.md). Each card's shared parts must come from compiled grammar (named per entry); hand-write only the one-off part.

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; the construct and why it is a one-off; the engine need):
- one-off constructs (family `one-off`): 5 when planned, 7 with the re-plan's two (Celebr-8000 and Curse of Leeches done).
  - River Song: "Meet in Reverse — You draw cards from the bottom of your library rather than the top." — changes which card each of its controller's draws takes (CR 121.1, 121.2). Search "draw.{0,30}bottom of", "from the bottom of your library rather": only River Song (Lantern of Undersight has the same rule but isn't legal in any format). Probes: fails on a neutral creature and with a duration (C5-rs-4, C5-R-oo-1). Engine need: a draw hook in draw_rules.rs that takes the bottom card for a player while the static applies; it is still a draw, so draw replacements and draw triggers apply as usual (ruling: "Drawing cards from the bottom of your library is exactly the same as drawing from the top, except that you take the bottom card"; it applies to every card you draw, not just the turn's draw). Do it after cards5-referents-statics compiles River Song's other text, or test this one alone with a custom card.
  - Deification: "As long as you control a creature, if damage dealt to a planeswalker you control of the chosen type would result in all loyalty counters on it being removed, instead all but one of those counters are removed." — a replacement of the result of damage to a planeswalker (CR 120.3c, 306.8, 614.1). Search "loyalty counters? on it being removed", "all but one": only Deification ("all but one" elsewhere is a selection, Aladdin's Lamp, Lily Bowen). Engine need: the loyalty-counter removal from damage (actions.rs) goes through replace(), and the hand-written replacement caps it at the counters minus one; only removal caused by damage is affected, and the damage is still dealt with all its other results (ruling: "Deification's last ability doesn't prevent damage"). Its "As ~ enters, choose a planeswalker type." is compiled in cards5-choices-players (`type-choices`), so "of the chosen type" is compiled grammar: do this after that family.
  - Vedalken Squirrel-Whacker: "If you would roll one or more six-sided dice, instead roll them and you may exchange one result with ~'s base power or base toughness." — exchanging a die result with a base P/T (C5-vsw-3 fails). Search "result with", "exchange one result": only this card; the head "If you would roll ... instead roll ..." is shared (Pixie Guide, Wyll, Barbarian Class, Krark's Thumb compile it) and must stay compiled. Engine need: after the roll, before results are used (CR 706.2), swap one result with the base power or toughness the permanent's as-enters rolls set (a characteristic-setting effect, CR 613.4b); its as-enters rolls are compiled in cards5-game-rules-dice (`dice`): do this after that family.
  - Celebr-8000: "At the beginning of combat on your turn, roll two six-sided dice. For each result of 1, ~ gets +1/+1 until end of turn. For each other result, it gains the indicated ability until end of turn. If you rolled doubles, it also gains double strike until end of turn." with a bulleted result table (2 — menace, 3 — vigilance, ...). CR 706.5 names it as the one card with a doubles rule; search "for each result of", "the indicated ability": only Celebr-8000. "roll two six-sided dice" and "If you rolled doubles" compile (C5-cel-2): hand-write only the per-result bonuses and grants (C5-cel-1, C5-cel-3 fail).
  - Curse of Leeches // Leeching Lurker: "As ~ transforms into ~, attach it to a player." — a permanent that becomes an Aura in place (day/night) and is attached to a player chosen as it transforms (CR 712.18, 303.4f; ruling: "Attaching Curse of Leeches as it transforms doesn't target a player, so it can be attached to a player with hexproof this way"). Search: every transforming DFC with an Aura face (Accursed Witch, Faithbound Judge, Radiant Grace, the Innistrad: Crimson Vow Spirits): the others enter "attached to" a target or are cast; "As ~ enters, attach it to a player" fails too (C5-col-3, C5-R-oo-2), but no other in-scope card attaches as it enters or transforms. cards4-cast-zones (`as-enters-actions`) left it. Engine need: an as-transforms replacement that attaches the permanent to a chosen player (it becomes an Aura with enchant player; if no player can be chosen it's put into the graveyard by the state-based action, CR 704.5m).

  - Lodestone Bauble (added by the re-plan; left by cards4-paper-effects): "{N}, {T}, Sacrifice ~: Put up to four target basic land cards from a player's graveyard on top of their library in any order. That player draws a card at the beginning of the next turn's upkeep." — a player chosen, not targeted, as the targets are chosen, even with zero targets, who then draws (ruling: "If you choose zero targets, you still choose a player. That player will draw a card at the beginning of the next turn's upkeep."). Search "from a player's graveyard", "single graveyard ... (that player|their owner)": only Lodestone Bauble (Serene Remembrance and Jötun Grunt use "a single graveyard" with no player referent; "for each opponent, ... up to one target ... that player controls" iterates players rather than choosing one). Probes: with "target player's graveyard" the whole text compiles (C5-W-lod-1), "a single graveyard" compiles (C5-W-lod-2); "a player's graveyard" fails (C5-W-lod-6), and "Choose a player." before it fails (C5-W-lod-4). Engine need: the player choice made with the targets (CR 601.2c via 602.2b), constraining them to that player's graveyard and kept for "That player" (the delayed draw at the next turn's upkeep and the move onto their library are compiled grammar: hand-write only the choice, as a manual ability built from the compiled AST with the player slot filled by the choice).
  - North Star (added by the re-plan; left by cards4-costs-mana-replacements): "{N}, {T}: For one spell this turn, you may spend mana as though it were mana of any type to pay that spell's mana cost." — a one-use permission for one spell of its controller's choosing cast later this turn (CR 609.4b; reminder text: additional costs are still paid normally). Search "For one spell", "spend mana as though[^.]*(next|one) spell", "(Until end of turn|this turn), (you|players) may spend mana as though": only North Star; every other "spend mana as though" effect is tied to named cards (Outrageous Robbery's "of any type", Daxos of Meletis, Grenzo, Breeches) or is a static. Probes: the permission fails as an activated effect in every form, with "Until end of turn", "the next spell you cast this turn" or "of any color" (C5-W-ns-1 to C5-W-ns-4); "The next spell you cast this turn costs {1} less" compiles (C5-W-ns-5). Engine need: a use-once spend permission the player applies to one spell as its cost is paid this turn (next_spell.rs has one-shot spell modifiers, as_though.rs the spend permission); the cost and tap are compiled.

COMMON RULES (hand-written definitions):
- Compile first (CLAUDE.md, src/cards/mod.rs): before writing a definition, re-run the search in its entry over the current card data; if another in-scope card shares the construct, compile it instead and say so in your report. Hand-write only the one-off part; the card's other parts must compile.
- Every manual card needs a test in tests/cards/m_<card>.rs asserting the in-game outcome with the real card (cr!() and ruling!() citations); `mtg-tools manual-check` (also run by `cargo test`) must pass.
- Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- DONE when the listed texts are supported with tests (or a text is listed with a reason: a compiled construct found, an engine gap) and `cargo test --workspace` passes.
