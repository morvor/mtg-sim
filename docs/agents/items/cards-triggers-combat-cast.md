# cards-triggers-combat-cast: Trigger grammar II: cast, attack, block, damage and targeting trigger conditions — 333 texts / 328 cards (266 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 333 unsupported ability texts (333 on paper cards) blocking 328 paper cards; 266 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards-triggers-combat-cast.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compositional cast/attack/block/damage/target triggers: "whenever you cast your first Human creature spell each turn", "whenever a player casts their second spell during their turn", "whenever you cast a noncreature spell that targets only ~", "whenever you attack with one or more non-Gnome creatures", "whenever two or more creatures you control attack a player", "whenever ~ attacks or blocks while you control a Dinosaur", "whenever equipped creature blocks or becomes blocked by a creature", "whenever one or more Pirates you control deal damage to your opponents", "whenever a source you control deals noncombat damage to an opponent during your turn", "whenever you or another permanent you control becomes the target of a spell or ability an opponent controls", "whenever an opponent activates an ability that isn't a mana ability", "whenever a spell or ability is put onto the stack". Also trigger bodies whose referent is the other object of a combat/damage event ("Whenever equipped creature deals damage to a creature, exile that creature").

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards):

APPROACH: 1. Cast triggers: caster phrase + ordinal ("your first/second ... each turn", NthSpellCast exists) + spell filter (reuse object-phrase grammar incl. "that targets only ~", "with mana value N or greater", "from anywhere other than your hand").
2. Attack triggers: "you attack with [N or more] [filter]" (PlayerAttacksWith), "[filter] attack(s) [a player | you | one of your opponents]", "attacks or blocks while [condition]" (TriggerCond::Where with a Condition).
3. Damage triggers: source filter + combat/noncombat + recipient filter (player/opponent/creature/battle/planeswalker) + batching ("one or more ... deal combat damage to a player").
4. Targeting triggers: "becomes the target of a [spell|ability] [controller filter]", subjects including players ("you or another permanent you control").
5. Expose referents: the damaged/blocked/blocking object as TriggerOtherObject, the damaged player as TriggerPlayer.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS:
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (an Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- A text can contain a second failing construct owned by another item. Fix it when it is small; otherwise leave it and list it under remaining gaps with the owning item.
- One-offs. If a text's construct is genuinely unique (no other card shares it) and compiling it would be hacky, don't force it: once cards-manual-defs has landed, give it a hand-written definition in src/cards/ with a test; before that, list it as a manual candidate.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` and the baseline filters above. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
