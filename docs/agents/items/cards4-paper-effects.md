# cards4-paper-effects: Effect grammar for the paper cards the tools counted as digital: choices made by other players, random hand cards, "or" choices, results "this way", referents, zone phrases, repeated processes, values and player verbs — 42 texts / 41 cards (38 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 42 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 41 paper cards; 38 become fully supported when this item alone is done (Tetravus, Storm Elemental and Soul Exchange also need another item's construct: ALSO BLOCKED BY). These cards are legal in paper formats (Legacy, Vintage, Premodern, Commander, ...), but the tools counted them as digital-only because their Scryfall oracle record is an MTGO-only reprint (Masters Edition I-IV, Tempest Remastered, Vintage Masters), so no earlier round planned them (they sat in the unscheduled cards-digital-alchemy list). Item paper-scope-1 fixes the tools; cards4-paper-plan planned the texts (card-support-plan-4.md, "Paper-scope texts"). Each text was probed (round3/autoprobe.py) and drilled to the failing words (round4/drills.tsv, ids `P-...`). The full list ships as items/cards4-paper-effects.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile the effect sentences of old cards (Alpha to Tempest, through the Masters Edition reprints): objects and targets chosen by a player other than the controller ("to any target of their choice", "sacrifice a land of an opponent's choice", "That land's controller may attach ~ to a land of their choice", "Target opponent chooses one."), random hand cards ("Look at a card at random in target player's hand", "reveals X cards at random", "Discard any number of cards at random"), effects joined by "or" with an implicit choice ("base power 1 or base toughness 1", "Put a +1/+1 counter on ~ or ~ gains flying, first strike, or trample", "loses first strike or swampwalk"), results "this way" in new forms ("If you sacrifice an Island this way", "If ~ deals damage to you this way", "If ~ is destroyed this way", "If those permanents are exchanged this way", "If you do, create that many", "the sacrificed artifact's mana value" and "the difference"), referents ("the creature", "the other", "each of those creatures with flying", "that Sliver", "the token" across abilities, "loses all poison counters ... that much", "the exiled card" of a cost), zone phrases ("shuffle ~ into your library", "the top card of your graveyard", "from it" after revealing a hand, "a player's graveyard ... their library"), repeated processes (mill until a condition, repeat with payments), values ("half X damage, rounded down, ... and half X damage, rounded up", "If target creature has toughness 5 or greater ... Otherwise", "no more scream counters", "You may pay {2} for each wage counter on it") and player verbs ("You destroy four lands you control", "they tap all lands ... and lose all unspent mana", "You and target player exchange control of the creature you each control with the greatest mana value", "you both own and control").

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards; the failing piece; the engine need):
- choices by another player (family `chooser-phrases`): 5. e.g. "Whenever a creature enters, that creature's controller may have it deal damage equal to its power to any target of their choice." [Pandemonium]; "At the beginning of your upkeep, unless you pay {B}{B}{B}, tap ~ and sacrifice a land of an opponent's choice." [Demonic Hordes]; "That land's controller may attach ~ to a land of their choice." [Kudzu]; "Target opponent chooses one." [Intuition]; "then discards a third of the cards in their hand, then sacrifices a third of the creatures they control of their choice ... Round up each time." [Pox]
  Failing piece: probes: "... to any target" compiles, "... to any target of their choice" fails; "Each player sacrifices a land of their choice" compiles, "Sacrifice a land of an opponent's choice" fails; "Attach ~ to target creature" compiles, "attach ~ to a creature of their choice" fails; "chooses one of them" compiles, the bare "chooses one" fails; "loses a third of their life, rounded up" compiles, a third of the cards or creatures fails.
  Engine need: targets chosen by a player other than the ability's controller (CR 115.1, 603.3d; Pandemonium's rulings: the entering creature's controller chooses and the damage source is the creature), a sacrifice whose object another player chooses (CR 701.21a), an untargeted attach by another player (CR 701.3), "one" as an elliptical object over the revealed set, fractions in discard and sacrifice counts with a trailing "Round up each time".
- random hand cards (family `random-hand`): 3. e.g. "Look at a card at random in target player's hand." [Urza's Bauble]; "Target opponent reveals X cards at random from their hand. Then that player discards all cards with that name revealed this way." [Nebuchadnezzar]; "Discard any number of cards at random." [Rites of Initiation]
  Failing piece: probes: "Target player reveals a card at random from their hand" compiles; looking (not revealing) at a random card, several random cards, and "any number ... at random" fail.
  Engine need: a private look at a random hidden card (CR 701.16; only the looking player learns it: mtg-api observations), several distinct random cards, and a number chosen on resolution before the random discard (Rites' rulings).
- "or" choices (family `or-choices`): 4. e.g. "Until end of turn, target creature has base power 1 or base toughness 1." [Vhati il-Dal]; "Put a +1/+1 counter on ~ or ~ gains flying, first strike, or trample." [Flowstone Sculpture]; "Put a +1/+1 counter on target creature or that creature gains banding, first strike, or trample." [Nature's Blessing]; "Target creature loses first strike or swampwalk until end of turn." [Urborg]
  Failing piece: probes: "~ gains your choice of flying, first strike, or trample" and "loses your choice of first strike or swampwalk" compile; without "your choice of", and two effects joined by "or", fail.
  Engine need: a choice between whole effects made by the controller on resolution (CR 608.2d), and the implicit "your choice of" for keyword lists; base power or base toughness (CR 613.4b).
- results "this way" (family `results`): 7. e.g. "If you sacrifice an Island this way, ~ deals N damage to you." [Serendib Djinn] (and Gargantuan Gorilla); "If ~ deals damage to you this way, tap it." [Minion of Leshrac]; "If ~ is destroyed this way, it deals N damage to you." [Cosmic Horror]; "If those permanents are exchanged this way, destroy all Auras attached to them." [Gauntlets of Chaos]; "you may remove any number of +N/+N counters from ~. If you do, create that many ..." [Tetravus]; "If that card's mana value is less than or equal to the sacrificed artifact's mana value ... If it's greater, you may pay {X}, where X is the difference." [Transmute Artifact]
  Failing piece: probes: "If you sacrificed an Island this way" and "If an Island was sacrificed this way" compile, the present tense fails; "If that creature is destroyed this way" compiles, with ~ it fails; the result test on damage and on an exchange fail; the remove-any-number-then-that-many form fails as an effect (compiles as a cost).
  Engine need: results of an earlier instruction in the same ability (damage dealt, destroyed, exchanged: CR 701.12b, a failed exchange does nothing), "that many" after an any-number effect, a cost referent compared with a found card, and X as a difference (Transmute Artifact's rulings: you pay X on resolution).
- referents (family `referents`): 9. e.g. "If the creature would leave the battlefield, exile it instead ..." [Dreams of the Dead]; "Exile one of those creatures and put two +N/+N counters on the other." [Cannibalize]; "~ deals N damage to each of those creatures with flying." [Winter Blast]; "that Sliver gets +N/+N until end of turn for each creature blocking it" [Spined Sliver]; "When ~ leaves the battlefield, exile the token." / "When the token leaves the battlefield, sacrifice ~." [Dance of Many]; "Target player loses all poison counters. ~ deals that much damage to that player." [Leeches]; "{U}, Exile the top card of your library: If the exiled card is a snow land, ..." [Storm Elemental]; "Put a +N/+N counter on that creature if the exiled creature was a Thrull." [Soul Exchange]
  Failing piece: probes: "If it would leave the battlefield" compiles, "If the creature would" fails; "Exile one of those creatures" compiles, "... on the other" fails in every wording; "each of those creatures" compiles, with "with flying" it fails; "it gets ... for each creature blocking it" compiles, "that Sliver" fails; the token works inside one ability and fails across abilities; "Remove all poison counters from target player" compiles, "loses all poison counters" fails; "If it's a snow land card" after the cost compiles, "If the exiled card is" after a cost and "if the exiled creature was a Thrull" after an additional cost fail.
  Engine need: mostly Builder referent tracking: "the creature" for a returned card, "the other" of two targets after "one of those" (the choice is made on resolution), a filter on a referent group, a subtype noun as the trigger subject's anaphor, a token linked across the card's abilities (CR 607.1d: the token and the source that created it; Stangg compiles it in one paragraph), a player losing counters as an action with "that much", the cost's exiled card or creature (cards4-referents-statics' `cost-referents`: reuse it).
- zone phrases (family `zone-phrases`): 4. e.g. "When ~ is put into your graveyard from the battlefield, you may shuffle ~ into your library." [Guan Yu, Sainted Warrior]; "{N}: Put the top card of your graveyard on the bottom of your library." [Soldevi Digger]; "you may reveal your hand and put all land cards from it onto the battlefield. If you do, discard your hand." [Manabond]; "Put up to four target basic land cards from a player's graveyard on top of their library in any order." [Lodestone Bauble]
  Failing piece: probes: "you may shuffle it into your library" compiles, "~" as the object fails; "a card from your graveyard" compiles, "the top card of your graveyard" fails as an object; "from your hand" compiles, "from it" after revealing fails; "from a single graveyard ... their owner's library" compiles, "a player's graveyard ... their library" fails.
  Engine need: graveyard order as an object ("the top card of your graveyard"; the engine keeps graveyard order: r404_graveyard_order.rs; round 4 put Phyrexian Grimoire and Spinning Darkness, which read it, in cards4-cast-zones: merge it), ~ found again in a graveyard after a zone change (CR 400.7: the trigger tracks the new object), several targets constrained to one player's graveyard.
- repeated processes (family `repeat-loops`): 2. e.g. "Target opponent mills a card, then repeats this process until a creature card or X cards have been put into their graveyard this way, whichever comes first." [Helm of Obedience]; "Target opponent chooses a card in your graveyard. You may pay {G}. If you do, repeat this process except that opponent can't choose a card already chosen for ~. Then put the last chosen card into your hand." [Forgotten Lore]
  Failing piece: probes: "reveals cards ... until they reveal a creature card" compiles; milling until a condition fails in every wording; the repeated choice with a payment fails.
  Engine need: a mill loop with two stop conditions measured by cards put into the graveyard (Helm's rulings: if an effect like Leyline of the Void keeps cards out of the graveyard, the process never stops and the whole library is milled; the engine must stop at an empty library), a repeated opponent choice with optional payments and a memory of chosen cards.
- values (family `values`): 4. e.g. "~ deals half X damage, rounded down, to any target, and half X damage, rounded up, to you." [Banshee]; "If target creature has toughness N or greater, it gets +N/-N until end of turn. Otherwise, it gets +N/-X until end of turn, where X is its toughness minus N." [Blood Lust]; "If there are no more scream counters on it, put it into your graveyard and each player returns ..." [All Hallow's Eve]; "You may pay {N} for each wage counter on it. If you don't, ..." [Rogue Skycaptain]
  Failing piece: probes: each half alone compiles, the two in one sentence fail; the trailing "if it has toughness 5 or greater" compiles, a leading "If target creature has ..." fails (shared with Hidetsugu's Second Rite and Vraska, Betrayal's Sting's "If target player has"); "Then if it has no scream counters on it" compiles, "no more" fails; "unless you pay {2} for each" compiles, "You may pay {2} for each" fails.
  Engine need: two rounded halves of one value, a condition that introduces the spell's target (CR 601.2c: the target is chosen on casting even if the condition fails), a counted optional payment.
- player verbs and filters (family `player-actions`): 4. e.g. "You destroy four lands you control, then target opponent destroys four lands they control." [Burning of Xinye]; "If that player doesn't, they tap all lands with mana abilities they control and lose all unspent mana." [Power Sink]; "You and target player exchange control of the creature you each control with the greatest mana value. Then exchange control of artifacts the same way. ..." [Juxtapose]; "Return target permanent you both own and control to your hand." [Obelisk of Undoing]
  Failing piece: probes: "sacrifices four lands" compiles, "destroys four lands they control" fails; after "If that player doesn't," "you draw a card" compiles and "they ..." fails; exchanging with one selected creature compiles, per-player selections fail; "you own" compiles, "you (both) own and control" fails as a target filter.
  Engine need: a player choosing objects to destroy (regeneration and indestructible apply), the unpaid player as a follow-up subject (shared with Braids's Frightful Return, Pillar Tombs of Aku, Umbilicus), per-player greatest-mana-value selections for an exchange with ties chosen by each controller and "the same way" (Balance's grammar), an owner-and-controller filter.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 4, paper-scope texts):
- These texts were never in a card item: the tools counted their cards as digital-only until paper-scope-1, so they sat in the unscheduled cards-digital-alchemy list (column previous_item). Many are first-edition wordings the compiler has never seen. The plan probed every text: column failing_piece is the first failing sentence (or cost, trigger, line), and column note has the drilled failing words ("drilled: ... compiles; ... fails", with the drill ids of round4/drills.tsv) and the second failing piece. Re-probe before starting a family (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; it picks the first oracle record of the name, so for a name that tokens share, such as Shapeshifter, check the record; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Before paper-scope-1 lands, `mtg-tools unsupported --paper` and `card-coverage` don't count these cards; use `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` (no --paper) to measure them, and `docs/agents/round4/check_plan.py --live`, which applies the paper rule itself.
- Where a text fails because the engine can't do it (the engine need in each family), build the engine part first and test it with a custom card before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). The plan searched every one-off-looking construct over all in-scope paper cards (by format legality, so including these cards); the genuine one-offs are in cards4-manual-defs. If a text here turns out to be one (search the card data before deciding), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards4-manual-defs. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`mtg-tools unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate, proposed deferral).
