### Question 1: "if it's the first time [the trigger event] this turn" on resolution

**The question.** CR 603.4 checks an intervening "if" again as the ability resolves. Botanical Brawler, Stalwart Successor and Captain America, Living Legend condition their abilities on the trigger event being the first of its kind this turn; Vengevine, Alania and Codespell Cleric on the triggering (or the source's own) spell being the Nth spell of a kind cast this turn. If another event of the kind happens while the ability waits (a second counter put on the permanent, the creature untapped and tapped again, another spell cast in response), is the condition still true as the ability resolves?

**Survey of the wordings** (`data/oracle_cards.jsonl.gz`, scripts in the investigator's scratchpad; every card whose Oracle text says "first/second/third ... time", "for the first time", "the Nth spell ... this turn", or an intervening "if" with an ordinal or "only"/"no other"/"another" about this turn):

**Readings.**
- **R1, about the trigger event (now the engine's throughout).** "It" in "if it's the first time ..." is the event that made the ability trigger (in Vengevine's, the triggering spell). Rechecking "the stated condition" (603.4) asks again whether that event was the first of its kind this turn, and it still was: what happened before it can't change. Counted as the ability resolves, only this turn's events up to the trigger event and those simultaneous with it count.
- **R2, a recount (the engine's for Botanical Brawler and Captain America).** "It's the first time ..." read as "it has happened exactly once this turn", a state of the game rechecked like 603.4's life total: a second event of the kind makes it false. Read the same way, Vengevine's "it's the second creature spell you cast this turn" would be false once a third is cast, against any reading of "it" as the triggering spell, and Alania's and Codespell Cleric's likewise.
- **For R1:** the pronoun; the spell ordinals, whose "it" can only be the spell; Mighty Servant of Leuk-o's and Tunnel Ignus's rulings evaluating an intervening "if" about the trigger event against that event; Vengevine's release notes listing what must still hold as the ability resolves (Vengevine in the graveyard) and not the count; 700.14 fixing the CR's one ordinal trigger event at the moment of the event; consistency (Stalwart Successor and the spell ordinals already read this way); and, an inference from templates rather than a source, the usual wording of the same idea is a trigger condition, never checked again ("Whenever you cast your second spell each turn", 163 cards; "... for the first time each turn", 51), with the intervening "if" used where the ordinal is about each of several objects ("that creature", "that permanent"). Tale of Katara and Toph (Oracle: `Creatures you control have "Whenever this creature becomes tapped for the first time during each of your turns, put a +1/+1 counter on it."`) words Captain America's idea as a trigger condition; only R1 has the two work alike.
- **For R2:** 603.4's recheck "at that time" and its state example; Luminarch Ascension's ruling (a "this turn" condition sees life lost while the ability waits; but it's about the turn, and R1 agrees with it); Aetherflux Reservoir's resolution-time count (an amount in the effect, not about the trigger event).

**Ruled out.**
- **Not A:** no two sources conflict; the question is a gap in them.
- **Not B:** no dated change bears on it (the recheck is in every archived CR since 1999; all the cards are from 2010 on).
- **Not C:** no ruling decides it: none speaks of an event of the kind happening while such an ability waits, and the cards most exposed (Stalwart Successor, Captain America) have no rulings.

**Engine: changed (Botanical Brawler and Captain America), unchanged elsewhere.**
- **Before** (confirmed with a scratch test, Nov 19, not committed): Botanical Brawler with a second +1/+1 counter put on the Bears while its ability waited: its ability did nothing (Brawler stayed at 2 counters); Captain America with the Bears untapped and tapped again in response: the Bears stayed tapped; Stalwart Successor in the same spot: still put its counter (3 on the Bears). Brawler's and Captain America's conditions were `Compare(EventsThisTurn(..), Eq, 1)` counted again as they resolved.
- **After:** both untap/put the counter. The conditions are `Value::EventsDuring(HistoryWindow::ThisTurnUntilTriggered, ..)`: as the ability triggers, this turn's events so far (as `EventsThisTurn` counts them, the rest of the trigger event's simultaneous batch included); as it resolves, only the first `EventInfo::turn_events_seen` of this turn's events, the number recorded when the ability triggered (`Game::seen_as_triggered`, stamped where `detect_triggers` and `check_batch_triggers` create the pending triggers). A copy of the triggered ability keeps it with the trigger event. Pending triggers created elsewhere (delayed, keyword-specific) have no stamp and count this turn's events so far (no such card has an "it's the first time" condition).
- **Code:** `ability.rs` (`HistoryWindow::ThisTurnUntilTriggered`), `object.rs` (`EventInfo::turn_events_seen`), `triggers.rs` (`seen_as_triggered`), `kw/value_results.rs` (`events_until_triggered`, `event_objects_until_triggered`, `turn_events_indexed` with a limit), `history_query.rs` (`events_during`, `objects_during`, `window_events`), `oracle/patterns/history_conditions.rs` (`nth_time_counters`; `window_suffix`), `oracle/patterns/history_conditions_4.rs` (`first_time_referent`), `oracle/render/events_happened.rs` (`window_phrase`, `happened`: the window reads "this turn"). ### Question 2: the order of simultaneous "as enters" replacement effects

**The question.** When several permanents enter the battlefield at the same time, the engine applied the replacement effects that modify how each enters (CR 614.1c–d: "As this creature enters, ...", "enters with ...", "enter as a copy of ...") one permanent after another, in the order of the moves (grouped per player in APNAP order, `Game::move_objects`), without asking. Does their controller choose that order, and when does it matter?

**Conclusion C (settled).** 616.1's words are about the effects modifying how an event affects one object; its own example in 616.1g and Thief of Blood's ruling settle that a player's permanents entering together have their effects applied in the order that player chooses. No source says otherwise (searched: every ruling with "at the same time" or "simultaneously" and "enter" with "apply", "order", "choose" or "replacement"). Not A or B: no two sources conflict, and no dated change bears on it (the 616.1g example has been in the CR since 2017; the Thief of Blood ruling is from 2015).

**Sources** (verbatim; from `data/` unless stated).
- **CR** (effective 2026-09-25):
  - 616.1: "If two or more replacement and/or prevention effects are attempting to modify the way an event affects an object or player, the affected object’s controller (or its owner if it has no controller) or the affected player chooses one to apply, following the steps listed below. If two or more players have to make these choices at the same time, choices are made in APNAP order (see rule 101.4)." 616.1e: "Any of the applicable replacement and/or prevention effects may be chosen." 616.1f: "Once the chosen effect has been applied, this process is repeated (taking into account only replacement or prevention effects that would now be applicable) until there are no more left to apply."
  - 616.1g and its example: "While following the steps in 616.1a–f, one replacement or prevention effect may apply to an event, and another may apply to an event contained within the first event. In this case, the second effect can’t be chosen until after the first effect has been chosen." / "Example: A player is instructed to create a token that’s a copy of Voice of All, which has the ability “As this creature enters, choose a color.” Doubling Season has an ability that reads “If an effect would create one or more tokens under your control, it creates twice that many of those tokens instead.” Because entering the battlefield is an event contained within the event of creating a token, the effect of Doubling Season must be applied first, and then the effects of the two Voice of All tokens may be applied in either order." The example is in every archived CR from the one dated 2017-04-28 (Amonkhet) on, then under 616.1f; it's absent from the one dated 2017-01-19.
  - 614.12: "Some replacement effects modify how a permanent enters the battlefield. (See rules 614.1c–d.) Such effects may come from the permanent itself if they affect only that permanent (as opposed to a general subset of permanents that includes it). They may also come from other sources. To determine which replacement effects apply and how they apply, check the characteristics of the permanent as it would exist on the battlefield, taking into account replacement effects that have already modified how it enters the battlefield (see rule 616.1), continuous effects from the permanent’s own static abilities that would apply to it once it’s on the battlefield, and continuous effects that already exist and would apply to the permanent." 614.12a: "If a replacement effect that modifies how a permanent enters the battlefield requires a choice, that choice is made before the permanent enters the battlefield." 614.12b: "If multiple replacement effects that require choices from a player would modify how multiple permanents enter the battlefield simultaneously, that player may not make choices for those effects that would cause the combined costs of those effects to not be payable." 614.13a: "While applying an effect that modifies how a permanent enters the battlefield, you may have to choose a number of objects that will also change zones. You can’t choose the object that will become that permanent or any other object entering the battlefield at the same time as that object."
- **Thief of Blood** ("As this creature enters, remove all counters from all permanents. - **Lakeside Shack** (and the other lands of its cycle; "This land enters tapped unless a player has 13 or less life."), ruling (2024-09-20): "If one of these lands is entering at the same time as a "shock land" (a cycle of lands originally from Ravnica block that allow you to pay life or have them enter tapped), use the life totals of all players before choices are made for that shock land's replacement effect to determine whether or not the land enters tapped. (They're 2/2 creatures.)"), rulings (2006-09-25): "If Ixidron and another creature are entering at the same time, the other creature enters face up."; (2018-04-27): "Creatures turned face down by Ixidron are 2/2 creatures with no text, no name, no subtypes, no expansion symbol, and no mana cost. ### For the user
- **Question 1 (provisional, `first-time-is-about-the-trigger-event`).** When Botanical Brawler's "if it's the first time +1/+1 counters have been put on that permanent this turn" (or Captain America's "if it's the first time that creature has become tapped this turn") is checked again as the ability resolves (CR 603.4), and the same thing happened again while the ability waited, is the condition still true?
  - **(1) Yes, it's about the event the ability triggered on (now the engine's, as it already was for Stalwart Successor, Vengevine, Alania and Codespell Cleric).** That event stays the first of its kind this turn whatever happens later; Vengevine's "if it's the second creature spell you cast this turn" can only be read this way. Rulings on intervening "if" clauses about the trigger event judge them by that event (Mighty Servant of Leuk-o, Tunnel Ignus). Conditions about the turn rather than the trigger event ("if you didn't lose life this turn", Luminarch Ascension's ruling) still see what happened since.
  - **(2) No, recount as it resolves (the engine's earlier behavior for Botanical Brawler and Captain America only).** "It's the first time" read as "it has happened only once this turn", a state like 603.4's life total: responding with a second counter or a second tapping stops the ability.
  - Recommendation: **(1)**. No ruling or rule decides it; the change back is the two patterns (`nth_time_counters`, `first_time_referent`) using `Value::EventsThisTurn` again, and the entry and the tests of `first_time_recheck.rs`.
- **Question 2 (decided, `simultaneous-entries-ordered-by-controller`).** A player's permanents entering together now have their "as this enters" effects applied in the order the player chooses, asked only when one of them changes the game as it's applied (Thief of Blood, Sutured Ghoul, Ixidron) and another reads or changes it too; the default is the order of the moves. The CR's own example (616.1g) and Thief of Blood's ruling decide it; the when-to-ask rule and the default are the engine's policy.
