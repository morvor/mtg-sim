# cards4-manual-defs: Hand-written card definitions IV: one-off abilities with no shared construct — 15 texts / 14 cards (14 unlocked alone; 13 texts / 12 cards added by cards4-paper-plan)

SCOPE: 15 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 14 paper cards; all 14 become fully supported when this item alone is done. 13 of them (12 cards; previous item cards-digital-alchemy) were added by cards4-paper-plan: see PAPER-SCOPE ADDITIONS below. The original 2: Both texts were in cards3-costs-mana-replacements, which has landed, and are still unsupported after it. The plan's one-off check searched all in-scope paper cards' Oracle text for each construct (and the review repeated it, see card-support-plan-4.md, "Adversarial review"); every other leftover text of the landed round-3 items has a construct that another in-scope card shares, so it is in a cards4 item. The full list ships as items/cards4-manual-defs.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Write hand-written definitions (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, a test per card in tests/cards/m_<card>.rs, `mtg-tools manual-check`) for the two texts below. Rules the AST can't express go in a `KeywordRules` impl with `kinds() -> &[]` using the registry-wide hooks, finding their sources by the custom ability, never by name (CLAUDE.md).

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; the construct and why it is a one-off; the engine need):
- one-off constructs (family `one-off`): 2.
  - Guile: "If a spell or ability you control would counter a spell, instead exile that spell and you may play that card without paying its mana cost." — a replacement of the counter event itself (CR 701.6, 614.1a). Probe: "If a spell or ability you control would counter a spell, you gain 1 life instead." fails. No other in-scope paper card replaces countering (searched "would counter", "would be countered"; "can't be countered" is a different rule). Rulings: a spell that can't be countered isn't affected; exiling is mandatory, casting optional and only right then; a spell exiled this way was never countered. Engine need: a replacement hook where a spell or ability you control counters a spell (stack.rs countering goes through `replace()` with the countering object's controller), then the exile and a one-time free-cast permission.
  - Torrent of Lava: "As long as ~ is on the stack, each creature has \"{T}: Prevent the next 1 damage that would be dealt to ~ by ~ this turn.\"" — a static ability of a sorcery that works while it is on the stack (CR 113.6 exception) and grants each creature an ability whose prevention names both the creature and the spell. Review correction: the on-the-stack condition is NOT a one-off. Kaervek's Torch (Mirage; legal in Legacy and Vintage) has "As long as ~ is on the stack, spells that target it cost {N} more to cast.", also unsupported; the plan's search missed it because the tools' `--paper` filter drops cards whose Scryfall oracle printing is an MTGO-only reprint (see card-support-plan-4.md, decision 2). So compile "As long as ~ is on the stack, <static>" as a static that functions on the stack (CR 113.6) and use it for both cards (Kaervek's Torch then needs only its cost increase for spells that target the source); only Torrent's granted prevention ability ("Prevent the next 1 damage that would be dealt to this creature by ~ this turn", naming both the creature and the spell) stays hand-written if nothing else shares it. Split second is the other on-stack static, a keyword. Probe: "Each creature has \"{T}: You gain 1 life.\"" fails on the sorcery too. Ruling: the ability can be activated and resolved before Torrent itself resolves. Engine need: a compiled static ability that functions on the stack for the spell's source (shared with Kaervek's Torch), a granted activated ability with a prevention shield from that source to that creature for the turn.

PAPER-SCOPE ADDITIONS (cards4-paper-plan, Oct 28): 13 texts on 12 cards. These cards are legal in paper formats but the tools counted them as digital-only (their Scryfall oracle record is an MTGO-only reprint; item paper-scope-1 fixes that), so no earlier item had them; they sat in the unscheduled cards-digital-alchemy list (column previous_item). Until paper-scope-1 lands, measure them with `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` (no --paper) and `docs/agents/round4/check_plan.py --live`. Drill ids `P-...` are in round4/drills.tsv. Each row's note gives the search that makes it a one-off and the shared parts that must come from compiled grammar; the plan's review repeated every search (card-support-plan-4.md, "Paper-scope texts", review).
- Kaervek's Torch (family `on-stack-static`): "As long as ~ is on the stack, spells that target it cost {N} more to cast." Not a one-off: the compiled on-stack static that Torrent of Lava's entry above already plans (CR 113.6). Compile it here and use it for both cards; then Kaervek's Torch is fully compiled ("Spells that target ~ cost {2} more to cast." compiles on a permanent).
- one-offs (family `one-off`), 12 texts on 11 cards:
  - Illusionary Mask: casting a creature card face down from an ability, limited by "the mana you spent on {X}", and its own turn-face-up replacement (facedown.rs, CR 708).
  - Tawnos's Coffin: noting "the number and kind of counters" and returning the card with them and its Auras attached (CR 607.2e notes). Its trigger "When ~ leaves the battlefield or becomes untapped" is cards4-triggers-durations' either-event delayed trigger (The Pandorica): do this card after that item and hand-write only the note and the return.
  - Volrath's Shapeshifter: "has the full text of that card" (rulings: a text-changing effect; it keeps "{2}: Discard a card."). Its condition "the top card of your graveyard" is shared graveyard-order grammar (cards4-paper-effects' Soldevi Digger).
  - Ice Cauldron (two texts): noting the type and amount of mana spent on an activation and adding "~'s last noted type and amount of mana", spendable only on "the last card exiled with ~" (CR 607.2e).
  - Titania's Song: "If ~ leaves the battlefield, this effect continues until end of turn." (a static's effect that outlives its source). March of the Machines' "is an artifact creature" wording compiles; compile "becomes" if another card needs it.
  - Time Vault: "If you would begin your turn while ~ is tapped, you may skip that turn instead. If you do, untap ~." (skip.rs, CR 614.10; rulings: decided as the turn would begin; Time Vault untaps before anything else).
  - Sorrow's Path: swapping two blockers ("Each one then blocks all creatures the other was blocking").
  - Takklemaggot: returning as a non-Aura enchantment that loses "enchant creature" and gains an upkeep trigger aimed at the chosen creature's controller ("return ~ to the battlefield attached to target creature" compiles; "attached to a creature of your choice" is cards4-paper-effects' `chooser-phrases` construct).
  - Cyclopean Tomb: counters tracked by the source that put them ("a land that a mire counter was put onto with ~ but that a mire counter has not been removed from with ~"); its "at the beginning of each of your upkeeps for the rest of the game" delayed trigger is shared (epic, CR 702.50): compile that part.
  - Firestorm Phoenix: "Until that player's next turn, that player plays with that card revealed in their hand and can't play it." (rulings: only that card is tracked; another Phoenix can be played).
  - Musician: "If it doesn't have "At the beginning of your upkeep, destroy ~ unless you pay {N} for each music counter on it," it gains that ability." (rulings: a creature gains the ability once however many Musicians target it); the grant and its "for each music counter" cost compile.
- Kaervek's Torch and the Torrent of Lava entry above share the on-stack static (compiled, not hand-written).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 4):
- Before writing a definition, re-run the one-off check: search all in-scope paper cards' Oracle text for the construct (a card that shares it means it must be compiled instead, CLAUDE.md "Compile first"). `mtg-tools manual-check` (also run by `cargo test`) fails on stale, now-compilable, duplicate or untested entries.
- The exact normalized block for `ManualAbility` comes from `mtg-tools unsupported --card "Name" --raw`.
- Faithfulness first. Each card's test asserts the in-game outcome with TestGame (citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for the card's Scryfall rulings exercised). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- DONE when both cards are fully supported with tested definitions, or a remaining text is listed with a reason.
