# cards6-history-permissions: Turn history and permissions VII: block history anchored on a target (with the blocked creature's controller at the block), damage history per source spell with an untargeted player choice by history, and play/look permissions over another player's library top — 4 texts / 4 cards (4 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 4 unsupported ability texts on 4 paper cards (Glyph of Reincarnation, Glyph of Delusion, Backdraft, Xanathar, Guild Kingpin); each is its card's only unsupported text. All four were in cards5-referents-statics (landed; families `history` and `permissions-attach`) and in round-3/4 items before. Three need a history the engine keeps for the turn read in a new way; Xanathar needs a permission over another player's library.

GOAL: Compile "Destroy all creatures that were blocked by target Wall this turn. They can't be regenerated. For each creature that died this way, put a creature card from the graveyard of the player who controlled that creature the last time it became blocked by that Wall onto the battlefield under its owner's control." [Glyph of Reincarnation]; "Put X glyph counters on target creature that target Wall blocked this turn, where X is the power of that blocked creature. ..." [Glyph of Delusion]; "Choose a player who cast one or more sorcery spells this turn. ~ deals damage to that player equal to half the damage dealt by one of those sorcery spells this turn, rounded down." [Backdraft]; "At the beginning of your upkeep, choose target opponent. Until end of turn, that player can't cast spells, you may look at the top card of their library any time, you may play the top card of their library, and you may spend mana as though it were mana of any color to cast spells this way." [Xanathar, Guild Kingpin].

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards; the failing piece; the engine need):
- block history (family `block-history`): 2. [Glyph of Reincarnation], [Glyph of Delusion]
  Failing piece: "creatures that were blocked by that creature this turn" compiles with the source's own creature (Glyph of Doom) and "target creature that was blocked by a Wall this turn" compiles; a target as the anchor ("blocked by target Wall this turn", "that target Wall blocked this turn") fails in every form; then Reincarnation's "the graveyard of the player who controlled that creature the last time it became blocked by that Wall".
  Engine need: the turn's block history queried with a target object as the blocker (CR 509.1, 115.1; the anchor is a target, so it is checked on resolution, 608.2b); the controller of the blocked creature recorded with each block (only Glyph of Reincarnation reads it; keep it in the same history record, not a card-specific store). Rulings: all creatures are destroyed first, then the returns; the returned cards may be the same creatures.
- damage history (family `damage-history`): 1. [Backdraft]
  Failing piece: "Choose a player who cast one or more sorcery spells this turn" fails where the targeted form and "Choose a player." compile; "half the damage dealt by one of those sorcery spells this turn, rounded down" fails where "half the damage dealt to you this turn" compiles.
  Engine need: an untargeted player choice with a history filter; the turn's damage history keyed by the source spell (CR 120.1, 120.3; the spell is gone, so key the record by the spell's object id and its cast record), with one of the chosen player's sorcery spells chosen on resolution (who chooses: Backdraft's controller; record as an interpretation if the CR is silent), halved and rounded down (CR 107.1a). If the damage history keeps no source spell, add it as a small extension of kw/damage_history_by.rs.
- permissions over another player's library (family `permissions`): 1. [Xanathar, Guild Kingpin]
  Failing piece: "you may look at the top card of their library any time" and "you may play the top card of their library" fail in every form (target opponent's, that player's, each opponent's; statics and until end of turn); the same permissions over your own library compile; the spend rider "to cast spells this way" compiles after a play permission over exiled cards (The Ruinous Powers).
  Engine need: the top-of-library permission with the library's owner as a parameter: looking at another player's library top any time for the turn (CR 401.5; ruling: as a spell is cast from it, the new top card can't be seen until the casting finishes), and playing it (CR 601.3, 305.2: the land counts as your land play; normal timing; costs paid, alternative costs allowed; the card stays owned by the opponent and goes to their graveyard). Only Xanathar has this construct among in-scope paper cards (search in the list's note), but it is the compiled "top card of your library" permission with a player parameter: compile it; if playing from another player's library turns out to need casting-pipeline changes out of proportion, report it as a manual candidate.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 6):
- These texts are the last of the long tail: every one was in at least one landed item (column previous_item; most in two to five) and is still unsupported after it. The plan re-probed each on Nov 4 (column failing_piece: the first failing sentence) and drilled it (column note: "drilled: X compiles; Y fails", with the drill ids of round6/drills.tsv, `C6-...`, and the second and third failing pieces). Re-probe before starting (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; write the card's own name as ~ in probe texts; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Each item is one engine feature or one grammar area, not a family of wordings: build the engine part first and test it with a custom card (`CardDef::custom`) before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several wordings, including the drills' neutral-card forms.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). Add TestGame tests with the real card asserting the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for the card's Scryfall rulings exercised: these cards' rulings are all UNCOVERED today). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). The plan searched every one-off-looking construct over all in-scope paper cards (paper and legal or restricted in a format; the searches are in each note); the genuine one-offs are in cards6-manual-defs. If a text here turns out to need a construct no other card has and the engine can't express it as grammar, don't force a pattern: list it in your report as a manual candidate. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) may be edited by other items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files (`oracle/patterns/<topic>_6.rs`), and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` (base on Nov 4: 32,059 / 32,111 paper cards), `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards, and `docs/agents/round6/check_plan.py`, which rebuilds the live list from the release binary and lists the snapshot texts supported since. Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when every text of the item compiles with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per card), or, for a text that can't, it is listed in the report with the reason (engine gap with what is missing, belongs to item X, manual candidate with the search that makes it one, proposed deferral). With so few texts per item, "about 85%" means all but at most one.
