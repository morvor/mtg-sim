# cards3-triggers-durations: Trigger and duration grammar IV: trigger events and causes, delayed and "this turn" triggers created by effects, "for as long as" and next-turn durations, triggers that trigger additional times or not at all, costs paid to activate the triggering ability — 68 texts / 66 cards (66 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 68 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 66 paper cards; all of them become fully supported when this item alone is done. Added by the plan's review: cards2-triggers-delayed landed while round 3 was planned, and 67 of its texts (62 from its list, 5 from the round-3 pending re-check list that waited for it) are still unsupported after it landed; Verrak, Warped Sengir came from cards3-manual-defs (shared construct, see `trigger-modifiers`). 17 texts have a second failing piece found by the probes (column note). The full list ships as items/cards3-triggers-durations.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: What cards2-triggers-delayed left. (1) Trigger events: causes ("Whenever a spell or ability causes a player to shuffle their library", "Whenever a white instant or sorcery spell causes you to gain life", "Whenever a spell or ability an opponent controls causes a land to be put into your graveyard from the battlefield"), exact amounts ("deals exactly 1 damage", "each lose exactly 1 life"), batched exile from several zones ("Whenever one or more cards are put into exile from your hand or a spell or ability you control exiles one or more permanents from the battlefield"), plays of linked exiled cards ("Whenever a player plays a card exiled with ~", "with the same name as one of the exiled cards"), untap-step events ("Whenever you untap one or more permanents during your untap step"), objects linked to the source ("When the creature put onto the battlefield with ~ dies", "When the chosen creature leaves the battlefield", "When the chosen player loses the game"), teamwork, Saga final chapters triggering, gaining control "from another player", "enters attached to a creature", time counters removed while exiled, "When ~ enters and whenever ...". (2) Triggers created by effects: "this turn" triggers about a target or a chosen player ("Whenever target creature deals combat damage to a non-Wall creature this turn", the Great Train Heist mode), delayed triggers on a referenced object ("When that creature dies this turn", "When that creature leaves the battlefield, return ~ from exile"), "at the beginning of the next end step, if the player hasn't played the card", "at the beginning of the end step of that player's next turn", delayed mana ("add ... at the beginning of your next main phase"), "until the end of defending player's next turn, that player gets two rad counters whenever they cast a spell", granted abilities that are triggers or replacements until end of turn. (3) Durations: "for as long as ~ remains tapped / attached to it / on the battlefield", "for as long as that creature remains tapped", "for as long as they're the monarch", "during that player's next turn", "during their next turn", "This turn, that player ... plays with their hand revealed". (4) Trigger modifiers and activation history: triggers an additional time for a scoped set of sources or events (Cloud, Panoptic Projektor, The Masamune), trigger suppression scoped to dying creatures or to opponents' permanents (Hushbringer, Elesh Norn), and "if [a cost] was paid to activate it" for the ability whose activation triggered (Ashnod the Uncaring, Way of the Mind Sculptor, Verrak).

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards; the failing piece; the engine need):
- trigger events and causes (family `trigger-events`): 30. e.g. "Whenever a spell or ability causes a player to shuffle their library" [Psychogenic Probe]; "Whenever one or more cards are put into exile from your hand or a spell or ability you control exiles one or more permanents from the battlefield" [Hero of Bretagard, Ranar the Ever-Watchful]; "Whenever a player plays a card exiled with ~" [Prowl, Stoic Strategist]; "When the creature put onto the battlefield with ~ dies" [Diabolic Servitude]
  Failing piece: the trigger condition (probe: the longest "TRIGGER, draw a card." that compiles).
  Engine need: causes recorded on events (which spell or ability caused the event, its controller and colour; CR 603.2, and CR 609.7 for an event's source), exact-amount filters on damage and life-loss events, batched exile events across zones (CR 603.10a for leaving the battlefield), play events of cards linked to the source (CR 607.2a), untap-step untap events, objects and players chosen or put onto the battlefield by the source as trigger subjects, "triggers" of Saga chapter abilities as an event (CR 714.2e), control-gain events with the previous controller.
- delayed and "this turn" triggers created by effects (family `delayed-effect-triggers`): 15. e.g. "Whenever target creature deals combat damage to a non-Wall creature this turn, destroy that non-Wall creature." [Acidic Dagger]; "When that creature dies this turn, return a creature card from its owner's graveyard to the battlefield under the control of that creature's owner." [Reincarnation]; "At the beginning of the end step of that player's next turn, that player returns those cards to their hand." [Suppress]
  Failing piece: the delayed trigger sentence.
  Engine need: delayed triggers bound to targets, referents and players of the creating ability (CR 603.7a-c), with "this turn" durations (603.7b) and intervening ifs about the referent ("if the player hasn't played the card"), steps of another player's next turn, delayed mana (CR 605.1b does not apply: it uses the stack), granted triggers and replacements on a referenced object.
- durations (family `durations`): 15. e.g. "For as long as ~ remains tapped, another target permanent loses all abilities and can't attack or block." [Immovable Rod]; "gain control of target creature that player controls for as long as they're the monarch." [Garland, Royal Kidnapper]; "During that player's next turn, the chosen creatures attack if able, and other creatures can't attack." [Oracle en-Vec]
  Failing piece: the effect with its duration.
  Engine need: "for as long as" durations on a condition of the source or of another object or player (CR 611.2b: an effect whose duration never starts does nothing, one whose condition ends stays ended), "during that player's next turn" effects that start later (CR 611.2a), copy and control effects with these durations (layers 1 and 2), and player-level effects (playing with the hand revealed, "can't attack you during their next turn").
- trigger modifiers and activation history (family `trigger-modifiers`): 8. e.g. "Creatures entering or dying don't cause abilities to trigger." [Hushbringer]; "If turning a face-down permanent face up causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time." [Panoptic Projektor]; "if one or more permanents were sacrificed to activate it, you may copy that ability." [Ashnod the Uncaring]; "if life was paid to activate it, you may pay that much life again." [Verrak, Warped Sengir]
  Failing piece: the static or the condition.
  Engine need: additional-time and suppression statics scoped by event kind (turning face up, dying) and by source (the equipped creature and its Equipment, emblems you own) (CR 603.2d); the costs paid for an activation recorded on the stack object and readable by "Whenever you activate ..." triggers (sacrificed permanents, loyalty counters removed, life paid and its amount; cf. Dynaheir's "by spending four or more mana to activate it", which already compiles).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 3):
- These texts are the long tail: most were in a round-1 or round-2 item (column previous_item) and are still unsupported after it landed. Before starting a family, read that item's brief and its commits (`git log --grep '^<item>:'`) for the reason it stopped; re-probe the text (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text) to find the exact failing words: the failing_piece column is the sentence (or cost, trigger, mode, table row) that fails, not always the smallest failing phrase. Column note "then also: ..." is the next piece that still failed with the first one replaced by a filler (sometimes only a referent to the replaced words).
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts of cards that also have a text owned by a round-2 item still in progress (round3/inflight.tsv: cards2-copies-tokens-counters, cards-manual-defs-2; cards2-triggers-delayed landed while round 3 was planned, and its still-unsupported texts are planned in cards3 items) are listed under ALSO BLOCKED BY: do your text; the card becomes supported when both land.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other in-scope paper card shares the construct: search the card data before deciding), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for a manual-definitions item. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --paper --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate, proposed deferral).
