# ruling-research-1: control of the source lost and regained before a "for as long as you control ~" ability resolves

Research item, effort max, with an independent investigator subagent and a separate adversarial skeptic subagent (as in ruling-conflicts-1). The user (Nov 19), on the provisional entry `control-lost-before-a-for-as-long-as-you-control-effect-begins` (docs/interpretations/triggers-durations.md): "you should have a worker look into this more deeply, as this affects a lot of cards and is a common case. There should be a fairly standard resolution for things like this."

**The question.** A source's ability creates an effect lasting "for as long as you control ~" (Tidebinder Mage, Dungeon Geists, Icefall Regent, Olivia Voldaren, Willbreaker, Kellogg, Orcish Squatters, The Wretched, Old Man of the Sea, Rubinia Soulsinger, Seasinger, Willow Satyr ...). Control of the source is lost while the ability is on the stack and regained before it resolves. Does the effect begin (the duration holds when the effect would first apply) or do nothing (the duration "ended before the moment the effect would first be applied", CR 611.2b)? Also cover the plain case (control lost and not regained), the tapped-twin ("and ~ remains tapped": untapped and tapped again, Willow Satyr's ruling), the source leaving and returning (a new object, CR 400.7), phasing out and in, and a "for as long as" about another object.

**Research, comprehensively:**
1. The CR: read 611.2b in full and every rule that bears on durations, control and "you control" (611.2, 611.2a–c, 108.4, 109.4–5, 110.2, 400.7, 603.10, 608.2h, 613.x, 702.26 phasing, 800.4a), and any example in the CR text. Check the CR history for 611.2b's wording (when "and doesn't begin again during that spell or ability's resolution" was added and with what release notes): archived CR texts, e.g. https://github.com/pit142857/mtg-cr.
2. Every ruling in data/rulings.jsonl.gz on "for as long as you control" and "remains tapped" (with dates), and every card with these durations (data/oracle_cards.jsonl.gz).
3. The web: search for judge answers and discussions of this exact case (control lost and regained, or untapped and retapped, before resolution): release notes and update bulletins (magic.wizards.com), Judge Academy / judge blog articles, judge Q&A archives, MTG Salvation rules Q&A, r/mtgrules, the Magic Judges' rules forums, Comprehensive Rules commentary (e.g. Yawgmoth's Thesis, Ancestral Recall / Cranial Insertion articles on Willow Satyr, Old Man of the Sea, Rubinia, Seasinger, Tidebinder Mage, Olivia). Use the WebSearch and WebFetch tools. If a host is blocked by the network policy, list which ones and go on with the others (don't try to get around the block); the coordinator tells the user.
4. Look for a general principle in those sources ("the duration is checked at the moment the effect would begin" vs. "a duration that has ended can't restart"), and whether Wizards has answered the regain case for any card.

**Outcome.** A source decides it → implement generally (the code paths in the entry: untap_choice.rs `ended_before_it_began`, `changed_while_on_stack`, resolve.rs, control_change.rs) with cr!/ruling! tests and a decided register entry citing it. Nothing decides it → the best-supported reading, with the entry kept PROVISIONAL and rewritten to quote every source verbatim with dates and URLs, and a short summary for the user of what the web sources say (quote them, with links). The skeptic's verdict goes in the report. Follow worker.md's one-item-per-session rule and notify the coordinator when it lands.

STATUS (implemented, reviewed Nov 20 (see "Review" below); the main register entry decided by the Seasinger ruling (the coordinator, Nov 20, following decision-audit-1), the four judgment-call entries provisional for the user):
- **Research.** The investigator's and the adversarial skeptic's write-ups are kept in docs/agents/research/ruling-research-1-investigator.md and ruling-research-1-skeptic.md. Skeptic's verdict: "UPHELD WITH CORRECTIONS". A WotC ruling decides the main case: Seasinger (2007-09-16), "Whether you control Seasinger is checked continually, starting when the ability is activated. If, before the ability resolves, there's any point at which you don't control Seasinger, the ability has no effect — even if you control Seasinger again by the time the ability resolves. The same is true regarding whether Seasinger remains tapped." Every CR change since (2008 broadening, 2009 rewording, the 2024 "doesn't begin again during that spell or ability's resolution" clause) agrees with it, and no source says otherwise.
- **Standard resolution.** A "for as long as" duration of an ability's effect is a period: it starts as the ability becomes activated (after its costs are paid) or triggers (a copy: as it's put onto the stack), ends the first moment its condition is false, and doesn't begin again before the ability resolves; during the resolution, the state as the effect would begin decides (CR 611.2b).
- **Engine** (crates/mtg-engine/src/duration_windows.rs, one hook in `Game::exec` plus `control_change::plan_gain_control`): the check now covers every effect kind created with such a duration (play permissions, player effects, replacement effects, besides control changes, modifications and rule effects); the period of a triggered ability starts as it triggers (`EventInfo::turn_events_seen`), of a copy as it's put onto the stack (`StackInfo::window`, copy.rs); the source changing control either way, tapping or untapping, or phasing out or in ends it; changes during the ability's own resolution don't, and a duration restored during the resolution applies (611.2b's 2024 clause); "and" durations with a "~ remains tapped" part are checked (latent before: only restrictions); conditions about other objects (Old Man of the Sea's power clause, Garland's monarch, Eriette's Aura, Rootwater Matriarch's "enchanted") are checked at each recomputation while the ability waits: one that stops holding has ended, and one that didn't hold at first and holds while the ability waits never started (the review; it can still begin during the resolution); conditions the ability sets up itself (tapped, counters: Xolatoyac, Braided Net, Zygon Infiltrator) are checked as the effect begins.

### Review (Nov 20)

Adversarial review of the implementation (worker.md's reviewer brief). The research's conclusion and the engine's design stand; four problems fixed, and the register brought up to date.
- **Over-breadth: "until ~ leaves the battlefield" ended by phasing (fixed; part pre-existing).** The compiler read " until ~ leaves the battlefield" as `Duration::WhileSourceOnBattlefield` (the renderer's comment: "is the same duration"), so the new waiting check ended it when the source phased out and in while its ability waited (Gaea's Liege's land didn't become a Forest), and, before this item, an existing effect ended as the source phased out. Phasing out isn't leaving the battlefield (702.26d: "The phasing event doesn’t actually cause a permanent to change zones or control"), and the July 2009 Update Bulletin, which introduced today's 702.26f, sets the "for as long as" durations that "lose track" of a phased-out permanent against the rest: "But other continuous effects will happily continue, blithely unaware that the permanent they're hovering over has ceased to exist for a while." New `Duration::UntilSourceLeavesBattlefield` (ability.rs; compiled in oracle/effects.rs `duration_suffix`; ended only by leaving in layers.rs `effect_expired`; `remains` in duration_windows.rs `needs`; rendered "until ~ leaves the battlefield", and the old duration only "for as long as ~ remains on the battlefield", so the round trip tells them apart). Cards: Gaea's Liege, Graceful Antelope, Secret Invasion (no other card compiles "until ... leaves the battlefield" to the old duration; `roundtrip --check`: every card of docs/roundtrip-passing.txt still passes). Recorded in `phasing-out-ends-a-waiting-for-as-long-as-duration`; test `until_the_source_leaves_the_battlefield_isnt_ended_by_phasing`.
- **A condition about another object that didn't hold at first (c1, fixed).** The implementation checked such a condition only as the effect began once it had failed the first check, so a creature unenchanted as Rootwater Matriarch's ability was activated and enchanted in response was stolen. Rootwater's ruling ("Can target an unenchanted creature but has no effect on that creature.") read with the period, and Seasinger's ("If, before the ability resolves, there’s any point at which you don’t control Seasinger, the ability has no effect"), say otherwise; the engine can tell a condition that begins while the ability waits from one that begins during the resolution (what the implementer's case, Vislor Turlough's goad, needs). Now `Watch::NotYet`: holding at a later check while the ability waits ends it; still false as the ability begins to resolve, it's checked as the effect begins. Entry `duration-about-another-object-ends-while-the-ability-waits` rewritten; tests `rootwater_matriarch_needs_the_creature_enchanted_from_activation_to_resolution` and `a_condition_about_another_object_can_begin_during_the_resolution` (an Old Man of the Sea probe whose own resolution makes the power condition hold: it applies; made to hold by another effect while it waits: nothing).
- **A test without a positive control (fixed).** `great_synthesis_lost_and_regained_while_chapter_one_waits_keeps_the_maximum_hand_size` asserted only the maximum hand size of 7 after control was lost and regained, which a chapter I that did nothing would also pass; it now checks the untouched case too (no maximum hand size).
- **Register details (fixed).** The 2001 418.3d quote: the archive's file has an unreadable character (U+FFFD) where the entry shows its three dashes, now said; the copy entry quotes 707.10c ("Once the player has decided what the copy’s targets will be, the copy is put onto the stack with those targets.") and says that the engine first checks a copy's conditions about other objects as the copy is created, before new targets are chosen for it; the main entry says why a control change "either way" ends the duration (a change to the ability's controller means they didn't control the source just before: the engine records control changes only when the controller differs, `layers.rs`, so a change to the current controller can't happen).
- **The main entry decided (the coordinator's direction, Nov 20).** decision-audit-1 (e0d2878) found that the Seasinger ruling (2007-09-16), of a card in the entry's Affects, answers its question; the coordinator directed that `for-as-long-as-checked-continually-from-activation` be marked decided by that ruling, applied generally, and that Garland, Royal Kidnapper's ruling (2025-10-02) be quoted in it as support.
