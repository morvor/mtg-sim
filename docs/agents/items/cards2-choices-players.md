# cards2-choices-players: Choice and player grammar II: hidden choices, guesses and bidding, votes, per-player stored choices, multi-value choices and notes, choices while casting, object choices by several players, player phrases — 152 texts / 134 cards (129 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 152 unsupported ability texts on paper cards (78 grammar; 46 engine; 18 former one-off claims re-checked as shared constructs; 10 round-1 manual candidates re-checked as shared constructs), blocking 134 paper cards; 129 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item or wait for a round-1 item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards2-choices-players.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Choices the engine can't represent yet and the player phrases around them. Hidden information: "As ~ enters, secretly choose an opponent" with the cost "Reveal the player you chose", "each player secretly chooses a number. Then those numbers are revealed", "An opponent guesses whether ...", "Each player may bid life". Votes: secret council, votes for players and for up to one object, "Whenever players finish voting". RULES NOTES:
- CR 101.4, 101.4b: simultaneous choices are made in APNAP order, each player knowing the earlier choices (Archangel of Strife ruling: "The active player chooses war or peace first, followed by each other player in turn order. Each player will know what previous players chose"); 101.4a: cards chosen in a hidden zone stay face down. A secret choice is the exception: it isn't revealed until the effect says so (Mob Verdict rulings: "each player writes down their chosen option without showing it to anyone else ... until all players simultaneously reveal their votes"; players may lie about how they intend to vote).
- CR 607.2d: "choose a [value]" and "the chosen [value]" / "the last chosen [value]" are linked; the second refers only to choices made by the first. Archangel of Strife rulings: the bonus a player's creatures get depends only on the choice that player made; two Archangels with different choices give both bonuses; choices end when it leaves the battlefield. Emissary of Grudges rulings: without a chosen opponent the reveal cost can't be paid; each Emissary has its own choice; a new object chooses again.
- Votes: CR 701.38a-d (each player in turn order starting with the specified player; several votes are cast at the same time); Mob Verdict ruling: "Abilities that trigger 'whenever players finish voting' trigger once all players have voted or once all secret votes are revealed, but they won't go on the stack until the current spell or ability finishes resolving."
- Bidding: Illicit Auction ruling: "This is a life loss and not a life payment so you can bid more life than you have." Menacing Ogre ruling: the secret number is zero or a positive integer. Liar's Pendulum rulings: the opponent guesses first; you may reveal your hand; you draw only if you revealed and they were wrong.
- Choices while casting: CR 601.2b (additional costs are announced while casting), 118.8 (additional costs).
- Object choices: Sundering Titan rulings: untargeted; one land for each basic land type; a land with several basic land types can be chosen more than once; if the only land of a type is yours you must choose it; all are destroyed simultaneously. Tragic Arrogance rulings: none of the chosen permanents are targets; a permanent with several of the types can count as any one of them. Erithizon ruling: "Erithizon's controller controls the triggered ability, but their opponent chooses the target."
- Types: CR 205.3j (planeswalker types). Bitter Feud rulings: several Bitter Feuds with the same players multiply the damage; it applies to any damage, not only combat damage.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 2):
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts marked "also needs <round-1 item>" belong to a round-1 item that is still in progress for one construct and to this item for the other. Do your construct; if that item has landed when you finish, make the whole text work, otherwise list the text as waiting for it. A text can also contain a second construct that the probes didn't reach: fix it when it is small, otherwise list it with the item that owns that area.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other card shares the construct), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards-manual-defs-2. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (TestGame tests per family with real cards; hidden choices also tested through mtg-api observations to show the choice isn't visible to other players before it is revealed) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
