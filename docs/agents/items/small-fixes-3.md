# small-fixes-3: Helm of Obedience's mill-until, unquoted rulings, and "for each" tokens/counters as one event

Implementation item (effort high; review at max).

1. **Helm of Obedience's process ends on a shuffled-back card** (decision-audit-1, results in docs/agents/items/decision-audit-1.md). `kw/mill_until.rs` stops the whole process whenever a mill leaves the library no smaller, so a milled card that is shuffled back (Darksteel Colossus: "If Darksteel Colossus would be put into a graveyard from anywhere, reveal Darksteel Colossus and shuffle it into its owner's library instead."; also Blightsteel Colossus, Progenitus, Nexus of Fate, Legacy Weapon) ends it, where Helm's text goes on with the next card. Fix: the process continues card by card until X cards were put into the graveyard this way or the library is exhausted of cards not yet milled by it; keep Helm's ruling (Leyline of the Void: the whole library is exiled). The other half (Helm against Wheel of Sun and Moon, a mandatory loop that CR 104.4b makes a draw, where the engine ends the process after one card) is NOT for this item: register it in the entry `mill-until-stops-when-nothing-moves` as a known deviation pending the loops work (C4, which the user postponed), quoting 104.4b and the Arcbond (2014-11-24) and Faceless Butcher (2022-12-08) rulings, and add it to the loops bullet in finish-line-plan §5 "Revisit later". Tests: Colossus on top with X = 3; Leyline; Wheel of Sun and Moon pinned as the deviation.
2. **Unquoted rulings found by the audit** (its item 5): add each ruling, verbatim with date, to the Rules field of its entry (changeling-cda-only: Oddric, Lunar Marquis 2024-11-08; commander-identity-protection-player: Armory Automaton 2016-11-08, Cloak and Dagger and the four other 2008-04-01 Equipment, Ardenn 2020-11-10, Enslave 2021-03-19; copy-exception-ability-stacks: Progenitor Mimic 2013-04-15; linked-this-way-includes-copies: Strionic Resonator 2018-03-16 and the others the audit lists; opponents-choice-untargeted: Steam Augury 2013-09-15, the five Masteries 2021-04-16, Rohgahh of Kher Keep 2009-10-01; toymakers-trap-every-number-chosen: CR 118.12). Where the ruling settles the entry's question, say "decided by the ruling" in its Decision. Add the missing test the audit noted for commander-identity-protection-player (an Equipment's "you" when the creature's controller differs from the Equipment's).
3. **G32: tokens, counters and life "for each" as one event**, now that `copy-for-each-one-copy-event` is decided by the user (one instruction, one event). See docs/agents/items/ruling-conflicts-11.md around line 104: with Xorn, Brass's Bounty ("For each land you control, create a Treasure token.") makes N + 1 Treasures (one CreateToken with a count), but Peregrin Took with Endless Ranks of HYDRA ("For each opponent, you create a 2/1 ... token") makes a Food per opponent (compiled to Repeat), and Tempting Contract with Xorn adds one per opponent (ForEachPlayer). Make one "for each" instruction's tokens (and, by the same rule, counters put and life gained) one event for every compiled form, consistent with Wurmquake's (2023-02-04) and Tempting Contract's ("you create your Treasure tokens all at the same time") rulings; where a form genuinely is several events (each player a different "you", separate instructions), keep it and say why. Register: extend `copy-for-each-one-copy-event`'s reading by analogy in a new decided-by-analogy entry, or a provisional one if any ruling points the other way (check every ruling of the cards involved).
