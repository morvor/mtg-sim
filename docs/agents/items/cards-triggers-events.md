# cards-triggers-events: Trigger grammar I: zone-change, counter, player-action and state trigger conditions — 295 texts / 293 cards (228 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 295 unsupported ability texts (295 on paper cards) blocking 293 paper cards; 228 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards-triggers-events.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Parse trigger conditions as [subject phrase] [event verb] [qualifiers], where the subject is any object phrase (with "~ or another [filter]", "one or more [filter]", "another [filter] you control with toughness greater than its power"), the event is any zone change or game event the engine emits, and qualifiers include zones ("from anywhere", "from the battlefield", "to a player's hand"), controllers/owners ("under an opponent's control", "an opponent owns"), timing ("during your turn", "for the first time each turn", "while ~ is in your graveyard"), and causes ("a spell or ability an opponent controls destroys", "without dying", "without being played"). Families: batched "one or more" triggers (CR 603.2c), counters put/removed by a player ("whenever you put one or more +1/+1 counters on a creature you don't control", "when the twelfth hour counter is put on ~"), zone changes with qualifiers, enters/dies with conditions, player actions (sacrifice/discard/tap for mana/search/shuffle/cycle/scry), life and damage events, and state triggers ("when you control no permanents with phylactery counters on them", CR 603.8).

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards):
- "one or more [objects] [event]" batched triggers (CR 603.2c): 77. e.g. "Whenever one or more Scouts" [Vaan, Street Thief]; "Whenever you get one or more {E}" [Aether Revolt]; "Whenever one or more cards are put into a library from anywhere" [Dutiful Knowledge Seeker]
- counters put/removed ("whenever you put one or more +1/+1 counters on a creature", "when the twelfth hour counter is put"): 24. e.g. "Whenever there are four or more tide counters on ~" [Tidal Influence]; "Whenever another creature you control enters while ~ has a -N/-N counter on it" [Bristlebane Battler]; "When there are five or more plot counters on ~" [Deadly Designs]
- zone changes with qualifiers ("put into an opponent's graveyard from anywhere", "returned to a player's hand", "leaves the battlefield without dying", "is exiled"): 43. e.g. "When ~ enters from your hand" [Thousand-Faced Shadow]; "When a player has no cards in hand" [Veiled Crocodile]; "Whenever a spell or ability an opponent controls causes a land to be put into your graveyard from the battlefield" [Sacred Ground]
- enters with conditions ("enters during your turn", "enters transformed", "or another [filter] with [stat] enters"): 24. e.g. "When ~ enters attached to a creature" [Infinite Reflection]; "Whenever a creature enters this turn" [Beck // Call]; "Whenever a permanent you control transforms or a permanent you control enters transformed" [Corruption of Towashi]
- dies with qualifiers ("dies while ~ is in your graveyard", "creature dealt damage this turn by a Spider dies"): 16. e.g. "When ~ dies or is put into exile" [Kaya the Inexorable]; "When target creature dies this turn" [Graceful Reprieve]; "Whenever another creature dies during your turn" [Vogar, Necropolis Tyrant]
- player actions ("sacrifice another nontoken permanent during your turn", "discards", "taps a Mountain", "searches", "shuffles"): 33. e.g. "you may untap it." [Legolas, Counter of Kills]; "Whenever a player taps a Mountain" [Keeper of Progenitus]; "Whenever enchanted player draws a card" [Curse of Fool's Wisdom]
- life / damage ("one or more opponents lose life", "is dealt damage for the first time each turn"): 20. e.g. "Whenever you pay life" [Font of Agonies]; "When you have N or less life" [Opal Avenger]; "~ deals damage equal to that creature's toughness to the creature's controller." [Creature Bond]
- state triggers ("when you control no ...", "when an opponent controls a creature with power 4 or greater", "when you have N or more life"): 2. e.g. "When you control seven or more Thrulls" [Endrek Sahr, Master Breeder]; "When an opponent controls a creature with power N or greater" [Hidden Predators]
- "for the first time each turn" / "during your turn" qualifiers: 2. e.g. "Whenever you commit a crime during your turn" [Overzealous Muscle]; "Whenever you investigate for the first time each turn" [Erdwal Illuminator]
- other (mixed; read the texts): 54. e.g. "When there are N" [The Millennium Calendar]; "When ~ has flying" [Student of Elements // Tobita, Master of Winds]; "When a player other than ~'s owner controls it" [Bronze Bombshell]

APPROACH: 1. Restructure `triggers::core_trigger_condition` around a subject parser + event table + qualifier suffixes instead of whole-phrase matches; keep the existing TriggerCond variants as targets and use `TriggerCond::Where`/`Batched`/`FirstTimeEachTurn`/`AnyOf` combinators for qualifiers.
2. Referents: every trigger must say what "it", "that creature", "that card", "that player" mean (TriggerObject/TriggerLki/TriggerOtherObject/TriggerPlayer); several failures are bodies like "exile that creature" or "return that card" whose referent the trigger condition doesn't expose — fix in the trigger parser, not the body.
3. Look-back (CR 603.10a) for leaves-the-battlefield/"is put into a graveyard" triggers and "while ~ is in your graveyard" zones (CR 113.6).
4. State triggers (CR 603.8): "When/Whenever [state]" with the existing `TriggerCond::State` and the condition grammar.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS:
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (an Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- A text can contain a second failing construct owned by another item. Fix it when it is small; otherwise leave it and list it under remaining gaps with the owning item.
- One-offs. If a text's construct is genuinely unique (no other card shares it) and compiling it would be hacky, don't force it: once cards-manual-defs has landed, give it a hand-written definition in src/cards/ with a test; before that, list it as a manual candidate.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` and the baseline filters above. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
