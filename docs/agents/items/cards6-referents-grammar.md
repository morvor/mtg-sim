# cards6-referents-grammar: Referent and value grammar VII: "count the number of X" with "the number" as its referent and two continuous effects under one duration, an unattach cost's object as a divided damage amount, a created token's given name as its self-reference, and gain control of a spell with a target filter plus random new targets for a spell and its copy — 4 texts / 4 cards (4 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 4 unsupported ability texts on 4 paper cards (Chaos Moon, Captain America, First Avenger, Toggo, Goblin Weaponsmith, Chef's Kiss); each is its card's only unsupported text, so all 4 cards become fully supported when this item is done. All four were in cards5-referents-statics (landed; its families `values`, `cost-referents`, `copies`, `spell-targets`), which left them; most were in round-4 and round-3 items before. The engine support exists for three of them (the drills compile each piece alone); Chef's Kiss needs a random reselection of targets.

GOAL: Compile "At the beginning of each upkeep, count the number of permanents. If the number is odd, until end of turn, red creatures get +N/+N and whenever a player taps a Mountain for mana, that player adds an additional {R}. If the number is even, ... if a player taps a Mountain for mana, that Mountain produces colorless mana instead of any other type." [Chaos Moon]; "{N}, Unattach an Equipment from ~: He deals damage equal to that Equipment's mana value divided as you choose among one, two, or three targets." [Captain America, First Avenger]; "create a colorless Equipment artifact token named Rock with "Equipped creature has '{N}, {T}, Sacrifice Rock: This creature deals N damage to any target'" and equip {N}." [Toggo, Goblin Weaponsmith]; "Gain control of target spell that targets only a single permanent or player. Copy it, then reselect the targets at random for the spell and the copy. The new targets can't be you or a permanent you control." [Chef's Kiss].

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards; the failing piece; the engine need):
- counted values (family `values`): 1. "count the number of permanents. If the number is odd, ..." [Chaos Moon]
  Failing piece: the parity condition with "the number of permanents" compiles and each of the three effects compiles alone under the upkeep trigger; the step "count the number of X" followed by "If the number is odd/even" fails with any body (Touch of the Eternal, Invincible Hymn and Rumbling Ruin compile "count the number of X" followed by "that number"); two continuous effects joined by "and" under one "until end of turn" fail; "that Mountain" for the tapped land fails where "it" compiles.
  Engine need: none expected (a count kept as the resolution's value, CR 608.2h; parity; Pale Moon's mana-type replacement and the additional-mana trigger for the turn).
- cost referents in divided damage (family `cost-referents`): 1. [Captain America, First Avenger]
  Failing piece: the amount "that Equipment's mana value" (from the unattach cost) compiles for one target and fails divided; a sacrifice cost's object compiles divided ("the sacrificed artifact's mana value").
  Engine need: none expected; the engine divides an amount set by a cost already (the sacrifice forms). Record the reading in the interpretation register with `interp!` in the test: CR 601.2d has the division announced before costs are paid (601.2h); the amount is known only after the Equipment is chosen for the cost (carried over from rounds 4 and 5; reuse the entry the sacrifice forms use if one exists).
- token names (family `token-names`): 1. [Toggo, Goblin Weaponsmith]
  Failing piece: "Sacrifice Rock" inside the token's (granted) ability; "Sacrifice this artifact"/"this token" compile.
  Engine need: none: a compiler normalization. A created token's given name, inside the quoted abilities the creating effect gives it (also nested grants), refers to that token (CR 201.5, 201.5a, 111.4), as a card's own name does. Only Toggo has this wording among in-scope cards today (search in the list's note), but the fix is a rule of name normalization: compile it.
- spell targets (family `spell-targets`): 1. [Chef's Kiss]
  Failing piece: "Gain control of target spell" compiles without a filter; "that targets only a single permanent or player" compiles on counter and copy targets, not on the gain-control target. Then "Copy it, then reselect the targets at random for the spell and the copy" (random new targets for a spell fail in every form; random target choice exists for abilities: "any target chosen at random", Goblin Test Pilot, Knight Rampager) and "The new targets can't be you or a permanent you control".
  Engine need: choosing new targets at random among the legal targets for a spell and for its copy (CR 115.7, 115.7d-e, 707.10c), with an exclusion set (you and your permanents); rulings: if no legal target other than the excluded ones can be selected, the targets stay unchanged; each instance of "target" is reselected separately; copying and reselecting isn't casting.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 6):
- These texts are the last of the long tail: every one was in at least one landed item (column previous_item; most in two to five) and is still unsupported after it. The plan re-probed each on Nov 4 (column failing_piece: the first failing sentence) and drilled it (column note: "drilled: X compiles; Y fails", with the drill ids of round6/drills.tsv, `C6-...`, and the second and third failing pieces). Re-probe before starting (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; write the card's own name as ~ in probe texts; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Each item is one engine feature or one grammar area, not a family of wordings: build the engine part first and test it with a custom card (`CardDef::custom`) before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several wordings, including the drills' neutral-card forms.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). Add TestGame tests with the real card asserting the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for the card's Scryfall rulings exercised: these cards' rulings are all UNCOVERED today). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). The plan searched every one-off-looking construct over all in-scope paper cards (paper and legal or restricted in a format; the searches are in each note); the genuine one-offs are in cards6-manual-defs. If a text here turns out to need a construct no other card has and the engine can't express it as grammar, don't force a pattern: list it in your report as a manual candidate. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) may be edited by other items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files (`oracle/patterns/<topic>_6.rs`), and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` (base on Nov 4: 32,059 / 32,111 paper cards), `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards, and `docs/agents/round6/check_plan.py`, which rebuilds the live list from the release binary and lists the snapshot texts supported since. Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when every text of the item compiles with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per card), or, for a text that can't, it is listed in the report with the reason (engine gap with what is missing, belongs to item X, manual candidate with the search that makes it one, proposed deferral). With so few texts per item, "about 85%" means all but at most one.
