Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: The remaining library, hand and graveyard flows. Digs: per-type and per-color selections ("For each card type, you may exile a card of that type from among them"), "all of them with that name", group constraints ("up to two creature cards with total mana value 4 or less"), the rest after a cast from a dig ("the exiled cards that weren't cast this way"), each-player digs and another player choosing. Zone moves that enter attached ("it enters attached to that creature", "return it to the battlefield attached to you"), from either of two zones, under an opponent's control, and per-player piles (with gap-residual-1 R6).

RULES NOTES:
- Searching: CR 701.23a/b (a search for cards with a stated quality may fail to find them even if present; Gifts Ungiven ruling: "You can choose to find fewer than four cards ... If you find one or two cards, your opponent must choose for them to be put into your graveyard"); Lobotomy rulings: the chosen card and all others with that name in hand, library and graveyard are exiled; copies in public zones must be found, finding them in hidden zones is optional. Doubling Chant rulings: search for the creature's current name (a Clone copying something uses the copied name); with no creatures you may still search and must shuffle; the found cards must be creature cards.
- Reveal-until and digs: cards revealed stay in the library until moved; "the rest" are exactly the cards revealed or exiled this way that the instruction didn't take (CR 608.2c reading of "the rest"). Muse Vortex ruling: if you don't cast a spell, all exiled instant and sorcery cards go to your hand.
- Library and graveyard order: CR 401.2 (a library is a face-down pile; nobody may change its order unless instructed), 404.2 (a graveyard is a face-up pile whose order normally can't change: "reorder your graveyard" is an instruction that allows it). Unexpectedly Absent rulings: X = 0 puts it on top; with fewer than X cards it goes to the bottom.
- Hidden zones and exile: CR 406.3 (face-down exiled cards can't be examined except as allowed), 701.24a (shuffle).
- Attach on entry: CR 303.4f (an Aura put onto the battlefield other than by resolving: its controller chooses what it enchants if the effect doesn't say), 303.4g (no legal object: it stays where it is), 301.5c (Equipment restrictions). Grifter's Blade rulings: it must enter attached to a creature you control if possible, otherwise it enters unattached. Sovereigns of Lost Alara rulings: the Aura must be able to enchant the attacking creature as it currently exists (enchant ability and protection), the creature isn't targeted (shroud doesn't stop it), and if the creature has left the battlefield no Aura can be put onto the battlefield.
- Control: CR 110.2a (a player told to put an object onto the battlefield controls it unless the effect says otherwise). Warp World rulings: each player counts the permanents they own (tokens count, but only cards are revealed), artifact, creature and land cards enter at the same time, then enchantments, and triggers wait until resolution is complete.
- Piles: CR 700.3 (gap-residual-1 R6 extends piles.rs).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 2):
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts marked "also needs <round-1 item>" belong to a round-1 item that is still in progress for one construct and to this item for the other. Do your construct; if that item has landed when you finish, make the whole text work, otherwise list the text as waiting for it. A text can also contain a second construct that the probes didn't reach: fix it when it is small, otherwise list it with the item that owns that area.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other card shares the construct), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards-manual-defs-2. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (TestGame tests per family with real cards; for digs, assert where every revealed card ends up) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
