# small-fixes-1: small gaps found by the Nov 19 rulings batches

Implementation item (effort high; review at max). Each part is small and independent; land them together.

1. **Unregistered judgment call (rulings-P167):** split cards and Adventures foretold through a "foretell cost equal to its mana cost reduced by {2}" grant (Dream Devourer) use the mana cost of the half/Adventure cast, by analogy with Dream Devourer's ruling (2021-02-05) "If you foretell a modal double-faced card, the foretell cost will be based on the mana cost of the face you cast from exile." and CR 709.3b "While on the stack, only the characteristics of the half being cast exist." (and the Adventure rules, 715). Add a register entry (provisional unless a source decides it; quote every source verbatim with dates) and pin it with interp! in a test for a split card and an Adventure (kw/foretell.rs, Ethereal Valkyrie's per-face mark).
2. **Heroes' Podium (rulings-P168):** its ruling (2014-02-01) "The bonus given by Heroes' Podium counts only legendary creatures. It won't count itself unless some other effect causes it to be a creature in addition to being a land (in which case it will also get the bonus)." contradicts the Oracle text (Legendary Artifact; no land) with no dated change. Apply CLAUDE.md's `ruling-contradicting-oracle-gives-way`: exempt the land clause only, cite the rest with the existing test heroes_podium_counts_itself_only_as_a_creature, and move its docs/RULING_DISCREPANCIES.md entry out of "Not yet investigated" to the right category (check the Born of the Gods release notes text if reachable).
3. **Zevlor, Elturel Exile (rulings-P170 gap):** "for each other opponent, ... copy that spell, and the copy targets the chosen player or permanent" (custom effect in crates/mtg-engine/src/kw/choices_5_filters.rs) puts the copies on the stack without letting the controller order them (CR 405.3, as fixed for Feather, Radiant Arbiter via copy::order_copies in resolve.rs). Route Zevlor's copies through copy::order_copies, with a regression test; survey other custom effects that put several copies on the stack at once and fix the same way.
4. **Six permanent types hard-coded in 7 places (user-decisions-nov19b gap):** make them all use CardType::is_permanent_type (or one shared const), no behavior change; keep the tests green.

Out of scope (separate later items): the shuffling player ≠ library owner (Green Sun's Zenith with Knowledge Pool, ~75 match sites); Word of Command's choice of lands to tap (no mana-payment decision).
