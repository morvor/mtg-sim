# cards5-costs-sets: Cost and set grammar: untap and exile costs over sets with totals, costs that pay a chosen card's mana cost, choices made as additional costs and their referents, repeatable additional costs that add targets, and the results of optional set actions — 14 texts / 11 cards (10 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 14 unsupported ability texts on paper cards, blocking 11 paper cards; 10 become fully supported when this item alone is done (Baron Helmut Zemo also needs cards5-referents-statics' limited cast from among copies). Every text was in a landed round-4 item and is still unsupported after it: 13 were left by cards4-costs-mana-replacements (landed at 15183001; its families `cost-sets` and `additional-costs`) and Tetravus by cards4-paper-effects (landed at ea388c71; its reported blocker, "~ can't be enchanted", compiles since cards4-paper-combat-statics landed). The re-plan ("Update: cards4-paper-effects and cards4-costs-mana-replacements landed" in card-support-plan-5.md) re-probed every text (round3/autoprobe.py) and drilled it to the failing words on the real card and on neutral cards (round5/drills.tsv, ids `C5-W-...`). The full list ships as items/cards5-costs-sets.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece, family, previous item, note). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Compile costs over sets and the costs' links ("Untap a tapped land an opponent controls", "Exile one or more other artifacts you control with total mana value X" with X read by the target, "with fifteen or more black mana symbols among their mana costs", "Sacrifice ~ and any number of creatures you control" and "then exile ~ and those creature cards", "Exile a creature card from your graveyard and pay its mana cost"); choices made as additional costs ("choose a creature you control or a warped creature card you own in exile" and "the chosen creature or card", "you may choose a creature type and behold two creatures of that type" and "the revealed card is the chosen type", "If ~'s additional cost was paid and ..., put that card onto the battlefield instead of putting it into your hand"); two repeatable optional additional costs ("you may pay {1}{R} and/or {1}{G} any number of times") with "For each additional {1}{R} you paid, destroy another target artifact"; and results of optional or each-player set actions ("you may exile any number of cards from your graveyard with four or more card types among them. If you do, put a permanent card from among them ...", "that many" after "you may remove any number of +1/+1 counters from ~", "They each have flying and ...", "you may play cards you own exiled this way" after each player exiles their hand).

  Failing piece: drills: "Untap a tapped land you control" and "Tap an untapped land an opponent controls" compile as costs, the untap cost over an opponent's land fails; "Exile X other artifacts you control" compiles, "with total mana value X" fails (a fixed "15 or greater" compiles); a total of one color's mana symbols fails; "Sacrifice ~ and any number of other creatures" compiles (Emrakul's Evangel), without "other" it fails, and the sacrificed cards as "those creature cards" fail; "and pay its mana cost" (or "and pay {2}") fails as part of one cost.
  Engine need: a cost that untaps a permanent another player controls (no target; can't be activated without one, ruling), X announced (CR 107.3a) and matched exactly by a cost set's total, mana-symbol counts over a cost set (CR 107.4a, 107.4e), the sacrificed creatures followed to the graveyard (CR 400.7), a mana cost taken from the card chosen for the same cost (not a cast: no reductions, X is 0, ruling). - choices made as additional costs (family `additional-cost-choices`): 6. e.g. "As an additional cost to cast ~, choose a creature you control or a warped creature card you own in exile." and "~ deals damage equal to the power of the chosen creature or card to target creature." [Close Encounter]; "As an additional cost to cast ~, you may choose a creature type and behold two creatures of that type." and "If ~'s additional cost was paid and the revealed card is the chosen type, put that card onto the battlefield instead of putting it into your hand."   Failing piece: drills: an additional cost "choose a creature you control" fails even with "Draw a card." (Monstrous Emergence's "choose ... or reveal ..." compiles); "choose a creature type and behold ... of that type" fails (plain behold compiles); after a search, "If <condition>, put that card onto the battlefield instead of putting it into your hand" fails with any condition (Caravan Vigil's and Back for Seconds' forms compile), and "the revealed card is the chosen type" fails; two repeatable costs with "and/or" fail (one compiles, Taste of Paradise; two multikickers compile) and "For each additional {1}{R} you paid," fails as an opener.
  Engine need: a choice of an object (on the battlefield or a warped card in exile, CR 702.185) or of a creature type recorded as part of the spell's costs (CR 601.2b), read by the effect with last known information (ruling); two independent repeat counts (CR 601.2f) that set the number of targets of later target groups (CR 601.2c; the same artifact can't be targeted twice, ruling).
- results of optional and each-player set actions (family `set-action-results`): 3. e.g. "you may exile any number of cards from your graveyard with four or more card types among them. If seven or more {E} was paid this way, you may play cards you own exiled this way until the end of your next turn." [Wheel of Potential]
  Failing piece: drills: the set quality compiles in Nethergoyf's cost but fails in an effect, and "from among them" fails after an optional exile ("a permanent card exiled this way" compiles); "that many" fails after removing counters from ~ (it compiles after removing them "from among creatures you control", and "you gain that much life" compiles) and "They each have" fails where "They have" compiles; "cards exiled this way" fails after "Each player exiles their hand" (it compiles after "Exile your hand" and after each player exiles the top card of their library).
  Engine need: none beyond the Builder's referents for these sets (the optional action's chosen set and count, every player's exiled hand with an owner filter); the set qualities reuse cards4-costs-mana-replacements' cost aggregates.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 5):
- These texts are the long tail: every one was in at least one landed item (column previous_item) and is still unsupported after it. The plan re-probed each (column failing_piece: the first failing sentence, cost, trigger or line) and drilled it (column note: "drilled: X compiles; Y fails", with the drill ids of round5/drills.tsv, and the second failing piece where there is one). Re-probe before starting a family (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; write the card's own name as ~ in probe texts; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text).
- Where a text fails because the engine can't do it (the engine need in each family), build the engine part first and test it with a custom card before the grammar.
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised). Judgment calls go in the interpretation register (docs/interpretations/, `interp!`).
- Running while this item was planned: cards5-choices-players, cards5-referents-statics and cards5-game-rules-dice (their lists are separate; no card mixes their texts with this item's); cards5-manual-defs has hand-written cards pending. Don't build another item's construct here; if a text turns out to need one, list it in your report.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). The plan searched every one-off-looking construct over all in-scope paper cards (legal or restricted in a format); the genuine one-offs of these two round-4 items (Lodestone Bauble, North Star) are in cards5-manual-defs. If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (search the card data before deciding), don't force a pattern: list it in your report. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`mtg-tools unsupported --paper --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards; `docs/agents/round5/check_plan.py --live` lists the snapshot texts supported since. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
