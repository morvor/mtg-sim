# decision-audit-1: check every provisional and non-user-decided register entry against all rulings of its cards

Audit item, effort max, with an independent auditor subagent and a separate adversarial checker subagent. Why: the user found that the coordinator's relay of `copy-of-a-card-that-left-is-made-where-it-was` said no ruling covered a case that a God-Eternal Kefnet ruling (2019-05-03) does cover ("If the card leaves your hand before Kefnet's triggered ability resolves, you'll copy it using its last known information."), and asked how it was missed. The user is concerned about the integrity of the project; this audit checks the other entries for the same kind of miss.

For every register entry (docs/interpretations/*.md) that is (a) provisional, or (b) decided but NOT "Decided by the user" (decided by the coordinator or a worker; about 57):
1. Take every card in its Affects (and the cards its Question and tests name). Pull EVERY ruling of those cards from data/rulings.jsonl.gz (all dates), not only the ones the entry quotes.
2. Decide, per entry: does any ruling (or CR rule) answer the entry's question directly? Does any contradict its Decision? Is any ruling the entry quotes misquoted, misdated or quoted out of context? Does the entry claim "no ruling/rule addresses this" when one does?
3. Also check the entry's Status claim: a provisional entry that a ruling settles should be decided "by the ruling"; a decided entry that rests on a ruling that doesn't actually say it should be flagged.
Fix plain errors (misquotes, dates, a settled entry wrongly provisional) in place, with a note in the entry. Anything that would change engine behavior, or a Decision a ruling contradicts, goes in the report for the coordinator and the user with every source quoted verbatim with dates, and is not changed without the user.

Also check the coordinator's presentation path: list any entry whose Rules field quotes a ruling that answers its own Question (as Kefnet's did) — these are the cases a summary can most easily misstate.

Report: per entry, OK / fixed (what) / for the user (what, with quotes). `cargo test -p mtg-data` passes; yield row; follow worker.md's one-item-per-session rule and notify the coordinator.

## Results (Nov 20)

Two independent auditors (A: characteristics-layers, combat, copy, decisions-policies, dice-coins, game-end, players-multiplayer; B: replacement-prevention, stack-casting-costs, triggers-durations, zones-objects) audited the 79 listed entries (all provisional entries and every decided entry not decided by the user, plus `growing-loops-run-to-the-limit` and `conspiracy-draft-needs-three-players`), and the two frozen entries in their files that weren't listed (`subtype-gained-only-if-it-corresponds-as-the-effect-begins`, `target-player-reference-rechecked`); an adversarial checker verified every fix against the data, re-checked every "for the user" claim (scratch tests for the engine claims) and re-audited 18 of the entries marked OK (all the provisional ones, those claiming "no ruling"/"silent", and those resting on a ruling; spot checks of 4 more), and checked every quoted CR fragment and ruling of the 81 entries by script. Frozen entries (other sessions were editing them) were reported on, not edited.

**Fixed (16 entries, 15 by the auditors and 1 by the checker, and one test comment; no Decision or engine behavior changed; every new quote checked character by character against the data):** misquotes corrected in `commander-identity-protection-player` (702.16a), `default-cost-helpers` (crew, 702.122a), `unbounded-number-ceiling` (Menacing Ogre's 2004-10-04 ruling; the negative-number sentence is 107.1b's), `deck-order-ids-before-shuffle` (103.3's opening), `draft-three-colors-default` (905.2b), `growing-loops-run-to-the-limit` (Charismatic Conqueror's token has lifelink, not flying; the same misquote in the comment of `a_loop_that_grows_the_game_state_runs_until_the_action_limit`), `per-source-prevention-division` (616.1 typography), `entering-counter-modifiers-after-enters-with` (Master Biomancer's Oracle text), `more-loyalty-activations-add-up` (The Chain Veil was quoted as "once more"; the rulings settling "rather than only once" added: Urza Assembles the Titans, 2022-09-09, Oath of Teferi, 2018-04-27, Kaito, Dancing Shadow, 2023-02-04), `cost-paid-reflexive-trigger` (the engine's casualty-template rewording had been presented as casualty's own text), `one-spell-spend-permission` (a stray quotation mark in 601.2f), `anchor-target-chosen-first` and `guff-players-who-controlled` (608.2b quoted in older CR wording), `choose-for-each-player-distinct-objects` (101.4b miscited for 101.4c), `face-down-instant-or-sorcery-stays-face-down` (a missing separator); false claims corrected in `backdraft-controller-chooses-the-spell` ("the CR is silent": 608.2c–d answer it).

**For the user (each source verbatim; the entries not frozen carry an audit note):**
1. `mill-until-stops-when-nothing-moves` (Helm of Obedience), two engine findings. (a) Engine bug: `kw/mill_until.rs` stops the process when a mill leaves the library no smaller, so a milled card shuffled back into the library ends it (Darksteel Colossus: "If Darksteel Colossus would be put into a graveyard from anywhere, reveal Darksteel Colossus and shuffle it into its owner's library instead."; also Blightsteel Colossus, Progenitus, Nexus of Fate, Legacy Weapon), where the card's text, "Target opponent mills a card, then repeats this process until a creature card or X cards have been put into their graveyard this way, whichever comes first.", has it go on with the next card. A scratch test (X = 3, Darksteel Colossus on top of a 35-card library) put no card into the graveyard. (b) Deviation: the Decision calls the Wheel of Sun and Moon case ("If a card would be put into enchanted player's graveyard from anywhere, instead that card is revealed and put on the bottom of that player's library.") "a loop the rules leave open", but CR 104.4b says "If a game that’s not using the limited range of influence option (including a two-player game) somehow enters a “loop” of mandatory actions, repeating a sequence of events with no way to stop, the game is a draw. ..." (rulings applying it: Arcbond, 2014-11-24; Faceless Butcher, 2022-12-08: "If no one can stop the loop, the game will be a draw."); the engine ends the process after one card and the game goes on. The empty-library stop agrees with Helm's ruling (2008-10-01: "... Your opponent’s entire library will be exiled, even if X is 1.").
2. `simultaneous-entries-ordered-by-controller`: (a) a judgment call presented as decided: an effect whose source another entering permanent's effect makes leave the battlefield still applies (Rhythm of the Wild, 2019-05-03: "If Rhythm of the Wild leaves the battlefield at the same time that a nontoken creature enters the battlefield (most likely because that creature has a replacement effect, such as that of Rescuer Sphinx), that creature still gets a +1/+1 counter or haste."), but one whose source stays and loses its abilities (Ixidron turning Grumgully, the Generous face down) doesn't (616.1f); no ruling covers the second case. (b) Engine inconsistency with the entry's own Decision: `Game::replace_rec` adds back all of an entering permanent's effects determined at the proposal once any permanent then on the battlefield has left it, so with Ixidron, Rescuer Sphinx and Grizzly Bears entering together (Grumgully and Ornithopter on the battlefield, the default order), the Sphinx returning the face-down Ornithopter gives the Bears the face-down Grumgully's counter (1), while without the Sphinx they get none (0). Scratch tests also showed a lone Rescuer Sphinx returning Grumgully gets Grumgully's counter in either order (2 counters; no test pins it).
3. `control-lost-before-a-for-as-long-as-you-control-effect-begins` (frozen; ruling-research-1): the same kind of miss as Kefnet's. A ruling of a card in the entry's own Affects answers its Question, in the Decision's direction, and the entry doesn't quote it: Seasinger (2007-09-16): "Whether you control Seasinger is checked continually, starting when the ability is activated. If, before the ability resolves, there’s any point at which you don’t control Seasinger, the ability has no effect — even if you control Seasinger again by the time the ability resolves. The same is true regarding whether Seasinger remains tapped." The engine already does this (`willow_satyr_untapped_or_stolen_before_resolution_gains_nothing`, `tidebinder_mage_control_lost_and_regained_before_its_trigger_resolves_only_taps`, `olivia_voldaren_control_lost_and_regained_before_resolution_gains_nothing`); by the item's rule the entry is "decided by the ruling". An analog for a duration about another condition: Garland, Royal Kidnapper (2025-10-02): "While Garland's second ability is on the stack, if that opponent stops being the monarch before it resolves, you won't gain control of the target creature at all."
4. `copy-of-a-card-that-left-is-made-where-it-was` (frozen; user-decisions-nov20): its own two Kefnet rulings (2019-05-03) settle it, as the user said; its Why also paraphrases 707.12 as "the zone the object is in" (the rule: "the copy is created in the same zone the object is in").
5. Decided entries whose answer a ruling gives that the entry didn't quote (no conflict; a relay must not say "no ruling covers this"): `changeling-cda-only`: Oddric, Lunar Marquis (2024-11-08, a playtest card legal in no format): "Gaining changeling or devoid works exactly how you would expect it to unless you’ve spent too much time looking at the Magic rules." (the same day: "For many playtest cards, you’ll need to make a generous assumption that basic game rules would be updated to allow them to work. ..."): it implies the rules' answer is the Decision's and asks that card's players to play it as Alternative A; `commander-identity-protection-player`: Armory Automaton (2016-11-08): "... However, if the Equipment says that the equipped creature has an ability, the word "you" in that ability refers to you, the controller of the creature." and Cloak and Dagger and four other Equipment (2008-04-01): "... "you" in any abilities granted to the creature refers to that player." agree with the Decision, while Ardenn, Intrepid Archaeologist (2020-11-10): "Auras and Equipment that say "you" still refer to you, as you still control them." and Enslave (2021-03-19) don't make the distinction; no test pins different controllers (a scratch test showed the engine follows the Decision); `copy-exception-ability-stacks`: Progenitor Mimic (2013-04-15): "... If another Progenitor Mimic enters the battlefield as a copy of that creature, it will be a Runeclaw Bear with two instances of the triggered ability."; `linked-this-way-includes-copies`: Strionic Resonator (2018-03-16): "If a triggered ability is linked to a second ability, copies of that triggered ability are also linked to that second ability. ..." (and the same in general form on 11 more cards); `opponents-choice-untargeted`: Steam Augury (2013-09-15): "Steam Augury doesn't target any opponent. In a multiplayer game, you choose the opponent as the spell resolves." (and the Masteries, 2021-04-16; Rohgahh of Kher Keep, 2009-10-01); `toymakers-trap-every-number-chosen`: CR 118.12 and its Standstill example.
6. Minor: `choose-for-each-player-distinct-objects`: the engine fixes the order of one player's choices, which 101.4c leaves to the player ("If no order is specified, the player chooses the order."; no outcome differs); `one-spell-spend-permission`: North Star doesn't cover Phyrexian symbols (disclosed), though 107.4f ("A Phyrexian mana symbol represents a cost that can be paid either with one mana of its color or by paying 2 life.") with 609.4b would let any mana pay them: a deviation inside an interpretation entry; `unrevealed-top-card-grants-no-abilities` (frozen): a neighbouring case it doesn't cover, a condition on the top card's characteristics while the new top card is unrevealed (Vampire Nocturnus: "As long as the top card of your library is black, ..."), has no ruling either.

**Presentation risk (the Rules field, or for `cast-order-as-chosen` the Question, quotes a ruling that answers its own Question):** high: `copy-of-a-card-that-left-is-made-where-it-was` (Kefnet), `base-pt-set-on-a-noncreature-applies-once-it-is-a-creature` (Shadow Puppeteers' "it won't have base power and toughness 4/4" reads like an answer but covers only the time it isn't a creature), `control-lost-before-a-for-as-long-as-you-control-effect-begins` (Willow Satyr's ruling quoted, Seasinger's, which answers it, not); low (the entry says it follows the ruling): `subtype-gained-only-if-it-corresponds-as-the-effect-begins`, `copy-exception-ability-stacks`, `player-destroys-chosen-permanents`, `dice-highest-result-ties`, `greatest-card-unique-player`, `shield-divided-among-recipients`, `entering-counter-modifiers-after-enters-with`, `simultaneous-damage-counters-one-event`, `simultaneous-entries-ordered-by-controller`, `more-loyalty-activations-add-up`, `may-gain-control-if-you-do-follows-the-choice`, `cast-order-as-chosen`, `mana-ability-cost-trigger-uses-the-stack`, `simultaneous-events-count-as-an-ability-triggers`, `sequential-investigations-are-separate-events`, `outside-the-game-is-the-sideboard`, `the-token-another-ability-created`, `face-down-instant-or-sorcery-stays-face-down`, `unrevealed-top-card-grants-no-abilities`; partial (a neighbouring case): `requirements-of-creatures-unable-to-attack`, `mill-until-stops-when-nothing-moves`, `per-source-prevention-division`, `player-chosen-before-targets`, `one-target-per-card-type`, `total-mana-value-choice-fitted`, `next-turn-effects-start-with-the-turn`, `choose-for-each-player-distinct-objects`. The opposite risk (an answering ruling not quoted): the entries of point 5 and `control-lost-before-a-for-as-long-as-you-control-effect-begins`.

Verdicts on the auditors: every fix verified, none reverted (one reworded: `cost-paid-reflexive-trigger`'s Decision no longer says casualty's definition "reads" the engine's sentence). Overstated: `changeling-cda-only` (A: the ruling "points the other way from the Decision": it implies the rules' answer is the Decision's, for a card legal in no format) and `commander-identity-protection-player` (A: Ardenn's ruling "can be read against" the Decision: the rulings specific to abilities an Equipment grants support it). Incomplete: `mill-until-stops-when-nothing-moves` (A found the Wheel loop but not the Colossus bug) and `simultaneous-entries-ordered-by-controller` (B found the judgment call but not the engine's add-back of a face-down source's effect). `that-opponent-fixed-as-it-triggers`'s analogs (Tainted Aether, the monarch draw ruling, Celestial Mantle) are in the entry since ruling-research-2 landed.
