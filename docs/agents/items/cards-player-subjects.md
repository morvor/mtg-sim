# cards-player-subjects: Instruction grammar: any player as the subject ("each player may ...", "target opponent reveals ...", "that player exiles the top ...", "each player who ...", "you and target opponent each ...") — 329 texts / 336 cards (292 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 329 unsupported ability texts (329 on paper cards) blocking 336 paper cards; 292 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards-player-subjects.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Every instruction the parser understands for "you" should also parse with any player subject and verb agreement: "each player may [verb]", "that player may pay {2}", "target player reveals X cards from their hand", "target opponent puts a card from their hand on top of their library", "that player exiles the top four cards of their library", "each opponent reveals their hand", relative-clause subjects ("each player who controls six or more lands chooses five lands they control and sacrifices the rest", "each opponent who didn't discard a nonland card this way loses 2 life"), compound subjects ("you and target opponent each draw two cards", "you and that player each gain that much life"), and votes/secret choices by players.

PHRASE FAMILIES (texts in family; representative failing pieces and cards):
- "each player may [instruction]" / "that player may" optional instructions for other players: 67. e.g. "defending player may draw a card." [Harbor Guardian]; "Each player may draw up to two cards." [Temporary Truce]; "that player may discard a card at random." [Apathy]
- "target player/opponent reveals ..." / hand reveal & opponent-chosen cards: 70. e.g. "each opponent reveals their hand." [Valki, God of Lies // Tibalt, Cosmic Impostor]; "The chosen player reveals each card they draw." [Booby Trap]; "target player reveals a number of cards from their hand equal to the number of Allies you control." [Bala Ged Thief]
- "[player] exiles/puts/mills the top N cards of their library": 67. e.g. "Target player mills half their library, rounded down." [Traumatize]; "Then that player puts the exiled cards that weren't cast this way on the bottom of their library in a random order." [Gríma, Saruman's Footman]; "each player exiles the top card of their library." [Rocco, Street Chef]
- relative-clause subjects ("each player who controls six or more lands", "each opponent who didn't discard ..."): 12. e.g. "each player who controls a white creature." [Disorder]; "Target player who lost life this turn loses N life." [Rix Maadi Guildmage]; "Each player chooses a number of lands they control equal to the number of lands controlled by the player who controls the fewest, then sacrifices the rest." [Restore Balance]
- compound subjects ("you and target opponent each draw", "you and that player each"): 22. e.g. "You and target opponent each draw two cards." [Love Song of Night and Day]; "you and that player each draw that many cards." [Xyris, the Writhing Storm]; "each of its controller's opponents draws a card and gains N life." [Bounty Board]
- player counters / designations as subject ("they get N rad counters"): 6. e.g. "they get four rad counters." [Glowing One]; "they get that many rad counters." [Infesting Radroach]; "that opponent gets a poison counter." [Norn's Decree]
- other (mixed; read the texts): 85. e.g. "that player gains N life." [Laquatus's Champion]; "that player untaps a land they control." [Rising Waters]; "Each opponent exiles a card from their hand or a permanent they control." [Nicol Bolas, Dragon-God]

APPROACH: 1. Generalize subject handling once in `effects::parse_sentence`/`parse_clause`: strip a player subject (PlayerRef incl. "each player who [condition]", "you and [player] each", "any number of target opponents each"), map third-person verbs and possessives (their/they/them) to the canonical second-person instruction, parse the rest with the existing patterns, then wrap: `Effect::AsPlayer(player, e)` for a single player, `ForEachPlayer(filter, e)` for each/compound, `May{who}` for "may".
2. Possessives: "their library/hand/graveyard" must bind to the subject player, not to "you".
3. Relative clauses on players use the condition grammar (cards-conditions-* items) — accept the subset already supported, list the rest.

ENGINE CAPABILITIES: Effect::AsPlayer, ForEachPlayer and May{who} exist. Check that every primitive honours the acting player (zones "their library", who chooses, who pays). Votes/secret choices exist (a701_secret_votes.rs).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS:
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (an Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- A text can contain a second failing construct owned by another item. Fix it when it is small; otherwise leave it and list it under remaining gaps with the owning item.
- One-offs. If a text's construct is genuinely unique (no other card shares it) and compiling it would be hacky, don't force it: once cards-manual-defs has landed, give it a hand-written definition in src/cards/ with a test; before that, list it as a manual candidate.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage` and the baseline filters above. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
