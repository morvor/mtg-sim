SCOPE: 113 unsupported ability texts on paper cards (68 grammar; 20 engine; 2 with a second construct owned by an in-progress round-1 item ("also needs"); 20 former one-off claims re-checked as shared constructs; 3 round-1 manual candidates re-checked as shared constructs), blocking 112 paper cards; 104 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item or wait for a round-1 item). RULES NOTES:
- Costs: CR 601.2b (X and cost choices are announced while casting; Open the Way ruling: "The number of players in the game is checked only as you choose the value for X"), 601.2f (total cost: cost increases, reductions and additional costs; amounts per target are known after targets are chosen), 602.1b (text after the colon may state which players may activate), 602.2b, 602.5 / 602.5a-b (activation prohibitions and restrictions), 107.3a/c/f (X), 118.9 (alternative costs), 702.21a (ward), 702.24a (cumulative upkeep), 702.30a (echo).
- Replacements: CR 614.1a ("instead"), 614.6 (a replaced event never happens; a reflexive "When you do" on a static replacement is a triggered ability, Head of the Hunt rulings: creatures are exiled instead of dying and "abilities that would trigger when those creatures die won't trigger"), 614.11 (draw replacements apply even with an empty library; Zur's Weirding rulings: with several draws, each is replaced one at a time, and a card put into the graveyard this way was never drawn), 614.12 (entering replacements; Lotus Vale ruling: "If you don't sacrifice the lands, Lotus Vale never enters — it goes directly to your graveyard"), 119.10 (life-gain replacements read as "if a source would cause ..."), 106.12b (mana replacements on a land tapped for mana).
- Enduring Renewal rulings: only cards "drawn" are affected; the revealed card is the one drawn; its last ability is a trigger (the card can be removed from the graveyard in response).
- Regeneration: CR 701.19a-c (a regeneration shield; "can't be regenerated" doesn't stop creating a shield, only its use).
- Damage: CR 120.4a (excess damage dealt to another permanent or player instead; Ram Through rulings: excess is determined like trample (deathtouch makes 1 lethal) and both are dealt simultaneously; if either target is illegal, no damage is dealt), 614.9 (redirection), 615.12 / 615.12a (unpreventable damage; prevention shields still apply their other effects once).
- As-enters exile: CR 614.12, 607.2b (cards exiled by an as-enters replacement effect are linked to the abilities that refer to the exiled cards). Sutured Ghoul rulings: it can't exile itself even entering from your graveyard; characteristic-defining abilities of exiled cards apply in exile.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 2):
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts marked "also needs <round-1 item>" belong to a round-1 item that is still in progress for one construct and to this item for the other. Do your construct; if that item has landed when you finish, make the whole text work, otherwise list the text as waiting for it. A text can also contain a second construct that the probes didn't reach: fix it when it is small, otherwise list it with the item that owns that area.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other card shares the construct), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards-manual-defs-2. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
