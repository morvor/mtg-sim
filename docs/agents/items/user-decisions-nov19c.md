**Changed to the alternative:** Shadow Puppeteers on an object that isn't a creature as the effect begins (open question in rulings-residue.md, "Shadow Puppeteers' trigger resolving for a creature that Swift Reconfiguration made a ..."). 1. Rule to implement, generally: when a resolving effect makes an object gain a subtype "in addition to its other types" (and, with it, a creature's base power and toughness), the subtypes that don't correspond to one of the object's types as the effect begins (CR 205.3d "An object can't gain a subtype that doesn't correspond to one of that object's types.") are never gained by that effect, for its whole duration, even if the object gets that card type later (crewed). The same for the effect's base power/toughness setting when the object isn't a creature as the effect begins (Shadow Puppeteers 2023-09-01: "Since it's not a creature, it won't gain the Dragon subtype, and it won't have base power and toughness 4/4."). The other parts (the color) apply. An object that was a creature as the effect began and stops being one later keeps following 205.3d continuously: Terror of Towashi (2023-04-14) "If the creature stops being a creature after the ability has resolved, it will also stop being a Phyrexian until it becomes a creature again, if applicable."
