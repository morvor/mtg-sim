# small-fixes-4: leftovers from wave 6

Implementation item (effort high; review at max). Each part is small; quote every CR rule and ruling you rely on verbatim, and check every ruling of the cards involved before calling anything provisional.

1. **CR 118.8c for a choice of additional costs** (rulings-P078's report): "If an effect instructs a player to cast a spell "if able," and that spell has a mandatory additional cost that includes actions involving cards with a stated quality in a hidden zone, the player isn't required to cast that spell, even if those cards are present in that zone." The engine doesn't apply it when the mandatory additional cost is a choice one of whose options involves such cards (Thunderherd Migration's "As an additional cost to cast this spell, reveal a Dinosaur card from your hand or pay {1}"; behold-or-pay costs). Decide by the rule's text and any rulings (the option involving the hidden zone vs the whole cost), register the reading (decided if the text settles it, else provisional), implement, test with Wild Evocation (or another "cast if able" effect).
2. **Inquisitor Eisenhorn's "this way" trigger for a card drawn while casting** (finish-line-plan C9; roundtrip-tail-5's Remaining): CR 121.8 "If a spell or ability causes a card to be drawn while another spell is being cast, the drawn card is kept face down until that spell becomes cast ... If an effect allows or instructs a player to reveal the card as it's being drawn, it's revealed after the spell becomes cast or the ability becomes activated. ..." The trigger never fires for such a card; it should fire when the card is revealed. Fix generally for "reveal as you draw" triggers (God-Eternal Kefnet's too, if it shares the path), with tests.
3. **CR 113.11 for entering riders** (user-decisions-nov19c's remaining gaps): `drop_ungrantable_keywords` isn't applied to `Destination::with_mods` riders (Valkyrie's Call's "It has flying" on a creature that "can't have flying"). Apply it, test.
4. **Siege of Towers' template-copy ruling** (RULING_DISCREPANCIES.md "Not yet investigated"; ruling-conflicts-11's Noticed): its 2024-01-12 ruling names Shattering Spree ("As the replicate triggered ability resolves, you'll copy Shattering Spree for each time you paid its replicate cost, ..."). Investigate (same-day Shattering Spree ruling) and move it to a proper "template error" entry; the ruling stays cited under Siege of Towers and tested on the card it describes, per CLAUDE.md.
5. **Tangle Wire's round trip** (rulings-residue-burn-freeze): the renderer's "of that player's choice" wording no longer matches the compiled behavior (the affected player taps); fix the renderer so it round-trips.
6. **Seasinger listed as digital**: docs/agents/items/cards-digital-alchemy.texts.tsv line 877 lists Seasinger (a paper card, Future Sight) as digital (ruling-research-1's side finding). Check why (the tool that wrote the file) and fix the cause, not only the line; check for other paper cards misclassified the same way.

`cargo test --workspace` passes; yield row; follow worker.md's one-item-per-session rule and notify the coordinator.

## Result (implementer, Nov 20)

1. `cost_rules::additional_cost_involves_hidden_quality` looks into `AdditionalCostChoice` options, `CostPart::OneOf` and behold cost parts: the whole mandatory cost excuses a cast "if able" when any option involves cards of a stated quality in a hidden zone. Register `cast-if-able-hidden-cost-choice` (provisional in this version, the alternative being to excuse only the option; decided in the review, below). Tests `tests/rulings/r_cast_if_able_hidden_cost_choice.rs` (Wild Evocation, Omen Machine; Thunderherd Migration, Caustic Exhale, Lash of the Balrog).
2. "Whenever you reveal a [card] this way" after "(You may) reveal the first card you draw ..." is now a trigger on the reveal (`TriggerCond::Custom(kw::reveal_first_draw::REVEALED_FIRST_DRAW)`), which `kw/reveal_first_draw.rs` performs as the card is drawn or, for a card drawn while casting, from `draw_rules::finish_casting` (CR 121.8). It triggers only for the permanent whose ability revealed the card (CR 607.2h); each "you may reveal" ability is chosen for separately (God-Eternal Kefnet's ruling). Tests `tests/cr/r121_8_reveal_as_drawn.rs`.
3. `drop_ungrantable_keywords` runs on `Destination::with_mods` effects (`actions.rs`). Test `tests/rulings/r_cant_have_entering_riders.rs`.
4. Siege of Towers: template-error entry in docs/RULING_DISCREPANCIES.md; exemption of "you'll copy Shattering Spree" (`rulings-P222.tsv`); test `tests/rulings/r_siege_of_towers_replicate.rs`.
5. Renderer: "[player] taps [N] untapped [objects] they control [for each ...]" (`render/effects.rs`); Tangle Wire added to docs/roundtrip-passing.txt (its tier in docs/verified-cards.tsv updates at the next sweep).
6. cards-digital-alchemy.texts.tsv refreshed (600 rows; the 254 paper-card "digital" rows, Seasinger's among them, were round-1 output from before paper-scope-1, and their texts all compile now); check in `crates/mtg-data/tests/paper.rs`.

## Review (Nov 20)

1. Decided (was provisional): 118.8c's text settles it. Its condition is a property of the spell known before anything is chosen ("that spell has a mandatory additional cost that includes actions involving cards with a stated quality in a hidden zone"), the card states one mandatory additional cost that includes the hidden-zone action, and the consequence ("isn't required to cast that spell, even if those cards are present in that zone") has no exception for a cost payable another way. Engine (review): behold costs wrapped with what's done with the beheld object were missed, so the five behold-and-exile champions (Champion of the Weird, ...) and Monstrous Emergence ("choose a creature you control or reveal a creature card from your hand") still had to be cast (`cost_rules::beholds_quality`, test `wild_evocation_need_not_cast_a_spell_that_beholds_and_exiles_or_chooses_or_reveals`).
2. Registered both calls the implementer left out: `reveal-as-drawn-for-each-ability` (607.2h, Kefnet's and Primitive Etchings' rulings; decided) and `reveal-as-drawn-chosen-when-revealed` (the reveal choice for a card drawn while casting is made when 121.8 reveals it; a reversed casting reveals it then, as `illegal-action-reversal-restores-libraries` says; decided). New tests: Primitive Etchings with Inquisitor Eisenhorn (the mandatory reveal isn't Eisenhorn's "this way"; the implementer's fix of it had no test) and a reversed casting. The Kefnet test passed for the wrong reason (its library ran out: the "two cards" were Drawing Well's draw and one of the copy's, and P0 lost the game); rewritten.
3. Valkyrie's Call test: CR 611.2e now exercised (Bishop of Wings triggers on the returned Angel).
5. Only Tangle Wire's rendering uses the new Tap arm (`roundtrip --all`); no other card newly passes because of it.
6. Checked against `mtg-tools unsupported --by-card`: every row is still unsupported, the only paper cards are the deferred Pearl Collector, Tenacious Pup and Sigardian Evangel, and no text found only on non-paper cards is missing.
