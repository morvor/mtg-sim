# cards2-combat: Combat grammar III: damage assignment as though unblocked and unblocking, attack/block requirements, restrictions and taxes, scoped additional combats and entering attacking/blocking, goad durations, combat referents — 89 texts / 89 cards (86 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 89 unsupported ability texts on paper cards (54 grammar; 16 engine; 14 former one-off claims re-checked as shared constructs; 5 round-1 manual candidates re-checked as shared constructs), blocking 89 paper cards; 86 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item or wait for a round-1 item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards2-combat.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: The combat long tail. Damage assignment and blocks: "for each creature you control, you may have that creature assign its combat damage as though it weren't blocked", one-shot and enchanted-creature versions, "Creatures it was blocking that had become blocked by only that creature this combat become unblocked", "assigns no combat damage this combat", extra blocks per Equipment. Requirements, restrictions and taxes: companion restrictions ("can't attack unless a black or green creature also attacks"), block and attack taxes per creature keyed to the attacker or blocker, player-level requirements ("Each opponent must attack you or a planeswalker you control with at least one creature each combat if able"), requirements toward a computed or chosen player, per-creature attack history ("can't attack a player it has already attacked this turn"), "the active player has controlled continuously since the beginning of the turn". Additional combats with their own rules ("Only land creatures can attack during that combat phase") and entering the battlefield attacking a named player or blocking a given creature. Goad and suspect with other durations. Combat referents: "the attacking player", "players being attacked", "creatures attacking that player", "creatures banded with ~", "creatures blocking or blocked by target creature", block history this combat or turn.

RULES NOTES:
- Declarations: CR 508.1c/509.1b (restrictions), 508.1d (requirements: the maximum number of requirements obeyed without violating restrictions; Trove of Temptation rulings: one creature total satisfies it; creatures that can't attack don't have to; attacking a teammate doesn't satisfy it), 508.1h / 509.1d (costs to attack or block, paid as declared), 509.1g-h (blocked and unblocked status stays fixed until removed from combat; an effect that makes attackers unblocked is an exception the card states), 506.4 (removed from combat; False Orders ruling: triggers from being declared as a blocker still happen).
- Damage: CR 510.1a (0 or less assigns no damage; "assigns no combat damage" effects), 510.1c-d (assignment; "as though it weren't blocked": Siege Behemoth ruling: the choice is made per creature just before it assigns damage).
- Entering attacking or blocking: CR 506.3a/e (a noncreature put onto the battlefield attacking or blocking never attacks or blocks; a creature put onto the battlefield blocking an attacker that isn't attacking its controller, their planeswalker or a battle they protect enters but isn't blocking), 508.4 (the controller chooses which player, planeswalker or battle it attacks unless the effect names one). Kaalia of the Vast rulings: the creature enters attacking the opponent Kaalia attacked even if Kaalia now attacks something else; it was never declared as an attacker (no attack triggers); if that opponent left the game it enters but isn't attacking; it doesn't trigger when Kaalia attacks a planeswalker or battle.
- Additional combats: CR 500.8 (an effect can add phases; the added combat is a separate phase), so a restriction "during that combat phase" applies only to the phase the same effect created, not to later combats.
- Goad: CR 701.15a-d (goaded until the goading player's next turn by default; multiple goaders; Life of the Party ruling: a creature goaded by a player who left the game still must attack another player if able). Suspect: CR 701.60a, 701.60c.
- Banding: CR 702.22. Scarred Puma ruling: "It means 'a black or green creature other than itself attacks'."

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 2):
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts marked "also needs <round-1 item>" belong to a round-1 item that is still in progress for one construct and to this item for the other. Do your construct; if that item has landed when you finish, make the whole text work, otherwise list the text as waiting for it. A text can also contain a second construct that the probes didn't reach: fix it when it is small, otherwise list it with the item that owns that area.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other card shares the construct), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards-manual-defs-2. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (TestGame combat tests per family with real cards, including multiplayer attacks for player-level requirements and attack history) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
