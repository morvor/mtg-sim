GOAL: Texts tied to game rules beyond a single game's stack. (1) Draft-matters cards (CR 905.2; draft.rs models the Conspiracy draft with face-up drafting and some notes): "Reveal ~ as you draft it" followed by new note kinds, "As you draft a card, you may remove it from the draft face up/down", "As you draft a card, you may draft an additional card from that booster pack", "During the draft, you may turn ~ face down. If you do, look at ...", "Each player passes the last card from each booster pack to a player who drafted a card named ~", "Immediately after the draft, you may reveal a card in your card pool", values and conditions that read the notes ("a name you noted for cards named ~", "the colors chosen as you drafted cards named ~", "the number of cards you removed from the draft with cards named ~"), and pre-game exile ("Before you shuffle your deck to start the game, you may reveal ~ ... and exile ... cards you drafted that aren't in your deck", "a card you exiled with cards named ~"). (2) Dice and coins: results tables whose rows refer to the header's objects, results as values ("where X is the result", "If any of those results was 10 or higher", "For each different result"), dice replacements ("instead roll that many dice plus one and ignore one", "exchange one result with ~'s base power or base toughness"), dice in costs ("{6}, Roll a d8"), flips with new outcomes ("If the coin comes up heads, ~ enters as a 5/2 creature with haste", "each player whose coin comes up tails", "Flip a coin until you lose a flip or choose to stop flipping"). (3) Attractions and visits, outside-the-game cards and opening-hand actions, commander (color identity, commander creatures), extra loyalty activations, completed dungeons, emblems for other players, teams, speed, rad counters.

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 3):
- These texts are the long tail: most were in a round-1 or round-2 item (column previous_item) and are still unsupported after it landed. Before starting a family, read that item's brief and its commits (`git log --grep '^<item>:'`) for the reason it stopped; re-probe the text (`docs/agents/round3/probe.py --card "<Name>" --text "<edited text>"` compiles edited texts as variants of the real card, with its other paragraphs and types, in one run of the release mtg-tools; `mtg-tools unsupported --text "<text>" --type "<type line>" [--ast]` compiles one bare text) to find the exact failing words: the failing_piece column is the sentence (or cost, trigger, mode, table row) that fails, not always the smallest failing phrase. Column note "then also: ..." is the next piece that still failed with the first one replaced by a filler (sometimes only a referent to the replaced words).
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts of cards that also have a text owned by a round-2 item still in progress (round3/inflight.tsv: cards-manual-defs-2; cards2-triggers-delayed and cards2-copies-tokens-counters landed while round 3 was planned, and their still-unsupported texts are planned in cards3 items) are listed under ALSO BLOCKED BY: do your text; the card becomes supported when both land.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other in-scope paper card shares the construct: search the card data before deciding), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for a manual-definitions item. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --paper --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate, proposed deferral).
