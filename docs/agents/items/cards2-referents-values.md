# cards2-referents-values: Referent and value grammar III: "that many", "the difference", objects affected "this way" or paid as costs, batch and per-player referents, last-known attachments, aggregates over groups, X snapshots — 207 texts / 205 cards (192 unlocked alone)

SCOPE: 207 unsupported ability texts on paper cards (186 grammar; 6 engine; 7 with a second construct owned by an in-progress round-1 item ("also needs"); 6 former one-off claims re-checked as shared constructs; 2 round-1 manual candidates re-checked as shared constructs), blocking 205 paper cards; 192 of those become fully supported when this item alone is done (the others also have a text owned by another item or wait for a round-1 item). The full list, with the failing piece found by compiler probes, ships as items/cards2-referents-values.texts.tsv (columns: cards blocked, normalized text, example card, piece kind, failing piece). Normalized text = reminder text removed, the card's own name as ~, numbers as N.

GOAL: Bind the pronouns, noun-phrase referents and amounts that point back to something earlier in the ability: amounts ("create that many 1/1 tokens" after "if you control five or more Deserts", "put that many +1/+1 counters" after "if it had one or more +1/+1 counters on it", "equal to the difference", "equal to the damage", "draws that many cards minus one", "for each 1 life you lost, ..."), objects ("Choose a nonland card exiled this way", "the creature tapped to pay ~'s additional cost", "untap all Forests put onto the battlefield this way", "Choose a creature at random, then destroy the rest", "remove all of them"), batch and per-player referents ("you may sacrifice one of them", "the power of the creature they exiled", "The controller of each of those artifacts gains life equal to its mana value"), last-known attachments and counters ("draw a card for each Aura you controlled that was attached to it", "put ~'s counters on that token"), aggregates over the previous instruction's objects or over groups ("equal to their total power", "the number of colors it is", "half the creatures they control, rounded up", Balance), and X values defined or locked at a particular time ("X is the greatest power among creatures you controlled as you cast ~", "where X is ~'s power as this ability resolves"). The Value AST already has most of the arithmetic and aggregates (Prev, Var, EventAmount, Aggregate, DistinctAmong, ColorPairsAmong, OverPlayers, Diff, Mul, Div, Min, Max, Extreme); the work is the Builder binding these phrases to what earlier instructions, costs and trigger events recorded, plus recording the results that aren't recorded yet.

RULES NOTES:
- CR 608.2h: information from the game is determined once, when the effect is applied; information about a specific object uses its current state if it's still in the expected public zone, otherwise its last known information (Angrath, Minotaur Pirate ruling: "uses the power of those creatures as they last existed on the battlefield to determine their total power"; a creature that wasn't destroyed still counts with its current power; negative power subtracts).
- CR 400.7: a card that changed zones is a new object; referents such as "the exiled card", "that card", "those cards" follow the card to the zone the instruction put it in, never further (Desert Warfare ruling: a Desert that left your graveyard before the delayed trigger resolves isn't returned).
- CR 603.4: an intervening-if condition is checked when the trigger event occurs and again on resolution; "that many" bound to the condition's count uses the count on resolution. CR 603.10a: leaves-the-battlefield and other look-back triggers see the object as it last existed (counters on a dying creature, Shadow Urchin ruling: effects that refer to the counters on a creature when it died see all of its +1/+1 counters even if -1/-1 counters killed it).
- Last-known attachments: CR 704.5m (an Aura whose object left goes to the graveyard as a state-based action, a new object), 704.5n (Equipment stays, unattached). Hateful Eidolon rulings: "If an Aura you control is destroyed at the same time as the creature it enchants, Hateful Eidolon's triggered ability counts that Aura"; it triggers for each enchanted creature that dies at the same time, itself included.
- X: CR 107.3 (X defined by text vs chosen), 107.3f (X in text only: chosen when put on the stack or as it resolves), 107.3m (an ETB ability of a permanent spell uses the X chosen for the spell; the permanent's own X is 0). Lifestream's Blessing ruling: "The value of X is determined only once, as you cast Lifestream's Blessing."
- Rounding: CR 107.1a (the text says whether to round up or down). Fraying Omnipotence rulings: "The amounts that are lost are rounded up, not the amounts kept"; first all players lose life at once, then each player in APNAP order chooses the cards to discard, and so on. Balance rulings: "Each type of object is counted during the corresponding part of the process"; the player whose turn it is chooses what to keep first, then each other player in turn order; the unchosen permanents are sacrificed simultaneously.
- Batch triggers: Basri Ket ruling: the delayed trigger "counts how many nontoken creatures attacked. Attacking nontoken creatures that leave combat before the triggered ability resolves are counted."
- CR 702.164b: total toxic value is the sum of the creature's toxic N values. CR 607.2n and 905.1d: cards exiled "before you shuffle your deck to start the game" come from the drafted card pool and are linked to all cards with that name (Arcane Savant ruling: with several copies you may reveal any number of them and exile that many cards).

COMMON RULES FOR CARD ITEMS (round 2):
- Generalize. Implement each construct as grammar (subject / verb / object phrase / qualifiers composed from phrases.rs, the value and condition parsers and the Builder's referent tracking), never one pattern per card wording; a fix should also cover wordings that aren't in the list. Unit-test the grammar on several real wordings.
- Faithfulness first. The parser must not accept text the engine doesn't implement exactly (the Oracle round-trip check audits every compiled card). For each family add TestGame tests with real cards that assert the in-game outcome (crates/mtg-engine/tests/cards/ or tests/cr/, citing cr!() for rules exercised and ruling!() for Scryfall rulings exercised).
- Texts marked "also needs <round-1 item>" belong to a round-1 item that is still in progress for one construct and to this item for the other. Do your construct; if that item has landed when you finish, make the whole text work, otherwise list the text as waiting for it. A text can also contain a second construct that the probes didn't reach: fix it when it is small, otherwise list it with the item that owns that area.
- One-offs. The hand-written mechanism exists (src/cards/, `ManualAbility`, `mtg-tools manual-check`; policy in src/cards/mod.rs). If a text here turns out to be a genuine one-off (no other card shares the construct), don't force a pattern: list it in your report for cards-manual-defs-2. Never hand-write a construct that two or more cards share.
- Shared core files (oracle/effects.rs, phrases.rs, statics.rs, triggers.rs, costs.rs, ability.rs, eval.rs, resolve.rs) are edited by several items in parallel: keep edits there small (hooks, new enum variants handled everywhere), put grammar in this item's own pattern files, and merge origin often.
- Build hygiene (CLAUDE.md): `export CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=2`, one cargo build at a time per worktree, no second checkout for before/after numbers.
- Measure before and after: `cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- card-coverage`, the baseline filters (`cargo run --release -q -p mtg-tools -- unsupported --limit 500 --filter "<s>"`) and `mtg-tools unsupported --card "<Name>"` for the listed cards. Report per family: texts fixed, texts remaining (each with a reason). Run `cargo run --release -p mtg-sim -- --random-decks --games 300` before finishing to catch panics in newly supported cards.
- DONE when about 90% or more of this item's texts compile with faithful, tested behaviour (at least one TestGame test per family with a real card; for last-known information, test the creature and its Auras dying at the same time) and every remaining text is listed with a reason (engine gap, belongs to item X, manual candidate).
