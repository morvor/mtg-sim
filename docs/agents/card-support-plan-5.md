## Method

1. Snapshot: `mtg-tools unsupported --paper --by-card` (normalized) and `--raw` for the same lines. Texts were matched by normalized text against every earlier `.texts.tsv` (the latest round that listed a text is its previous item) and `round4/deferred.tsv`: texts of the in-flight round-4 items go to `round5/inflight.tsv` (with their item), deferred texts to `round5/deferred.tsv`, and the 110 leftovers of landed items are probed.
2. Reasons from the landed items: the items' reports aren't in the repository and their commits name few leftovers, so every leftover was re-probed; the notes of the last list that held a text (round 4's drills, round 3's "then also") were read first, and each note here says where the text's state changed since (constructs that compile now, such as Eriette's attach trigger, Chef's Kiss' gain of control of a spell, Kefnet's linked reveal trigger, an opponent's choice among exiled cards).
3. Probing, as rounds 3 and 4 did (`round3/autoprobe.py`, with the card's other paragraphs kept as context): the cost alone, the longest compiling trigger, the effect sentences added one at a time, modal headers and modes. Two texts compile when autoprobe flattens them into one paragraph (Wand of Wonder, Danse Macabre): their results tables are separate lines on the card, and the drills probed them as such.
4. Drilling: 348 hand-written probes (`round5/drills.tsv`: id, card, probe text, OK/FAIL; ids `C5-...`) cut each failing sentence down to the failing words, mostly by replacing one phrase with a simpler one that compiles and by probing the construct on a neutral card (Divination, Grizzly Bears, Icy Manipulator, Glorious Anthem, Pacifism). Column note in each list starts with what was drilled ("drilled: X compiles; Y fails"), names the second failing piece, and names the engine work where the gap is in the engine.
5. Families and items: the texts were grouped by the missing construct into 20 families, and the families into items by parser and engine area (choices and players; referents, notes, copies and history; dice, coins, Attractions, game start and draft), so that two items rarely edit the same files. 110 texts make two items of 42, one of 19 (cards3-game-rules-dice's leftovers, its own area: dice.rs, attraction_cards.rs, opening_hand.rs, draft.rs) and a manual item of 5. A card whose texts fail in two areas has each text where its construct is (Scheming Fence's optional choice stays with its two statics).
6. One-off check: for every construct that looked unique, all in-scope paper cards' Oracle text (paper by the rule above, legal or restricted somewhere) was searched for the same construct, not the same words (`round5/drills.tsv` has the probes; the brief entries name the searches). Unique wording isn't a one-off when the grammar is shared: Timesifter's "the player who exiled the card with the greatest mana value" is Psychic Battle's comparison; Lim-Dûl's Vault and Dance with Calamity share "As many times as you choose"; Deification's and Leori's planeswalker-type choice; Psychic Paper's and Paleontologist's Pick-Axe's as-attached choice (it compiles for colors); Storage Matrix's "can untap only" is the untap-limit grammar with a type set; Goblin Artisans' "the target of an ability from another creature named ~" extends the "is the target of" filters of 178 cards; Chancellor of the Annex' "first spell of the game" extends the ordinal cast triggers; Gemstone Caverns extends the Leylines; Fiery Gambit extends the flip-until loops (Zndrsplt, Okaun). Five texts are genuine one-offs (cards5-manual-defs): River Song's draws from the bottom, Deification's loyalty replacement, Vedalken Squirrel-Whacker's result exchange, Celebr-8000's per-result table (CR 706.5 names it as the one such card) and Curse of Leeches' attach as it transforms.
7. Pending: a text goes to `round5/pending-recheck.tsv` when its first failing construct belongs to an in-flight item's family (checked against that item's brief and list): Season of the Bold's "Until the end of your next turn, whenever you cast a spell, ..." (cards4-triggers-durations' Nuka-Nuke Launcher) and Mr. House's "for each mana from Treasures spent to activate this ability" (cards4-costs-mana-replacements' `mana-provenance`). Texts whose first failing construct is not in flight but whose second piece is stay in the cards5 item, with the dependency under ALSO BLOCKED BY (Krovikan Vampire, Chaos Moon, Eriette).
8. Counts: "touched" = paper cards with at least one of the item's texts; "all texts here" = cards whose every unsupported text is in the item (what the check script computes); "unlocked alone" = those minus the cards that also need an in-flight construct (ALSO BLOCKED BY). Effort = texts × difficulty factor (1.0 = parser grammar over existing engine support; higher where engine work, shared-core risk or new decisions are expected). The factors are higher than round 4's because these texts have resisted two to four items.

## How far this gets

- The cards5 items alone: 103 cards have all their unsupported texts in cards5 items and need no in-flight construct (100 unlocked by single items plus River Song, Deification and Vedalken Squirrel-Whacker, which need a cards5 item and cards5-manual-defs; Chaos Moon also needs an in-flight item). With each text done with probability p independently (expectation over the real card-to-text map): p = 70% → about 70 cards; p = 80% → about 81; p = 90% → about 92.
- These texts have resisted two to four items. The landed items' rates on texts that had resisted at least one earlier item: cards4-referents-statics fixed 61% of its texts, cards3-followups-choices 59%, cards3-game-rules-dice about 70% (of the 80 it kept), cards4-cast-zones 86%. So expect p nearer 55-70%: about 55-70 of the 103 cards.
- Everything planned (cards5 items, the in-flight round-4 items, the pending texts) among the 245 unsupported paper cards after cards4-manual-defs: with p = 60-70% for the cards5 texts and 70-85% for the in-flight items' texts (they are new to their items), about 135-160 of the 210 non-deferred cards (when planned: 165-200 of 255, counting the 30 cards cards4-triggers-durations and the 15 cards4-manual-defs have since unlocked; after cards4-triggers-durations: 145-175 of 225). 35 cards stay unsupported because of deferred texts (the 24 sticker cards, Pearl Collector, Tenacious Pup, Sigardian Evangel, Steamflogger Boss, Chaos Orb, Falling Star and the five Conspiracy face-down draft cards).
- **What is left after round 5 (the long tail)**: about 50-75 non-deferred cards, nearly all with a single unsupported text each, from three sources: (a) the cards5 and round-4 texts that no round could compile (expect 35-50; mostly the `results`, `history`, `copies`, `per-player-results` and `each-player-choices` constructs, which have failed in three or more items); (b) genuine one-offs (5 here; cards4-manual-defs' dozen landed; expect a few more to surface as items report single-card constructs); (c) engine gaps that need a whole feature rather than grammar: game-long and cross-turn history per object and per source spell (Territorial Hellkite, Backdraft, Krovikan Vampire, the Glyphs), choices made by players other than the controller that the agent interface must route (Mogg Assassin, Confusion in the Ranks), manifest from a hand, card copies in hidden zones (Kefnet), notes kept on objects across turns (the Ents, Volo, Bell Borca), durations that end on events other than steps (a die roll, being turned face down), and draft-table rules (Lore Seeker, Canal Dredger). Finishing it would take a different kind of work than another grouped round: one engine feature per item (each worth one to four cards), more hand-written definitions under the existing policy when a construct proves to have only one in-scope card, and, for the last few, user decisions on deferral. Each remaining card will cost more than the cards so far; at 31,866 of 32,111 (99.2%) the expected gain of a round 6 is about 30-60 cards.

## Adversarial review

Done by the planner as a separate pass after the first placement. No subagent tool was available in this session, so the shards (one per previous item) and the review were run directly, with scripts, as in round 4:

- **Every family sampled with a fresh minimal construct on a neutral card** (48 review probes, `round5/drills.tsv` ids `C5-R-...`): confirmed generally failing for object-choices ("Choose any number of target spells", "Target opponent chooses target creature you control", "the other cards" after an opponent's choice), each-player-choices ("Each player chooses a land they control.
