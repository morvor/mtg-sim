# Card support, round 4: work plan for the texts the landed round-3 items left unsupported

Generated in the planning item `cards4-plan` (Oct 21) from the release `mtg-tools` and the Scryfall data in `data/`, first at the integration branch's commit c6c86559 and refreshed after cards3-triggers-durations landed during planning (see "Refresh after cards3-triggers-durations landed"; the counts below are the refreshed ones). Round 3: `card-support-plan-3.md`; round 2: `card-support-plan-2.md`; round 1: `card-support-plan.md`. The inputs, lists and the check script are in `round4/`; the probe scripts are round 3's (`round3/probe.py`, `round3/autoprobe.py`).

## Baseline

- `mtg-tools card-coverage` (after cards3-triggers-durations landed): **30,643 / 31,051** paper cards fully supported (98.7%; 130 with hand-written abilities); abilities 58,060 / 58,500. 48 sticker sheets are deferred and not counted. (First snapshot, before it landed: 30,593.)
- Unsupported paper cards: **408**, with **438** distinct unsupported texts (440 card-text lines; `mtg-tools unsupported --paper --by-card`, numbers as N, the card's own name as ~). Snapshot: `round4/baseline.tsv`. No text is new since round 3: every one is in a round-3 list or in `round3/deferred.tsv`. (First snapshot: 458 cards, 490 texts.)
- Round-3 items: six have landed (cards3-referents-values, cards3-cast-zones, cards3-history-conditions, cards3-combat-statics, cards3-costs-mana-replacements, and cards3-triggers-durations during this plan); of their 584 texts, **192** are still unsupported (cards3-costs-mana-replacements 63 of 118, cards3-cast-zones 47 of 105, cards3-referents-values 38 of 133, cards3-triggers-durations 19 of 72 plus one moved to the deferred list, cards3-combat-statics 15 of 73, cards3-history-conditions 10 of 83). Three have not landed and keep their texts (`round4/inflight.tsv`, **213** texts): cards3-followups-choices (120 texts, 117 cards) and cards3-game-rules-dice (85, 72) with the cards-1 worker, and cards3-manual-defs (8, 7) running in parallel with this plan.
- This plan places all 438 texts exactly once (`round4/check_plan.py --live` passes against a fresh `mtg-tools unsupported --paper --by-card`): **188** in 4 cards4 items, **2** in cards4-manual-defs, **213** in flight with round-3 items, **2** waiting for an in-flight item's construct (`round4/pending-recheck.tsv`), **33** deferred under DEFERRED.md (`round4/deferred.tsv`: round 3's list, which gained Sigardian Evangel's conjure text when cards3-triggers-durations landed; nothing else new is out of scope). 4 cards have texts with two owners (3 of them a cards4 item and an in-flight item); none has three.

## Method

1. Snapshot: `mtg-tools unsupported --paper --by-card` (normalized) and `--raw` for the same lines. Texts were matched by normalized text against the round-3 `.texts.tsv` lists and `round3/deferred.tsv`: texts of the four in-flight items go to `round4/inflight.tsv` (with their item), deferred texts to `round4/deferred.tsv`, and the leftovers of the landed items are probed (173 at the first snapshot, plus 19 of cards3-triggers-durations and the 3 re-check texts that waited for it at the refresh).
2. Reasons from the landed items: the round-3 items' reports aren't in the repository, and their commits name few of the texts they left (the only recorded reason found is Jailbreak's: tests/cards/conditions_referents_2.rs pins that "When that permanent enters" can't follow the returned object, CR 400.7). So every text was re-probed and drilled instead, and where the drill shows an engine gap the brief names the engine work.
3. Probing, as round 3 did (`round3/autoprobe.py` on the 173 texts of the first snapshot and the 22 of the refresh, with the card's other paragraphs kept as context): the cost alone, the longest compiling trigger, the effect sentences added one at a time, modal headers and modes. A second pass replaced the failing piece with a filler and probed again: for 97 of the first 173 texts nothing else fails, for 38 another piece fails (column note, "second probe ..."), and for 37 the filler itself doesn't fit the ability (a static or a cost), which says nothing. One text (Talent of the Telepath) compiles as one paragraph: its spell mastery paragraph, which changes the previous paragraph's count, is the failing piece.
4. Drilling: about 340 hand-written probes (`round4/drills.tsv`: id, card, probe text, OK/FAIL) cut each failing sentence down to the failing words, mostly by replacing one phrase with a simpler one that compiles, and by probing the construct on a neutral card (Divination, Grizzly Bears, Pacifism). Column note in each list starts with what was drilled ("drilled: X compiles; Y fails").
5. Families and items: the texts were grouped by the missing construct into families, and the families into items by parser and engine area (card flow and permissions; costs, mana and replacements; referents, copies, grants and history; triggers and durations), so that two items rarely edit the same files. Round 3 had 100-200 texts per item; the 188 texts here make three items of 50-61 and one of 22 (cards3-triggers-durations' leftovers, which own triggers.rs and until.rs), each one area. A text whose card has another text in the same area went with it (Uba Mask's and Shared Fate's draw replacements feed their per-player exile sets, so both are in cards4-cast-zones).
6. One-off check: for every construct that looked unique, all in-scope paper cards' Oracle text (paper, legal or restricted somewhere) was searched for the same construct, not the same words. Unique wording isn't a one-off when the grammar is shared: Martyrdom's "Only you may activate this ability" is the counterpart of "Any player may activate this ability" (36 cards); Hellish Rebuke's "the player who cast ~" is a granter reference like round 3's "granted abilities that name the granter"; Hall of Gemstone shares mana-production replacements with False Dawn, Chaos Moon and the supported Pale Moon; Territorial Hellkite's "during your last combat" is attack history across turns like round 3's "during their controller's last turn"; Rain of Gore and Unpredictable Cyclone share cause-qualified replacements with the supported Silhouette; Phyrexian Grimoire and Spinning Darkness share graveyard order. Two texts are genuine one-offs: Guile (a replacement of countering a spell; nothing else in scope replaces the counter event) and Torrent of Lava (a sorcery's static ability that works on the stack; split second is the only other, and it is a keyword).
7. Pending: a text goes to `round4/pending-recheck.tsv` when its first failing construct belongs to an in-flight round-3 item's family (checked against that item's brief): object choices of cards3-followups-choices (Emergent Ultimatum, Mirror of Fate). Three more (Jailbreak, Stolen Uniform, False Cure) waited for cards3-triggers-durations until it landed; they still fail and are in cards4-triggers-durations. Texts whose first failing construct is new but whose second piece belongs to another item stay where their first piece is, with the dependency in the note (Eriette's "for as long as that Aura is attached to it", Chaos Moon's mana trigger for the turn).
8. Counts: "touched" = paper cards with at least one of the item's texts; "unlocked alone" = cards whose every unsupported text is in the item. Effort = texts × difficulty factor (1.0 = parser grammar over existing engine support; higher where engine work, shared-core risk or new decisions are expected).

## Dependencies and scheduling

- **On in-flight round-3 items**: the cards4 items don't need them to start. The three ALSO BLOCKED BY cards wait for cards3-followups-choices or cards3-manual-defs. Cross-item second pieces: cards4-cast-zones' Eriette ("for as long as that Aura is attached to it": cards4-triggers-durations' `durations`), cards4-referents-statics' Chaos Moon (a mana trigger for the turn; a production replacement of cards4-costs-mana-replacements), cards4-triggers-durations' Sen Triplets (playing from another player's hand: cards4-cast-zones' permissions) and its copy durations (layer-1 code shared with cards4-referents-statics' static copy effects). Zur's Weirding's "any other player may pay 2 life" may reuse cards3-followups-choices' punisher grammar if it has landed.
- **Between cards4 items**: cards4-cast-zones owns exile provenance (who exiled a card and with which ability); cards4-referents-statics' linked "the exiled card" in granted abilities (Timothar, Hofri) reads the same links (linked_notes.rs): merge often. cards4-costs-mana-replacements owns mana provenance and the mana-production replacements; Chaos Moon (cards4-referents-statics) needs the latter. "The chosen permanent/creature/card" as the object of a static is shared by cards4-referents-statics' `copies` and `granted-abilities` families (same item).
- **Shared files**: permissions.rs, linked_notes.rs, attach.rs, simultaneous.rs (cast-zones); mana.rs, mana_abilities.rs, mana_change.rs, cost_choices.rs, replacement.rs, prevention.rs (costs-mana-replacements); copy.rs, layers.rs, history_query.rs, combat.rs, excess_damage.rs (referents-statics); oracle/effects.rs and phrases.rs are shared by all three: keep edits small and merge often.
- **Suggested waves**: wave 1 — cards4-cast-zones and cards4-referents-statics (different files; 104 cards unlocked), and cards4-manual-defs (independent, small; after cards3-manual-defs lands so it reuses its hooks). Wave 2 — cards4-costs-mana-replacements and cards4-triggers-durations (both touch trigger grammar: mana triggers and effect-created triggers; merge often, or run them one after the other). Then re-check `round4/pending-recheck.tsv` when cards3-followups-choices lands.

## How far this gets

- The cards4 items alone: 181 cards have all their unsupported texts in a cards4 item. If each text is done with probability p, independently (expectation over the real card-to-text map): p = 85% → about 153 cards; p = 90% → about 162; p = 95% → about 172.
- Everything planned (cards4 items, the in-flight round-3 items and the pending texts) among today's 408 unsupported paper cards: p = 85% → about 318; p = 90% → about 338; p = 95% → about 358. 30 cards stay unsupported because of deferred texts (the 24 sticker cards, Pearl Collector, Tenacious Pup, Sigardian Evangel, Steamflogger Boss, Chaos Orb and Falling Star).
- The rest is again the long tail: 47 texts in the cards4 items have a second failing piece noted, and round 3's landed items fixed between 47% (cards3-costs-mana-replacements) and 88% (cards3-history-conditions) of their texts, so expect p nearer 70-80% for these 188, which have now resisted two or three items: about 126-144 of the 181 cards.

## Adversarial review

Done by the planner as a separate pass after the first placement (no subagents were available in this session, so the probing shards and the review were run directly, with scripts), on every family and every one-off claim:

- **Every family sampled, with a minimal construct on a neutral card** (42 probes, `round4/drills.tsv` ids starting with `R-` and the drills `ca*`, `cw*`, `eu1`; the first pass's neutral probes are the ids without a number prefix, such as `aura-anynum`): confirmed generally failing for exiled-by-player ("cards you exiled with ~"), play-permissions ("one of the exiled cards", "for each nonland card type", two target groups), attach-moves ("any number of Aura cards ... attached to creatures you control", "attach ~ to an opponent chosen at random", "If ~ is still on the battlefield"), as-enters-actions, split-moves, mana-provenance ("mana from an artifact", "Spend only mana produced by Treasures"), mana-production, mana-symbols, mana-replacements, additional-costs, draw-replacements, prevention (relational source choice), copies (static copy on an Aura), batch-referents, combat-history, values, spell-targets, instead-alternatives, history (untargeted player choice).
- **Where the minimal form compiled, the family's claim was narrowed and the notes corrected** (column note, "review: ..."): `cost-sets` — "Sacrifice any number of creatures with total power 5 or greater" compiles as a cost, so the brief now names the new aggregate kinds (card types among, mana symbols among, a total that defines X, totals in an unless cost); `granted-abilities` (Scheming Fence) — "~ has all activated abilities of creatures your opponents control" compiles, the gap is "the chosen permanent" as a static's object, which also links it to the `copies` family; `cost-referents` (Captain America) — the cost referent and the divided damage each compile, only their combination fails (and it raises the 601.2d ordering question); `prevention` (Cover of Winter) — "prevent X of that damage" compiles for one recipient, the gap is X over several recipients; `results` (Eumidian Wastewaker) — "for each land card put into a graveyard this way" compiles after "each player mills", the gap is a "you and [player] each" step; `zone-phrases` (Mister Immortal) — "target creature card from your graveyard or from exile" compiles, the gap is ~ itself from two zones; `excess-damage` — "the excess damage dealt this way" compiles as a value, the gaps are the replacement, fights and the trigger value.
- **Pending claims checked against the in-flight briefs**: Mythos of Snapdax moved out of the pending list into cards4-referents-statics (`instead-alternatives`): "you choose the permanents for each player instead" isn't one of cards3-followups-choices' families (its object choices are chosen by another player or with a relation, not an each-player step taken over by one player). Eriette, the Beguiler stays in cards4-cast-zones: its first failing piece is the trigger ("an Aura you control becomes attached to a nonland permanent an opponent controls" fails without the duration too), only the duration is cards3-triggers-durations'. The other five pending texts were confirmed: Emergent Ultimatum compiles with "You may cast those cards" (so the opponent's choice and "the other cards" are the gap), Mirror of Fate's targeted form compiles, Stolen Uniform compiles without its delayed trigger, False Cure's effect-created trigger fails alone, and Jailbreak's refusal is pinned by a test.
- **Every one-off claim re-searched** over in-scope paper cards: Guile ("would counter", "would be countered": only Guile) and Torrent of Lava ("on the stack" statics: only Torrent and the split second reminder text) stay manual. The candidates the first pass had kept compiled were re-checked and stay compiled with the shared construct named in their notes: Martyrdom ("Any player may activate" on 36 cards), Hellish Rebuke (granter references; its rulings bind each grant to its own caster), Hall of Gemstone (Pale Moon supported, False Dawn, Chaos Moon), Territorial Hellkite (history across turns), Rain of Gore and Unpredictable Cyclone (Silhouette supported), Phyrexian Grimoire and Spinning Darkness (graveyard order; "the top card of your graveyard" compiles for Alms, Nature's Kiss, Guiding Spirit), Central Elevator ("different names among" compiles for Monument to Perfection; Room doors are the new part), God-Eternal Kefnet (card copies from a graveyard compile: Flawless Forgery, Mnemonic Deluge), Shuriken (unattach-cost referents with Captain America), Evelyn and Xanathar (in families with shared constructs: exile provenance; permissions over another player's library top).
- **The refresh** (after cards3-triggers-durations landed) re-ran the same steps on its 19 leftovers and the 3 re-check texts: autoprobe, a second pass, 10 neutral probes (`T-` ids in drills.tsv). They showed that effect-created triggers ("Until end of turn, whenever ...") now compile, so False Cure's gap is its value, while delayed triggers on a returned object or on losing control still fail generally; the notes say so.
- **Placement changes from the review**: Uba Mask's and Shared Fate's draw replacements moved from cards4-costs-mana-replacements to cards4-cast-zones (`exiled-by-player`), so those two cards have one owner; North Star's family merged into `mana-replacements`; Mythos of Snapdax moved as above. `round4/check_plan.py --live` passes after the changes.
