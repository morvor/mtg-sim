# Web research request (for an agent with a real browser)

You are helping a Magic: The Gathering rules-engine project settle rules questions from official and expert sources. The project's own sandbox can't open these sites. For each question below, search and read the sources listed (and any others you find), then report back.

**How to report, for every question:**
- Quote every relevant passage word for word, in quotation marks, with its URL and its publication date.
- Say who wrote it (Wizards of the Coast release notes or update bulletin, a level-2+ judge's answer, the Magic Judges forums, Cranial Insertion, etc.), so its authority is clear.
- If you find nothing relevant, say "not found" and list where you looked.
- Don't summarize instead of quoting. Don't guess. Mark clearly anything that's your own reasoning rather than a source.
- Keep the questions numbered as below.

**Good places to look:** magic.wizards.com (release notes, "Update Bulletin" and "Comprehensive Rules changes" articles), media.wizards.com (CR archives), gatherer.wizards.com (card rulings, and Oracle text history where shown), blogs.magicjudges.org and apps.magicjudges.org (judge forums, "Rules Q&A"), cranial-insertion.com, reddit.com/r/mtgrules, mtgsalvation.com rules forum, the Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) for old pages, and judge chat or Discord logs where quoted publicly.

## Q1 (most important). "That player" of a trigger after its creatures change control

Coveted Jewel: "Whenever one or more creatures an opponent controls attack you and aren't blocked, that player draws three cards and gains control of this artifact. Untap it."
Norn's Decree: "Whenever one or more creatures an opponent controls deal combat damage to you, that opponent gets a poison counter."

If the Jewel's controller (the defending player) gains control of the unblocked attacking creature in response to the trigger, who is "that player": the opponent who controlled the creature when the ability triggered, or the creature's controller as the ability resolves (which would be the Jewel's own controller)? The same question for Norn's Decree, and for any trigger worded "[creatures/permanents] an opponent controls … , that player/that opponent …".

Look especially for:
- the Phyrexia: All Will Be One Commander release notes (Norn's Decree);
- the Commander Legends: Battle for Baldur's Gate release notes on the initiative ("Whenever one or more creatures a player controls deal combat damage to the player who has the initiative, the controller of those creatures takes the initiative"), and whether "the controller of those creatures" is fixed when the ability triggers or read when it resolves;
- Cranial Insertion articles 2760 and 2763 (Coveted Jewel);
- Magic Judges forum topic 49911 ("Coveted Jewel and creatures not declared attacking");
- Cardmarket's "#CMAskTheJudge Episode 66: A Real Jewel";
- any judge answer about Edric, Spymaster of Trest, Bident of Thassa, Coveted Jewel or Michiko Konda when the creature changes control before the trigger resolves.

Find magicjudges forum post 131920 (said to be from 2015), and any judge or Wizards statement after 2022-04-29 (when CR 601.4a, now 601.6a, was added) on whether a spell mastery card cast from the graveyard counts itself toward "two or more instant and/or sorcery cards in your graveyard".

## Q4. "For as long as" durations while the ability waits on the stack

Any judge answers on:
- (a) the source phasing out and back in while a "for as long as you control ~" ability is on the stack;
- (b) a copy of such an ability (Strionic Resonator, Lithoform Engine): does the copy's duration start with the original's or when the copy is created;
- (c) a condition about another object (Old Man of the Sea's power comparison, Eriette's Aura) failing and then holding again before resolution.

## Q5. Tempting offers with Doubling Season / Hardened Scales / Xorn

The tempting offer cards (Tempt with Glory, Tempt with Discovery, Tempt with Vengeance, Tempt with Bunnies, Tempt with Immortality, Tempt with Reflections, Tempt with Mayhem) say the effect "happens again for you a number of times equal to the number of opponents who accepted." With Hardened Scales or Xorn (which add one to "one or more" counters or tokens per event), is "happens again N times" N separate events or one? Also any Wizards ruling or judge answer on "for each opponent, you create a token" style effects with Peregrin Took or Xorn.

## Q6. Ixidron and other permanents' "enters with" effects

When Ixidron ("As Ixidron enters, turn all other nontoken creatures face down") enters at the same time as other creatures, and Grumgully, the Generous ("Each other non-Human creature you control enters with an additional +1/+1 counter on it") is turned face down by it, do the simultaneously entering creatures still get Grumgully's counter? Any judge answers on a replacement effect's source losing its abilities (turned face down, or Humility) while simultaneously entering permanents' replacement effects are applied.

## Q7. Seraph's ruling

Seraph's Gatherer ruling (2007-09-16) says "If the other creature is a token, it will cease to exist after being exiled. It won't be returned to the battlefield." The current Oracle text exiles nothing. Did Seraph's Oracle text ever involve exile? Check Gatherer's Oracle history for Seraph (Coldsnap / Masters Edition era), the Masters Edition update bulletin (2007-08-29), and whether Tawnos's Coffin's same-day ruling has the same sentence.

## Q8. Attack taxes and mana that can only pay {X} costs

With Sphere of Safety ("pays {X} for each of those creatures") and Ghostly Prison ("pays {2} for each creature") both applying, can mana that can be spent only on costs containing {X} (Rosheen Meanderer, Nexos, Elementalist's Palette) pay Ghostly Prison's part of the total cost to attack? Any judge answer on whether the "total cost to attack" (CR 508.1h) is one cost or several for such spending restrictions.

## Q9. Siege of Towers' replicate ruling

Siege of Towers' 2024-01-12 ruling names Shattering Spree ("you'll copy Shattering Spree for each time you paid its replicate cost"). Check the Ravnica Remastered release notes to see whether the ruling there names Siege of Towers, i.e. whether it's a copying error in Gatherer.

## Q10. Hybrid mana written as two symbols in 2024 rulings

Taeko, the Patient Avalanche (2024-11-08: "when you pay {U}{B} this way"; the card says {U/B}), Slinza, the Spiked Stampede ("{1}{R}{G}"; the card says {1}{R/G}), and the extort ruling on Syndicate Heavy (2024-02-02) and Sorin of House Markov (2024-06-07) ("{W}{B}"; extort is {W/B}). Check the Foundations and related release notes for how they're worded there.
